Bienville Parish ranks 52nd of 64 Louisiana parishes by number of residents age 65 and older, with about 2,651 people in that age group, or 21.4% of residents (statewide: 17.4%). About 972 more people are 60 to 64 and are heading toward Medicare; our turning 65 guide explains their first steps. Census 2023 estimates show 55% of the 65-plus population is under 75, 33% is 75 to 84 and 13% is 85 or older. CMS data show 53.6% of Bienville Parish residents with Medicare chose Medicare Advantage, which is close to the 57.5% average across Louisiana. Averages cannot choose a plan for you. Medicare Advantage and Original Medicare with a Medigap plan each suit different people, depending on doctors, medicines and how you prefer to handle costs, and we compare both at your ZIP code.

Who has Medicare in Bienville Parish

Bienville Parish is a rural county; the U.S. Department of Agriculture describes it as: urban population of fewer than 5,000, adjacent to a metro area. Rural counties tend to have fewer local hospitals and doctors, so a plan’s network and the distance to care matter a great deal.

CMS counts 3,582 Medicare beneficiaries in Bienville Parish (2023). Their average age is 73, about the same as the Louisiana average of 72. About 26% are “dual eligible,” meaning they have both Medicare and Medicaid, higher than the state figure of 20%. Where many people have both, special plans for dual-eligible members can matter; see our Medicaid and dual eligible guide, since availability varies by plan and area.

Among people in Original Medicare in Bienville Parish, CMS reported about 703 emergency room visits and 275 inpatient hospital stays per 1,000 beneficiaries in 2023, which is close to the state average for emergency visits (648) and higher than it for hospital stays (237). About 12% received home health care. These figures describe how people use care under Original Medicare; they do not show what any plan covers or costs.

Sources: CMS Medicare Geographic Variation, 2023; USDA Rural-Urban Continuum Codes, 2023.

Common questions

Bienville Parish Medicare FAQ

Can I get help with Medicare in Bienville Parish by phone?
Yes. You do not need to travel: we are licensed in Louisiana and help Bienville Parish residents by phone or video, comparing plans from many insurers at no cost to you.
Will my Bienville Parish doctors and hospitals be covered?
On a Medicare Advantage plan it depends on that plan’s network, so before you enroll we check whether your doctors and the hospitals you use, such as Bienville Medical Center, are in network. With a Medicare Supplement (Medigap) plan you can see any doctor who accepts Medicare.
How many people in Bienville Parish have Medicare Advantage?
CMS reported 1,899 people enrolled in a Medicare Advantage plan out of 3,545 people with Medicare in Bienville Parish in September 2026, or 53.6%. The Louisiana average was 57.5%.
Do you charge Bienville Parish residents for help?
No, there is no fee. The insurance company pays us when you enroll, and your premium does not go up because you used an agent.
When can I enroll or change plans?
You can enroll during your Initial Enrollment Period around your 65th birthday, during the Annual Enrollment Period from October 15 to December 7, or during a Special Enrollment Period if you have a qualifying event such as moving into or within the county.
