Insurance help for South Carolina residents

Choosing the right coverage shouldn't feel like a guessing game. Honorbrook is an independent agency that helps South Carolina residents sort through Medicare, health, and life insurance options without the hard sell. Whether you're turning 65, already on Medicare and wondering if you're overpaying, or shopping for coverage before you reach 65, we walk you through your real choices in plain English and let you decide at your own pace.

Our help is free — and it stays free. Carriers pay us a commission when we place a policy, and by law your premium is exactly the same whether you work with us or enroll on your own. You never pay a fee for our time, our comparisons, or the support that comes after you're enrolled. We simply earn from the carrier, so we can focus on finding the plan that actually fits you.

Coverage we offer in South Carolina

  • Medicare Advantage — all-in-one Part C plans, many with $0 premiums plus extras.
  • Medicare Supplement (Medigap) — see any doctor who accepts Medicare, with predictable costs.
  • Part D drug plans — we find the lowest true annual cost for your exact medication list.
  • final expense — small whole-life coverage to protect your family from funeral costs.
  • life insurance — term and whole-life options to fit your budget and goals.
  • ICHRA — individual coverage health reimbursement arrangements for employers and employees.
  • ACA plans (under 65) — marketplace health coverage with subsidy help if you qualify.
  • group / SHOP coverage — small-business health plans for South Carolina employers.

South Carolina Medicare & insurance FAQ

Do you charge South Carolina residents for help?
No — our help is completely free. Carriers pay us a commission when we place a policy, and by law your premium is identical whether you use an agent or enroll on your own. You get the comparison, the guidance, and the year-round support at no cost to you.
Which South Carolina cities do you serve?
All of South Carolina, handled by phone. That includes Charleston, Columbia, Greenville, Myrtle Beach, Spartanburg, Rock Hill, and every community in between — no office visit needed.
What plans can I get in South Carolina?
Plan availability varies by county in South Carolina. We compare Medicare Advantage, Medicare Supplement (Medigap), Part D drug plans, ACA, ICHRA, and life coverage offered at your exact address so you only see options you can actually enroll in.
Can you help if I'm new to South Carolina or moving?
Yes. A move into, out of, or within South Carolina can open a Special Enrollment Period that lets you change plans outside the usual windows. We'll check what's available at your new address and help you switch without a gap in coverage.
