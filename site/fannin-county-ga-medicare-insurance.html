Among Georgia’s 159 counties, Fannin County comes in 47th for residents age 65 and up (8,331). Older adults make up 32.1% of the population here; the state figure is 15.4%. About 2,441 more people are 60 to 64 and are heading toward Medicare; our turning 65 guide explains their first steps. Among residents 65 and older, 59% are 65 to 74, 33% are 75 to 84 and 8% are 85 or older (Census 2023 estimates). About 46.8% of people with Medicare in Fannin County are enrolled in a Medicare Advantage plan. That is lower than the Georgia average of 56.2%. What most people do in a county is less important than what fits you. We weigh Medicare Advantage against Original Medicare with Medigap using your doctors, your drugs and your budget.

Who has Medicare in Fannin County

The USDA classifies Fannin County as: urban population of fewer than 5,000, adjacent to a metro area. With fewer hospitals and clinics close by, the right Medicare plan here is often the one whose network includes the providers you can really reach.

CMS counts 9,209 Medicare beneficiaries in Fannin County (2023). Their average age is 73, about the same as the Georgia average of 73. About 10% are “dual eligible,” meaning they have both Medicare and Medicaid, lower than the state figure of 12%.

Among people in Original Medicare in Fannin County, CMS reported about 559 emergency room visits and 163 inpatient hospital stays per 1,000 beneficiaries in 2023, which is close to the state average for emergency visits (560) and lower than it for hospital stays (223). About 8% received home health care. These figures describe how people use care under Original Medicare; they do not show what any plan covers or costs.

Sources: CMS Medicare Geographic Variation, 2023; USDA Rural-Urban Continuum Codes, 2023.

Common questions

Fannin County Medicare FAQ

Can I get help with Medicare in Fannin County by phone?
Yes. We are an independent agency licensed in Georgia, and we help Fannin County residents by phone or video call, so you can compare plans from home. There is no pressure to enroll.
Will my Fannin County doctors and hospitals be covered?
On a Medicare Advantage plan it depends on that plan’s network, so before you enroll we check whether your doctors and the hospitals you use, such as Blue Ridge Medical Center, are in network. With a Medicare Supplement (Medigap) plan you can see any doctor who accepts Medicare.
How many people in Fannin County have Medicare Advantage?
CMS reported 4,313 people enrolled in a Medicare Advantage plan out of 9,212 people with Medicare in Fannin County in September 2026, or 46.8%. The Georgia average was 56.2%.
Do you charge Fannin County residents for help?
No, there is no fee. The insurance company pays us when you enroll, and your premium does not go up because you used an agent.
When can I enroll or change plans?
You can enroll during your Initial Enrollment Period around your 65th birthday, during the Annual Enrollment Period from October 15 to December 7, or during a Special Enrollment Period if you have a qualifying event such as moving into or within the county.
