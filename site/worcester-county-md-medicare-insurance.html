Among Maryland’s 24 counties, Worcester County comes in 16th for residents age 65 and up (15,895). Older adults make up 29.3% of the population here; the state figure is 17.3%. About 4,679 more people are 60 to 64 and are heading toward Medicare; our turning 65 guide explains their first steps. Census 2023 estimates show 55% of the 65-plus population is under 75, 34% is 75 to 84 and 11% is 85 or older. CMS data show 25.1% of Worcester County residents with Medicare chose Medicare Advantage, which is close to the 24.8% average across Maryland. A county average does not tell you which kind of plan fits you. Whether Medicare Advantage or Original Medicare with a Medigap plan is the better fit depends on your doctors, your prescriptions and how you want to handle costs, so we compare both for your ZIP code.

Who has Medicare in Worcester County

The USDA classifies Worcester County as: urban population of 20,000 or more, adjacent to a metro area. In counties of this type, people often travel to a larger neighboring county for specialists, so we check whether your plan’s network reaches there.

CMS counts 17,004 Medicare beneficiaries in Worcester County (2023). Their average age is 74, about the same as the Maryland average of 73. About 14% are “dual eligible,” meaning they have both Medicare and Medicaid, lower than the state figure of 17%.

Among people in Original Medicare in Worcester County, CMS reported about 647 emergency room visits and 193 inpatient hospital stays per 1,000 beneficiaries in 2023, which is higher than the state average for emergency visits (555) and close to it for hospital stays (217). About 10% received home health care. These figures describe how people use care under Original Medicare; they do not show what any plan covers or costs.

Sources: CMS Medicare Geographic Variation, 2023; USDA Rural-Urban Continuum Codes, 2023.

Common questions

Worcester County Medicare FAQ

Can I get help with Medicare in Worcester County by phone?
Yes. You do not need to travel: we are licensed in Maryland and help Worcester County residents by phone or video, comparing plans from many insurers at no cost to you.
Will my Worcester County doctors and hospitals be covered?
On a Medicare Advantage plan it depends on that plan’s network, so before you enroll we check whether your doctors and the hospitals you use, such as Atlantic General Hospital, are in network. With a Medicare Supplement (Medigap) plan you can see any doctor who accepts Medicare.
How many people in Worcester County have Medicare Advantage?
CMS reported 4,394 people enrolled in a Medicare Advantage plan out of 17,513 people with Medicare in Worcester County in September 2026, or 25.1%. The Maryland average was 24.8%.
Do you charge Worcester County residents for help?
No. Our help is free to you. Carriers pay us a commission when we place a policy, and your premium is the same whether you use an agent or enroll on your own.
When can I enroll or change plans?
You can enroll during your Initial Enrollment Period around your 65th birthday, during the Annual Enrollment Period from October 15 to December 7, or during a Special Enrollment Period if you have a qualifying event such as moving into or within the county.
