What most people do in a county is less important than what fits you. We weigh Medicare Advantage against Original Medicare with Medigap using your doctors, your drugs and your budget.

Who has Medicare in Hansford County

Hansford County is a rural county (USDA: urban population of fewer than 5,000, not adjacent to a metro area). Plan choices can be narrower than in a city, and we focus on networks that include the doctors and hospital you actually use.

CMS counts 959 Medicare beneficiaries in Hansford County (2023). Their average age is 75, older than the Texas average of 73. About 11% are “dual eligible,” meaning they have both Medicare and Medicaid, close to the state figure of 11%.

Among people in Original Medicare in Hansford County, CMS reported about 688 emergency room visits and 191 inpatient hospital stays per 1,000 beneficiaries in 2023, which is higher than the state average for emergency visits (580) and lower than it for hospital stays (226). About 5% received home health care. These figures describe how people use care under Original Medicare; they do not show what any plan covers or costs.

Sources: CMS Medicare Geographic Variation, 2023; USDA Rural-Urban Continuum Codes, 2023.

Insurers choose which counties to serve each year, which means the Medicare Advantage and Part D plans you can pick in Hansford County can be different from the ones offered next door. We are not based in Hansford County; we help Hansford County residents by phone or video call.

Common questions

Hansford County Medicare FAQ

Can I get help with Medicare in Hansford County by phone?
Yes. Phone and video appointments are how we serve Hansford County. We hold a Texas license, compare plans from many carriers and walk you through them from home, with no pressure to enroll.
Will my Hansford County doctors and hospitals be covered?
On a Medicare Advantage plan it depends on that plan’s network, so before you enroll we check whether your doctors and the hospitals you use, such as Hansford County Hospital, are in network. With a Medicare Supplement (Medigap) plan you can see any doctor who accepts Medicare.
How many people in Hansford County have Medicare Advantage?
CMS reported 217 people enrolled in a Medicare Advantage plan out of 900 people with Medicare in Hansford County in September 2026, or 24.1%. The Texas average was 54.9%.
Do you charge Hansford County residents for help?
No. You pay nothing to work with us. Carriers compensate us, and the price of your plan is the same as if you enrolled directly.
When can I enroll or change plans?
You can enroll during your Initial Enrollment Period around your 65th birthday, during the Annual Enrollment Period from October 15 to December 7, or during a Special Enrollment Period if you have a qualifying event such as moving into or within the county.
