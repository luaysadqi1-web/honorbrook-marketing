Limestone County ranks 14th of 67 Alabama counties by number of residents age 65 and older, with about 18,332 people in that age group, or 16.0% of residents (statewide: 18.3%). The 7,584 residents now between 60 and 64 will become Medicare-eligible soon, and our turning 65 guide walks through it. Among residents 65 and older, 60% are 65 to 74, 31% are 75 to 84 and 10% are 85 or older (Census 2023 estimates). CMS data show 52.5% of Limestone County residents with Medicare chose Medicare Advantage, which is lower than the 61.5% average across Alabama. A county average does not tell you which kind of plan fits you. Whether Medicare Advantage or Original Medicare with a Medigap plan is the better fit depends on your doctors, your prescriptions and how you want to handle costs, so we compare both for your ZIP code.

Who has Medicare in Limestone County

Limestone County is a metro county; the U.S. Department of Agriculture describes it as: counties in metro areas of 250,000 to 1 million population. Metro counties usually have more hospitals and more insurers competing for Medicare members, so there can be more plans to compare.

CMS counts 21,299 Medicare beneficiaries in Limestone County (2023). Their average age is 73, about the same as the Alabama average of 72. About 9% are “dual eligible,” meaning they have both Medicare and Medicaid, lower than the state figure of 14%.

Among people in Original Medicare in Limestone County, CMS reported about 560 emergency room visits and 221 inpatient hospital stays per 1,000 beneficiaries in 2023, which is close to the state average for emergency visits (600) and close to it for hospital stays (247). About 9% received home health care. These figures describe how people use care under Original Medicare; they do not show what any plan covers or costs.

Sources: CMS Medicare Geographic Variation, 2023; USDA Rural-Urban Continuum Codes, 2023.

Common questions

Limestone County Medicare FAQ

Can I get help with Medicare in Limestone County by phone?
Yes. You do not need to travel: we are licensed in Alabama and help Limestone County residents by phone or video, comparing plans from many insurers at no cost to you.
Will my Limestone County doctors and hospitals be covered?
On a Medicare Advantage plan it depends on that plan’s network, so before you enroll we check whether your doctors and the hospitals you use, such as Athens Limestone Hospital, are in network. With a Medicare Supplement (Medigap) plan you can see any doctor who accepts Medicare.
How many people in Limestone County have Medicare Advantage?
CMS reported 11,713 people enrolled in a Medicare Advantage plan out of 22,327 people with Medicare in Limestone County in September 2026, or 52.5%. The Alabama average was 61.5%.
Do you charge Limestone County residents for help?
No. You pay nothing to work with us. Carriers compensate us, and the price of your plan is the same as if you enrolled directly.
When can I enroll or change plans?
You can enroll during your Initial Enrollment Period around your 65th birthday, during the Annual Enrollment Period from October 15 to December 7, or during a Special Enrollment Period if you have a qualifying event such as moving into or within the county.
