Among North Carolina’s 100 counties, Hertford County comes in 82nd for residents age 65 and up (4,485). Older adults make up 23.1% of the population here; the state figure is 17.6%. Another 1,491 residents are age 60 to 64 and will reach Medicare eligibility within about five years (see our turning 65 guide). Census 2023 estimates show 60% of the 65-plus population is under 75, 30% is 75 to 84 and 11% is 85 or older. CMS data show 58.5% of Hertford County residents with Medicare chose Medicare Advantage, which is close to the 57.9% average across North Carolina. A county average does not tell you which kind of plan fits you. Whether Medicare Advantage or Original Medicare with a Medigap plan is the better fit depends on your doctors, your prescriptions and how you want to handle costs, so we compare both for your ZIP code.

Who has Medicare in Hertford County

Hertford County is a rural county (USDA: urban population of fewer than 5,000, adjacent to a metro area). Plan choices can be narrower than in a city, and we focus on networks that include the doctors and hospital you actually use.

CMS counts 5,819 Medicare beneficiaries in Hertford County (2023). Their average age is 73, about the same as the North Carolina average of 73. About 23% are “dual eligible,” meaning they have both Medicare and Medicaid, higher than the state figure of 13%. Where many people have both, special plans for dual-eligible members can matter; see our Medicaid and dual eligible guide, since availability varies by plan and area.

Among people in Original Medicare in Hertford County, CMS reported about 697 emergency room visits and 221 inpatient hospital stays per 1,000 beneficiaries in 2023, which is higher than the state average for emergency visits (572) and close to it for hospital stays (210). About 7% received home health care. These figures describe how people use care under Original Medicare; they do not show what any plan covers or costs.

Sources: CMS Medicare Geographic Variation, 2023; USDA Rural-Urban Continuum Codes, 2023.

Insurers choose which counties to serve each year, which means the Medicare Advantage and Part D plans you can pick in Hertford County can be different from the ones offered next door. We are not based in Hertford County; we help Hertford County residents by phone or video call.

Common questions

Hertford County Medicare FAQ

Can I get help with Medicare in Hertford County by phone?
Yes. You do not need to travel: we are licensed in North Carolina and help Hertford County residents by phone or video, comparing plans from many insurers at no cost to you.
Will my Hertford County doctors and hospitals be covered?
On a Medicare Advantage plan it depends on that plan’s network, so before you enroll we check whether your doctors and the hospitals you use, such as Vidant Roanoke Chowan Hospital, are in network. With a Medicare Supplement (Medigap) plan you can see any doctor who accepts Medicare.
How many people in Hertford County have Medicare Advantage?
CMS reported 3,350 people enrolled in a Medicare Advantage plan out of 5,727 people with Medicare in Hertford County in September 2026, or 58.5%. The North Carolina average was 57.9%.
Do you charge Hertford County residents for help?
No. Our help is free to you. Carriers pay us a commission when we place a policy, and your premium is the same whether you use an agent or enroll on your own.
When can I enroll or change plans?
You can enroll during your Initial Enrollment Period around your 65th birthday, during the Annual Enrollment Period from October 15 to December 7, or during a Special Enrollment Period if you have a qualifying event such as moving into or within the county.
