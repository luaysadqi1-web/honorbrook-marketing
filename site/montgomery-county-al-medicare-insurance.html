With roughly 37,666 residents age 65 and older, Montgomery County is 6th among 67 Alabama counties for older residents. That group is 16.7% of its population, compared with 18.3% across Alabama. The 13,664 residents now between 60 and 64 will become Medicare-eligible soon, and our turning 65 guide walks through it. Among residents 65 and older, 60% are 65 to 74, 30% are 75 to 84 and 10% are 85 or older (Census 2023 estimates). Medicare Advantage enrollment in Montgomery County is 63.3% of people with Medicare, close to the Alabama average of 61.5%. What most people do in a county is less important than what fits you. We weigh Medicare Advantage against Original Medicare with Medigap using your doctors, your drugs and your budget.

Who has Medicare in Montgomery County

Montgomery County sits in a metro area, which the USDA defines as counties in metro areas of 250,000 to 1 million population. That usually means more doctors, more hospitals and more plan choices in the area.

CMS counts 47,047 Medicare beneficiaries in Montgomery County (2023). Their average age is 72, about the same as the Alabama average of 72. About 13% are “dual eligible,” meaning they have both Medicare and Medicaid, close to the state figure of 14%.

Among people in Original Medicare in Montgomery County, CMS reported about 531 emergency room visits and 226 inpatient hospital stays per 1,000 beneficiaries in 2023, which is close to the state average for emergency visits (600) and close to it for hospital stays (247). About 8% received home health care. These figures describe how people use care under Original Medicare; they do not show what any plan covers or costs.

Sources: CMS Medicare Geographic Variation, 2023; USDA Rural-Urban Continuum Codes, 2023.

Where you live decides which Medicare Advantage and Part D plans you can join, and Montgomery County may have a different set of plans than the counties around it. We are not based in Montgomery County; we help Montgomery County residents by phone or video call.

Hospitals located in Montgomery County

The Centers for Medicare & Medicaid Services (CMS) lists these hospitals in Montgomery County:

  • Baptist Medical Center East — Montgomery (acute care; government-owned; emergency services; CMS overall rating 4 of 5)
  • Baptist Medical Center South — Montgomery (acute care; government-owned; emergency services; CMS overall rating 2 of 5)
  • Jackson Hospital & Clinic — Montgomery (acute care; nonprofit; emergency services; CMS overall rating 2 of 5)

Network rules, not geography, decide whether a plan covers a hospital. We look up the hospitals and doctors you use and check them against each plan before you enroll. Ratings are CMS Care Compare overall hospital star ratings; they describe the hospital, not whether a plan covers it. Source: CMS Hospital General Information.

Common questions

Montgomery County Medicare FAQ

Can I get help with Medicare in Montgomery County by phone?
Yes. Phone and video appointments are how we serve Montgomery County. We hold a Alabama license, compare plans from many carriers and walk you through them from home, with no pressure to enroll.
Will my Montgomery County doctors and hospitals be covered?
On a Medicare Advantage plan it depends on that plan’s network, so before you enroll we check whether your doctors and the hospitals you use, such as Baptist Medical Center East, Baptist Medical Center South, Jackson Hospital & Clinic, are in network. With a Medicare Supplement (Medigap) plan you can see any doctor who accepts Medicare.
How many people in Montgomery County have Medicare Advantage?
CMS reported 29,370 people enrolled in a Medicare Advantage plan out of 46,410 people with Medicare in Montgomery County in September 2026, or 63.3%. The Alabama average was 61.5%.
Do you charge Montgomery County residents for help?
No. Our help is free to you. Carriers pay us a commission when we place a policy, and your premium is the same whether you use an agent or enroll on your own.
When can I enroll or change plans?
You can enroll during your Initial Enrollment Period around your 65th birthday, during the Annual Enrollment Period from October 15 to December 7, or during a Special Enrollment Period if you have a qualifying event such as moving into or within the county.
