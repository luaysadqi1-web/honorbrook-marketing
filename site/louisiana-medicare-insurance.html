Insurance help for Louisiana residents

If you live in Louisiana and you're sorting through Medicare, health, or life insurance, you don't have to do it alone — and you don't have to pay for help. Honorbrook works with people across the state who are turning 65, already on Medicare and wondering if they're on the right plan, retiring or losing employer coverage, or shopping for health insurance before they reach Medicare age. Whatever stage you're at, we walk through your options with you and let you decide at your own pace.

Our help is free because of how the industry works: carriers pay us a commission when we place a policy, and by law your premium is exactly the same whether you use an agent or enroll on your own. You're never billed, and there's never any pressure to buy. We'd rather earn your trust and your referrals than push a plan that doesn't fit.

Coverage we offer in Louisiana

  • Medicare Advantage — all-in-one Part C plans, many with $0 premiums plus dental, vision, and drug coverage.
  • Medicare Supplement (Medigap) — see any doctor who accepts Medicare, with predictable out-of-pocket costs.
  • Part D drug plans — we find the lowest true annual cost for your exact medication list.
  • Final expense — small whole-life policies that cover funeral and burial costs.
  • Life insurance — term and permanent coverage to protect your family.
  • ICHRA — individual coverage HRAs for employers who want to reimburse premiums tax-free.
  • ACA plans (under 65) — Marketplace health coverage with subsidy help for those not yet on Medicare.
  • Group / SHOP coverage — small-business health plans for Louisiana employers.

Common questions

Louisiana Medicare & Insurance FAQ

Do you charge Louisiana residents for help?
No — our help is completely free. Carriers pay us a commission when we place a policy, and by law your premium is identical whether you use an agent or enroll on your own. You get the comparison, the paperwork help, and year-round support at no cost.
Which Louisiana cities do you serve?
All of Louisiana, entirely by phone — including New Orleans, Baton Rouge, Shreveport, Lafayette, and Lake Charles, plus every smaller town and parish in between. No office visit is ever required.
What plans can I get in Louisiana?
Plan availability varies by parish and county in Louisiana. We compare what's actually offered at your exact address — Medicare Advantage, Medigap, Part D, ICHRA, ACA plans for those under 65, and life coverage — so you only see real options.
Can you help if I'm new to Louisiana or moving?
Yes. Moving to, from, or within Louisiana can open a Special Enrollment Period that lets you change your Medicare or health coverage outside the usual windows. Give us a call and we'll check your new address and timing.
