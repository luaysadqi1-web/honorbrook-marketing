Among South Carolina’s 46 counties, Barnwell County comes in 39th for residents age 65 and up (4,105). Older adults make up 20.1% of the population here; the state figure is 19.3%. Another 1,417 residents are age 60 to 64 and will reach Medicare eligibility within about five years (see our turning 65 guide). Census 2023 estimates show 60% of the 65-plus population is under 75, 31% is 75 to 84 and 9% is 85 or older. CMS data show 58.1% of Barnwell County residents with Medicare chose Medicare Advantage, which is higher than the 46.7% average across South Carolina. Averages cannot choose a plan for you. Medicare Advantage and Original Medicare with a Medigap plan each suit different people, depending on doctors, medicines and how you prefer to handle costs, and we compare both at your ZIP code.

Who has Medicare in Barnwell County

The USDA classifies Barnwell County as: urban population of fewer than 5,000, adjacent to a metro area. With fewer hospitals and clinics close by, the right Medicare plan here is often the one whose network includes the providers you can really reach.

CMS counts 5,314 Medicare beneficiaries in Barnwell County (2023). Their average age is 72, about the same as the South Carolina average of 73. About 12% are “dual eligible,” meaning they have both Medicare and Medicaid, higher than the state figure of 7%. Where many people have both, special plans for dual-eligible members can matter; see our Medicaid and dual eligible guide, since availability varies by plan and area.

Among people in Original Medicare in Barnwell County, CMS reported about 687 emergency room visits and 218 inpatient hospital stays per 1,000 beneficiaries in 2023, which is higher than the state average for emergency visits (563) and close to it for hospital stays (202). About 10% received home health care. These figures describe how people use care under Original Medicare; they do not show what any plan covers or costs.

Sources: CMS Medicare Geographic Variation, 2023; USDA Rural-Urban Continuum Codes, 2023.

Hospitals and doctors near Barnwell County

CMS Hospital General Information lists no acute care, critical access or children’s hospital located inside Barnwell County. Residents often use hospitals in neighboring counties, which matters for Medicare Advantage because a plan’s network may include or leave out the hospital you use. Where to get free Medicare help in Barnwell County

  • The federal Medicare helpline is 1-800-MEDICARE, 1-800-633-4227.
  • I-CARE, the South Carolina state health insurance assistance program (SHIP), offers free, unbiased Medicare counseling: 1-800-868-9095.
  • The Eldercare Locator, 1-800-677-1116, finds the Area Agency on Aging that serves your address (eldercare.acl.gov).

They are good places to ask questions. What we add is a side-by-side comparison of the plans you can really join, plus help with enrollment and yearly reviews.

Common questions

Barnwell County Medicare FAQ

Can I get help with Medicare in Barnwell County by phone?
Yes. You do not need to travel: we are licensed in South Carolina and help Barnwell County residents by phone or video, comparing plans from many insurers at no cost to you.
Will my Barnwell County doctors and hospitals be covered?
On a Medicare Advantage plan it depends on that plan’s network, so before you enroll we check whether your doctors and the hospitals you use are in network, including hospitals in neighboring counties. With a Medicare Supplement (Medigap) plan you can see any doctor who accepts Medicare.
How many people in Barnwell County have Medicare Advantage?
CMS reported 2,878 people enrolled in a Medicare Advantage plan out of 4,955 people with Medicare in Barnwell County in September 2026, or 58.1%. The South Carolina average was 46.7%.
Do you charge Barnwell County residents for help?
No. You pay nothing to work with us. Carriers compensate us, and the price of your plan is the same as if you enrolled directly.
When can I enroll or change plans?
You can enroll during your Initial Enrollment Period around your 65th birthday, during the Annual Enrollment Period from October 15 to December 7, or during a Special Enrollment Period if you have a qualifying event such as moving into or within the county.
