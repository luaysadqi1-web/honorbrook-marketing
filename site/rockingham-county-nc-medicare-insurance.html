Among North Carolina’s 100 counties, Rockingham County comes in 28th for residents age 65 and up (20,123). Older adults make up 21.8% of the population here; the state figure is 17.6%. The 7,152 residents now between 60 and 64 will become Medicare-eligible soon, and our turning 65 guide walks through it. By age, the 65-and-older group splits 60% ages 65 to 74, 31% ages 75 to 84 and 9% age 85 and up (Census 2023). About 76.8% of people with Medicare in Rockingham County are enrolled in a Medicare Advantage plan. That is higher than the North Carolina average of 57.9%. Averages cannot choose a plan for you. Medicare Advantage and Original Medicare with a Medigap plan each suit different people, depending on doctors, medicines and how you prefer to handle costs, and we compare both at your ZIP code.

Who has Medicare in Rockingham County

The U.S. Department of Agriculture classifies Rockingham County as a metro county (counties in metro areas of 250,000 to 1 million population). Counties like this tend to have a wider choice of hospitals and Medicare plans than rural ones, though networks still differ plan by plan.

CMS counts 25,616 Medicare beneficiaries in Rockingham County (2023). Their average age is 71, younger than the North Carolina average of 73. About 21% are “dual eligible,” meaning they have both Medicare and Medicaid, higher than the state figure of 13%. Where many people have both, special plans for dual-eligible members can matter; see our Medicaid and dual eligible guide, since availability varies by plan and area.

Among people in Original Medicare in Rockingham County, CMS reported about 642 emergency room visits and 229 inpatient hospital stays per 1,000 beneficiaries in 2023, which is higher than the state average for emergency visits (572) and close to it for hospital stays (210). About 10% received home health care. These figures describe how people use care under Original Medicare; they do not show what any plan covers or costs.

Sources: CMS Medicare Geographic Variation, 2023; USDA Rural-Urban Continuum Codes, 2023.

Common questions

Rockingham County Medicare FAQ

Can I get help with Medicare in Rockingham County by phone?
Yes. We are an independent agency licensed in North Carolina, and we help Rockingham County residents by phone or video call, so you can compare plans from home. There is no pressure to enroll.
Will my Rockingham County doctors and hospitals be covered?
On a Medicare Advantage plan it depends on that plan’s network, so before you enroll we check whether your doctors and the hospitals you use, such as UNC Rockingham, are in network. With a Medicare Supplement (Medigap) plan you can see any doctor who accepts Medicare.
How many people in Rockingham County have Medicare Advantage?
CMS reported 19,600 people enrolled in a Medicare Advantage plan out of 25,517 people with Medicare in Rockingham County in September 2026, or 76.8%. The North Carolina average was 57.9%.
Do you charge Rockingham County residents for help?
No, there is no fee. The insurance company pays us when you enroll, and your premium does not go up because you used an agent.
When can I enroll or change plans?
You can enroll during your Initial Enrollment Period around your 65th birthday, during the Annual Enrollment Period from October 15 to December 7, or during a Special Enrollment Period if you have a qualifying event such as moving into or within the county.
