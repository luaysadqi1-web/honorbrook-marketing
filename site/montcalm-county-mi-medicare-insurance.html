Montcalm County ranks 30th of 83 Michigan counties by number of residents age 65 and older, with about 12,630 people in that age group, or 18.5% of residents (statewide: 19.2%). Another 4,720 residents are age 60 to 64 and will reach Medicare eligibility within about five years (see our turning 65 guide). Among residents 65 and older, 61% are 65 to 74, 29% are 75 to 84 and 9% are 85 or older (Census 2023 estimates). About 71.7% of people with Medicare in Montcalm County are enrolled in a Medicare Advantage plan. That is higher than the Michigan average of 63.6%. A county average does not tell you which kind of plan fits you. Whether Medicare Advantage or Original Medicare with a Medigap plan is the better fit depends on your doctors, your prescriptions and how you want to handle costs, so we compare both for your ZIP code.

Insurers choose which counties to serve each year, which means the Medicare Advantage and Part D plans you can pick in Montcalm County can be different from the ones offered next door. We are not based in Montcalm County; we help Montcalm County residents by phone or video call.

Hospitals located in Montcalm County

The Centers for Medicare & Medicaid Services (CMS) lists these hospitals in Montcalm County:

  • Sheridan Community Hospital — Sheridan (critical access; nonprofit; emergency services)
  • Spectrum Health United Hospital — Greenville (acute care; nonprofit; no emergency department; CMS overall rating 4 of 5)
  • University of Michigan Health-Sparrow Carson — Carson City (acute care; nonprofit; emergency services; CMS overall rating 4 of 5)

Being located in the county is not the same as being in your plan’s network. Each Medicare Advantage plan builds its own network, so we confirm that your hospital and your doctors are included before you choose. Ratings are CMS Care Compare overall hospital star ratings; they describe the hospital, not whether a plan covers it. Source: CMS Hospital General Information.

Free help available in Montcalm County

  • MMAP, the Michigan state health insurance assistance program (SHIP), offers free, unbiased Medicare counseling: 1-800-803-7174.
  • Eldercare Locator connects you to your local Area Agency on Aging: 1-800-677-1116 (eldercare.acl.gov).
  • 1-800-MEDICARE (1-800-633-4227) is the federal Medicare helpline.

These services are worth using. Where we add to them is comparing specific plans and helping you enroll and stay on track year after year.

Common questions

Montcalm County Medicare FAQ

Can I get help with Medicare in Montcalm County by phone?
Yes. You do not need to travel: we are licensed in Michigan and help Montcalm County residents by phone or video, comparing plans from many insurers at no cost to you.
Will my Montcalm County doctors and hospitals be covered?
On a Medicare Advantage plan it depends on that plan’s network, so before you enroll we check whether your doctors and the hospitals you use, such as Sheridan Community Hospital, Spectrum Health United Hospital, University of Michigan Health-Sparrow Carson, are in network. With a Medicare Supplement (Medigap) plan you can see any doctor who accepts Medicare.
How many people in Montcalm County have Medicare Advantage?
CMS reported 11,604 people enrolled in a Medicare Advantage plan out of 16,176 people with Medicare in Montcalm County in September 2026, or 71.7%. The Michigan average was 63.6%.
Do you charge Montcalm County residents for help?
No. Our help is free to you. Carriers pay us a commission when we place a policy, and your premium is the same whether you use an agent or enroll on your own.
When can I enroll or change plans?
You can enroll during your Initial Enrollment Period around your 65th birthday, during the Annual Enrollment Period from October 15 to December 7, or during a Special Enrollment Period if you have a qualifying event such as moving into or within the county.
