Jeff Davis County ranks 113th of 159 Georgia counties by number of residents age 65 and older, with about 2,432 people in that age group, or 16.3% of residents (statewide: 15.4%). Another 926 residents are age 60 to 64 and will reach Medicare eligibility within about five years (see our turning 65 guide). Among residents 65 and older, 58% are 65 to 74, 33% are 75 to 84 and 10% are 85 or older (Census 2023 estimates). About 56.6% of people with Medicare in Jeff Davis County are enrolled in a Medicare Advantage plan. That is close to the Georgia average of 56.2%. A county average does not tell you which kind of plan fits you. Whether Medicare Advantage or Original Medicare with a Medigap plan is the better fit depends on your doctors, your prescriptions and how you want to handle costs, so we compare both for your ZIP code.

Insurers choose which counties to serve each year, which means the Medicare Advantage and Part D plans you can pick in Jeff Davis County can be different from the ones offered next door. We are not based in Jeff Davis County; we help Jeff Davis County residents by phone or video call.

Common questions

Jeff Davis County Medicare FAQ

Can I get help with Medicare in Jeff Davis County by phone?
Yes. You do not need to travel: we are licensed in Georgia and help Jeff Davis County residents by phone or video, comparing plans from many insurers at no cost to you.
Will my Jeff Davis County doctors and hospitals be covered?
On a Medicare Advantage plan it depends on that plan’s network, so before you enroll we check whether your doctors and the hospitals you use, such as Jeff Davis Hospital, are in network. With a Medicare Supplement (Medigap) plan you can see any doctor who accepts Medicare.
How many people in Jeff Davis County have Medicare Advantage?
CMS reported 1,683 people enrolled in a Medicare Advantage plan out of 2,972 people with Medicare in Jeff Davis County in September 2026, or 56.6%. The Georgia average was 56.2%.
Do you charge Jeff Davis County residents for help?
No. Our help is free to you. Carriers pay us a commission when we place a policy, and your premium is the same whether you use an agent or enroll on your own.
When can I enroll or change plans?
You can enroll during your Initial Enrollment Period around your 65th birthday, during the Annual Enrollment Period from October 15 to December 7, or during a Special Enrollment Period if you have a qualifying event such as moving into or within the county.
