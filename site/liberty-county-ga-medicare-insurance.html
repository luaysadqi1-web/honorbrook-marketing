Liberty County ranks 52nd of 159 Georgia counties by number of residents age 65 and older, with about 7,169 people in that age group, or 10.4% of residents (statewide: 15.4%). Another 2,907 residents are age 60 to 64 and will reach Medicare eligibility within about five years (see our turning 65 guide). Census 2023 estimates show 63% of the 65-plus population is under 75, 30% is 75 to 84 and 7% is 85 or older. About 59.1% of people with Medicare in Liberty County are enrolled in a Medicare Advantage plan. That is close to the Georgia average of 56.2%. Averages cannot choose a plan for you. Medicare Advantage and Original Medicare with a Medigap plan each suit different people, depending on doctors, medicines and how you prefer to handle costs, and we compare both at your ZIP code.

Who has Medicare in Liberty County

Liberty County is a metro county; the U.S. Department of Agriculture describes it as: counties in metro areas of fewer than 250,000 population. Metro counties usually have more hospitals and more insurers competing for Medicare members, so there can be more plans to compare.

CMS counts 9,338 Medicare beneficiaries in Liberty County (2023). Their average age is 70, younger than the Georgia average of 73. About 11% are “dual eligible,” meaning they have both Medicare and Medicaid, close to the state figure of 12%.

Among people in Original Medicare in Liberty County, CMS reported about 368 emergency room visits and 188 inpatient hospital stays per 1,000 beneficiaries in 2023, which is lower than the state average for emergency visits (560) and lower than it for hospital stays (223). About 7% received home health care. These figures describe how people use care under Original Medicare; they do not show what any plan covers or costs.

Sources: CMS Medicare Geographic Variation, 2023; USDA Rural-Urban Continuum Codes, 2023.

Insurers choose which counties to serve each year, which means the Medicare Advantage and Part D plans you can pick in Liberty County can be different from the ones offered next door. We are not based in Liberty County; we help Liberty County residents by phone or video call.

Common questions

Liberty County Medicare FAQ

Can I get help with Medicare in Liberty County by phone?
Yes. We are an independent agency licensed in Georgia, and we help Liberty County residents by phone or video call, so you can compare plans from home. There is no pressure to enroll.
Will my Liberty County doctors and hospitals be covered?
On a Medicare Advantage plan it depends on that plan’s network, so before you enroll we check whether your doctors and the hospitals you use, such as Liberty Regional Medical Center, are in network. With a Medicare Supplement (Medigap) plan you can see any doctor who accepts Medicare.
How many people in Liberty County have Medicare Advantage?
CMS reported 5,807 people enrolled in a Medicare Advantage plan out of 9,829 people with Medicare in Liberty County in September 2026, or 59.1%. The Georgia average was 56.2%.
Do you charge Liberty County residents for help?
No. You pay nothing to work with us. Carriers compensate us, and the price of your plan is the same as if you enrolled directly.
When can I enroll or change plans?
You can enroll during your Initial Enrollment Period around your 65th birthday, during the Annual Enrollment Period from October 15 to December 7, or during a Special Enrollment Period if you have a qualifying event such as moving into or within the county.
