With roughly 52,167 residents age 65 and older, Chatham County is 5th among 159 Georgia counties for older residents. That group is 17.2% of its population, compared with 15.4% across Georgia. About 18,420 more people are 60 to 64 and are heading toward Medicare; our turning 65 guide explains their first steps. By age, the 65-and-older group splits 59% ages 65 to 74, 31% ages 75 to 84 and 10% age 85 and up (Census 2023). About 55.0% of people with Medicare in Chatham County are enrolled in a Medicare Advantage plan. That is close to the Georgia average of 56.2%. What most people do in a county is less important than what fits you. We weigh Medicare Advantage against Original Medicare with Medigap using your doctors, your drugs and your budget.

Who has Medicare in Chatham County

The U.S. Department of Agriculture classifies Chatham County as a metro county (counties in metro areas of 250,000 to 1 million population). Counties like this tend to have a wider choice of hospitals and Medicare plans than rural ones, though networks still differ plan by plan.

CMS counts 57,307 Medicare beneficiaries in Chatham County (2023). Their average age is 73, about the same as the Georgia average of 73. About 9% are “dual eligible,” meaning they have both Medicare and Medicaid, lower than the state figure of 12%.

Among people in Original Medicare in Chatham County, CMS reported about 541 emergency room visits and 212 inpatient hospital stays per 1,000 beneficiaries in 2023, which is close to the state average for emergency visits (560) and close to it for hospital stays (223). About 7% received home health care. These figures describe how people use care under Original Medicare; they do not show what any plan covers or costs.

Sources: CMS Medicare Geographic Variation, 2023; USDA Rural-Urban Continuum Codes, 2023.

Common questions

Chatham County Medicare FAQ

Can I get help with Medicare in Chatham County by phone?
Yes. Phone and video appointments are how we serve Chatham County. We hold a Georgia license, compare plans from many carriers and walk you through them from home, with no pressure to enroll.
Will my Chatham County doctors and hospitals be covered?
On a Medicare Advantage plan it depends on that plan’s network, so before you enroll we check whether your doctors and the hospitals you use, such as Candler Hospital, Memorial Health University Medical Center, St. Joseph's Hospital - Savannah, are in network. With a Medicare Supplement (Medigap) plan you can see any doctor who accepts Medicare.
How many people in Chatham County have Medicare Advantage?
CMS reported 31,677 people enrolled in a Medicare Advantage plan out of 57,612 people with Medicare in Chatham County in September 2026, or 55.0%. The Georgia average was 56.2%.
Do you charge Chatham County residents for help?
No. Our help is free to you. Carriers pay us a commission when we place a policy, and your premium is the same whether you use an agent or enroll on your own.
When can I enroll or change plans?
You can enroll during your Initial Enrollment Period around your 65th birthday, during the Annual Enrollment Period from October 15 to December 7, or during a Special Enrollment Period if you have a qualifying event such as moving into or within the county.
