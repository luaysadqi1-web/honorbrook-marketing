Winston County ranks 40th of 67 Alabama counties by number of residents age 65 and older, with about 5,295 people in that age group, or 22.4% of residents (statewide: 18.3%). The 1,850 residents now between 60 and 64 will become Medicare-eligible soon, and our turning 65 guide walks through it. Among residents 65 and older, 56% are 65 to 74, 33% are 75 to 84 and 11% are 85 or older (Census 2023 estimates). CMS data show 60.1% of Winston County residents with Medicare chose Medicare Advantage, which is close to the 61.5% average across Alabama. Averages cannot choose a plan for you. Medicare Advantage and Original Medicare with a Medigap plan each suit different people, depending on doctors, medicines and how you prefer to handle costs, and we compare both at your ZIP code.

Who has Medicare in Winston County

Winston County is a rural county (USDA: urban population of fewer than 5,000, adjacent to a metro area). Plan choices can be narrower than in a city, and we focus on networks that include the doctors and hospital you actually use.

CMS counts 6,374 Medicare beneficiaries in Winston County (2023). Their average age is 73, about the same as the Alabama average of 72. About 20% are “dual eligible,” meaning they have both Medicare and Medicaid, higher than the state figure of 14%. Where many people have both, special plans for dual-eligible members can matter; see our Medicaid and dual eligible guide, since availability varies by plan and area.

Among people in Original Medicare in Winston County, CMS reported about 623 emergency room visits and 298 inpatient hospital stays per 1,000 beneficiaries in 2023, which is close to the state average for emergency visits (600) and higher than it for hospital stays (247). About 14% received home health care. These figures describe how people use care under Original Medicare; they do not show what any plan covers or costs.

Sources: CMS Medicare Geographic Variation, 2023; USDA Rural-Urban Continuum Codes, 2023.

Where you live decides which Medicare Advantage and Part D plans you can join, and Winston County may have a different set of plans than the counties around it. We are not based in Winston County; we help Winston County residents by phone or video call.

Common questions

Winston County Medicare FAQ

Can I get help with Medicare in Winston County by phone?
Yes. You do not need to travel: we are licensed in Alabama and help Winston County residents by phone or video, comparing plans from many insurers at no cost to you.
Will my Winston County doctors and hospitals be covered?
On a Medicare Advantage plan it depends on that plan’s network, so before you enroll we check whether your doctors and the hospitals you use, such as Lakeland Community Hospital, Lakeland Community Hospital, are in network. With a Medicare Supplement (Medigap) plan you can see any doctor who accepts Medicare.
How many people in Winston County have Medicare Advantage?
CMS reported 3,749 people enrolled in a Medicare Advantage plan out of 6,239 people with Medicare in Winston County in September 2026, or 60.1%. The Alabama average was 61.5%.
Do you charge Winston County residents for help?
No. Our help is free to you. Carriers pay us a commission when we place a policy, and your premium is the same whether you use an agent or enroll on your own.
When can I enroll or change plans?
You can enroll during your Initial Enrollment Period around your 65th birthday, during the Annual Enrollment Period from October 15 to December 7, or during a Special Enrollment Period if you have a qualifying event such as moving into or within the county.
