Monroe County ranks 46th of 67 Alabama counties by number of residents age 65 and older, with about 4,296 people in that age group, or 22.3% of residents (statewide: 18.3%). The 1,412 residents now between 60 and 64 will become Medicare-eligible soon, and our turning 65 guide walks through it. Among residents 65 and older, 59% are 65 to 74, 31% are 75 to 84 and 10% are 85 or older (Census 2023 estimates). Medicare Advantage enrollment in Monroe County is 68.1% of people with Medicare, higher than the Alabama average of 61.5%. A county average does not tell you which kind of plan fits you. Whether Medicare Advantage or Original Medicare with a Medigap plan is the better fit depends on your doctors, your prescriptions and how you want to handle costs, so we compare both for your ZIP code.

Who has Medicare in Monroe County

Monroe County is a rural county (USDA: urban population of fewer than 5,000, adjacent to a metro area). Plan choices can be narrower than in a city, and we focus on networks that include the doctors and hospital you actually use.

CMS counts 5,557 Medicare beneficiaries in Monroe County (2023). Their average age is 72, about the same as the Alabama average of 72. About 18% are “dual eligible,” meaning they have both Medicare and Medicaid, higher than the state figure of 14%. Where many people have both, special plans for dual-eligible members can matter; see our Medicaid and dual eligible guide, since availability varies by plan and area.

Among people in Original Medicare in Monroe County, CMS reported about 775 emergency room visits and 226 inpatient hospital stays per 1,000 beneficiaries in 2023, which is higher than the state average for emergency visits (600) and close to it for hospital stays (247). About 13% received home health care. These figures describe how people use care under Original Medicare; they do not show what any plan covers or costs.

Sources: CMS Medicare Geographic Variation, 2023; USDA Rural-Urban Continuum Codes, 2023.

Where you live decides which Medicare Advantage and Part D plans you can join, and Monroe County may have a different set of plans than the counties around it. We are not based in Monroe County; we help Monroe County residents by phone or video call.

Hospitals and doctors near Monroe County

CMS Hospital General Information lists no acute care, critical access or children’s hospital located inside Monroe County. Residents often use hospitals in neighboring counties, which matters for Medicare Advantage because a plan’s network may include or leave out the hospital you use. Common questions

Monroe County Medicare FAQ

Can I get help with Medicare in Monroe County by phone?
Yes. We are an independent agency licensed in Alabama, and we help Monroe County residents by phone or video call, so you can compare plans from home. There is no pressure to enroll.
Will my Monroe County doctors and hospitals be covered?
On a Medicare Advantage plan it depends on that plan’s network, so before you enroll we check whether your doctors and the hospitals you use are in network, including hospitals in neighboring counties. With a Medicare Supplement (Medigap) plan you can see any doctor who accepts Medicare.
How many people in Monroe County have Medicare Advantage?
CMS reported 3,659 people enrolled in a Medicare Advantage plan out of 5,372 people with Medicare in Monroe County in September 2026, or 68.1%. The Alabama average was 61.5%.
Do you charge Monroe County residents for help?
No. Our help is free to you. Carriers pay us a commission when we place a policy, and your premium is the same whether you use an agent or enroll on your own.
When can I enroll or change plans?
You can enroll during your Initial Enrollment Period around your 65th birthday, during the Annual Enrollment Period from October 15 to December 7, or during a Special Enrollment Period if you have a qualifying event such as moving into or within the county.
