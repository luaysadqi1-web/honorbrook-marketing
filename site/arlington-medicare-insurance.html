Knowing Arlington's doctors and hospitals

Arlington is a county all its own, packed into a compact stretch directly across the Potomac from Washington, D.C. — home to the Pentagon and busy neighborhoods like Ballston, Clarendon, Rosslyn, Shirlington, and Crystal City / National Landing. For Arlington residents, the anchor for hospital care is Virginia Hospital Center (VHC Health), and most local specialists and primary-care doctors are tied to that and the surrounding D.C.-area systems.

This local knowledge matters more than people expect. With a Medicare Advantage plan, the provider network is everything — a plan that looks great on paper isn't worth much if your Arlington doctors or VHC Health aren't in network. So before you enroll, we verify that your specific doctors and hospitals are covered, and we run your medication list through each plan to confirm your prescriptions are included at a good price. No surprises after you sign up.

Networks vary block to block: Medicare Advantage networks and plan availability differ even within Arlington County. We check exactly what's available and in-network at your Arlington address — not a generic county average — so the plan you pick actually fits the doctors and hospitals you use.

Common questions

Arlington Medicare FAQ

Can I meet with an Arlington Medicare agent in person?
Yes. We meet by appointment — our office is nearby in Vienna, just across the county line, or we can come to you. Plenty of Arlington clients prefer to handle everything by phone, and we're happy to do that too.
Will my Arlington-area doctors and hospitals be covered?
On a Medicare Advantage plan it depends on that plan's network, so we verify your specific doctors and hospitals — including Virginia Hospital Center (VHC Health) — are in network before you enroll. If you choose a Medicare Supplement (Medigap) plan instead, you can see any doctor or hospital that accepts Medicare, with no network to worry about.
Do you charge Arlington residents for help?
No, our help is completely free. The insurance carriers pay us when we place a policy, and by law your premium is exactly the same whether you use an agent or enroll on your own.
When can I enroll or change plans?
During your Initial Enrollment Period around your 65th birthday, during the Annual Enrollment Period (Oct 15 – Dec 7), or during a Special Enrollment Period triggered by a qualifying event — such as moving to or within Arlington.
