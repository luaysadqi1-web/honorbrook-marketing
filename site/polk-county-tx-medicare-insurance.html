With roughly 10,265 residents age 65 and older, Polk County is 61st among 254 Texas counties for older residents. That group is 18.9% of its population, compared with 13.7% across Texas. The 5,076 residents now between 60 and 64 will become Medicare-eligible soon, and our turning 65 guide walks through it. Census 2023 estimates show 74% of the 65-plus population is under 75, 22% is 75 to 84 and 3% is 85 or older. About 47.0% of people with Medicare in Polk County are enrolled in a Medicare Advantage plan. That is lower than the Texas average of 54.9%. A county average does not tell you which kind of plan fits you. Whether Medicare Advantage or Original Medicare with a Medigap plan is the better fit depends on your doctors, your prescriptions and how you want to handle costs, so we compare both for your ZIP code.

Who has Medicare in Polk County

The USDA classifies Polk County as: urban population of 5,000 to 20,000, adjacent to a metro area. In counties of this type, people often travel to a larger neighboring county for specialists, so we check whether your plan’s network reaches there.

CMS counts 20,025 Medicare beneficiaries in Polk County (2023). Their average age is 73, about the same as the Texas average of 73. About 7% are “dual eligible,” meaning they have both Medicare and Medicaid, lower than the state figure of 11%.

Among people in Original Medicare in Polk County, CMS reported about 516 emergency room visits and 193 inpatient hospital stays per 1,000 beneficiaries in 2023, which is close to the state average for emergency visits (580) and lower than it for hospital stays (226). About 7% received home health care. These figures describe how people use care under Original Medicare; they do not show what any plan covers or costs.

Sources: CMS Medicare Geographic Variation, 2023; USDA Rural-Urban Continuum Codes, 2023.

Where you live decides which Medicare Advantage and Part D plans you can join, and Polk County may have a different set of plans than the counties around it. We are not based in Polk County; we help Polk County residents by phone or video call.

Common questions

Polk County Medicare FAQ

Can I get help with Medicare in Polk County by phone?
Yes. You do not need to travel: we are licensed in Texas and help Polk County residents by phone or video, comparing plans from many insurers at no cost to you.
Will my Polk County doctors and hospitals be covered?
On a Medicare Advantage plan it depends on that plan’s network, so before you enroll we check whether your doctors and the hospitals you use, such as CHI St. Lukes Health Memorial Livingston, are in network. With a Medicare Supplement (Medigap) plan you can see any doctor who accepts Medicare.
How many people in Polk County have Medicare Advantage?
CMS reported 8,961 people enrolled in a Medicare Advantage plan out of 19,065 people with Medicare in Polk County in September 2026, or 47.0%. The Texas average was 54.9%.
Do you charge Polk County residents for help?
No. Our help is free to you. Carriers pay us a commission when we place a policy, and your premium is the same whether you use an agent or enroll on your own.
When can I enroll or change plans?
You can enroll during your Initial Enrollment Period around your 65th birthday, during the Annual Enrollment Period from October 15 to December 7, or during a Special Enrollment Period if you have a qualifying event such as moving into or within the county.
