With roughly 3,607 residents age 65 and older, Gonzales County is 127th among 254 Texas counties for older residents. That group is 18.1% of its population, compared with 13.7% across Texas. Another 1,327 residents are age 60 to 64 and will reach Medicare eligibility within about five years (see our turning 65 guide). By age, the 65-and-older group splits 58% ages 65 to 74, 31% ages 75 to 84 and 11% age 85 and up (Census 2023). About 47.4% of people with Medicare in Gonzales County are enrolled in a Medicare Advantage plan. That is lower than the Texas average of 54.9%. Averages cannot choose a plan for you. Medicare Advantage and Original Medicare with a Medigap plan each suit different people, depending on doctors, medicines and how you prefer to handle costs, and we compare both at your ZIP code.

Who has Medicare in Gonzales County

The USDA classifies Gonzales County as: urban population of 5,000 to 20,000, adjacent to a metro area. In counties of this type, people often travel to a larger neighboring county for specialists, so we check whether your plan’s network reaches there.

CMS counts 4,662 Medicare beneficiaries in Gonzales County (2023). Their average age is 74, about the same as the Texas average of 73. About 13% are “dual eligible,” meaning they have both Medicare and Medicaid, close to the state figure of 11%.

Among people in Original Medicare in Gonzales County, CMS reported about 591 emergency room visits and 195 inpatient hospital stays per 1,000 beneficiaries in 2023, which is close to the state average for emergency visits (580) and lower than it for hospital stays (226). About 9% received home health care. These figures describe how people use care under Original Medicare; they do not show what any plan covers or costs.

Sources: CMS Medicare Geographic Variation, 2023; USDA Rural-Urban Continuum Codes, 2023.

Common questions

Gonzales County Medicare FAQ

Can I get help with Medicare in Gonzales County by phone?
Yes. You do not need to travel: we are licensed in Texas and help Gonzales County residents by phone or video, comparing plans from many insurers at no cost to you.
Will my Gonzales County doctors and hospitals be covered?
On a Medicare Advantage plan it depends on that plan’s network, so before you enroll we check whether your doctors and the hospitals you use, such as Memorial Hospital, are in network. With a Medicare Supplement (Medigap) plan you can see any doctor who accepts Medicare.
How many people in Gonzales County have Medicare Advantage?
CMS reported 2,201 people enrolled in a Medicare Advantage plan out of 4,643 people with Medicare in Gonzales County in September 2026, or 47.4%. The Texas average was 54.9%.
Do you charge Gonzales County residents for help?
No, there is no fee. The insurance company pays us when you enroll, and your premium does not go up because you used an agent.
When can I enroll or change plans?
You can enroll during your Initial Enrollment Period around your 65th birthday, during the Annual Enrollment Period from October 15 to December 7, or during a Special Enrollment Period if you have a qualifying event such as moving into or within the county.
