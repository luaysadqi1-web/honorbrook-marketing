With roughly 58,041 residents age 65 and older, Beaufort County is 6th among 46 South Carolina counties for older residents. That group is 29.2% of its population, compared with 19.3% across South Carolina. The 15,930 residents now between 60 and 64 will become Medicare-eligible soon, and our turning 65 guide walks through it. Census 2023 estimates show 56% of the 65-plus population is under 75, 35% is 75 to 84 and 9% is 85 or older. CMS data show 32.6% of Beaufort County residents with Medicare chose Medicare Advantage, which is lower than the 46.7% average across South Carolina. Averages cannot choose a plan for you. Medicare Advantage and Original Medicare with a Medigap plan each suit different people, depending on doctors, medicines and how you prefer to handle costs, and we compare both at your ZIP code.

Who has Medicare in Beaufort County

Beaufort County is a metro county; the U.S. Department of Agriculture describes it as: counties in metro areas of fewer than 250,000 population. Metro counties usually have more hospitals and more insurers competing for Medicare members, so there can be more plans to compare.

CMS counts 61,276 Medicare beneficiaries in Beaufort County (2023). Their average age is 75, older than the South Carolina average of 73. About 2% are “dual eligible,” meaning they have both Medicare and Medicaid, lower than the state figure of 7%.

Among people in Original Medicare in Beaufort County, CMS reported about 498 emergency room visits and 173 inpatient hospital stays per 1,000 beneficiaries in 2023, which is close to the state average for emergency visits (563) and lower than it for hospital stays (202). About 8% received home health care. These figures describe how people use care under Original Medicare; they do not show what any plan covers or costs.

Sources: CMS Medicare Geographic Variation, 2023; USDA Rural-Urban Continuum Codes, 2023.

Insurers choose which counties to serve each year, which means the Medicare Advantage and Part D plans you can pick in Beaufort County can be different from the ones offered next door. We are not based in Beaufort County; we help Beaufort County residents by phone or video call.

Hospitals located in Beaufort County

The Centers for Medicare & Medicaid Services (CMS) lists these hospitals in Beaufort County:

  • Beaufort County Memorial Hospital — Beaufort (acute care; government-owned; emergency services; CMS overall rating 4 of 5)
  • Novant Health Hilton Head Medical Center — Hilton Head Island (acute care; nonprofit; emergency services; CMS overall rating 2 of 5)

Being located in the county is not the same as being in your plan’s network. Each Medicare Advantage plan builds its own network, so we confirm that your hospital and your doctors are included before you choose. Ratings are CMS Care Compare overall hospital star ratings; they describe the hospital, not whether a plan covers it. Source: CMS Hospital General Information.

Common questions

Beaufort County Medicare FAQ

Can I get help with Medicare in Beaufort County by phone?
Yes. We are an independent agency licensed in South Carolina, and we help Beaufort County residents by phone or video call, so you can compare plans from home. There is no pressure to enroll.
Will my Beaufort County doctors and hospitals be covered?
On a Medicare Advantage plan it depends on that plan’s network, so before you enroll we check whether your doctors and the hospitals you use, such as Beaufort County Memorial Hospital, Novant Health Hilton Head Medical Center, are in network. With a Medicare Supplement (Medigap) plan you can see any doctor who accepts Medicare.
How many people in Beaufort County have Medicare Advantage?
CMS reported 20,371 people enrolled in a Medicare Advantage plan out of 62,550 people with Medicare in Beaufort County in September 2026, or 32.6%. The South Carolina average was 46.7%.
Do you charge Beaufort County residents for help?
No, there is no fee. The insurance company pays us when you enroll, and your premium does not go up because you used an agent.
When can I enroll or change plans?
You can enroll during your Initial Enrollment Period around your 65th birthday, during the Annual Enrollment Period from October 15 to December 7, or during a Special Enrollment Period if you have a qualifying event such as moving into or within the county.
