Among North Carolina’s 100 counties, Carteret County comes in 32nd for residents age 65 and up (19,399). Older adults make up 27.9% of the population here; the state figure is 17.6%. What most people do in a county is less important than what fits you. We weigh Medicare Advantage against Original Medicare with Medigap using your doctors, your drugs and your budget.

Who has Medicare in Carteret County

The USDA classifies Carteret County as: urban population of 20,000 or more, adjacent to a metro area. In counties of this type, people often travel to a larger neighboring county for specialists, so we check whether your plan’s network reaches there.

CMS counts 21,928 Medicare beneficiaries in Carteret County (2023). Their average age is 73, about the same as the North Carolina average of 73. About 9% are “dual eligible,” meaning they have both Medicare and Medicaid, lower than the state figure of 13%.

Among people in Original Medicare in Carteret County, CMS reported about 551 emergency room visits and 192 inpatient hospital stays per 1,000 beneficiaries in 2023, which is close to the state average for emergency visits (572) and close to it for hospital stays (210). About 8% received home health care. These figures describe how people use care under Original Medicare; they do not show what any plan covers or costs.

Sources: CMS Medicare Geographic Variation, 2023; USDA Rural-Urban Continuum Codes, 2023.

Common questions

Carteret County Medicare FAQ

Can I get help with Medicare in Carteret County by phone?
Yes. Phone and video appointments are how we serve Carteret County. We hold a North Carolina license, compare plans from many carriers and walk you through them from home, with no pressure to enroll.
Will my Carteret County doctors and hospitals be covered?
On a Medicare Advantage plan it depends on that plan’s network, so before you enroll we check whether your doctors and the hospitals you use, such as Carteret General Hospital, are in network. With a Medicare Supplement (Medigap) plan you can see any doctor who accepts Medicare.
How many people in Carteret County have Medicare Advantage?
CMS reported 9,411 people enrolled in a Medicare Advantage plan out of 22,745 people with Medicare in Carteret County in September 2026, or 41.4%. The North Carolina average was 57.9%.
Do you charge Carteret County residents for help?
No. You pay nothing to work with us. Carriers compensate us, and the price of your plan is the same as if you enrolled directly.
When can I enroll or change plans?
You can enroll during your Initial Enrollment Period around your 65th birthday, during the Annual Enrollment Period from October 15 to December 7, or during a Special Enrollment Period if you have a qualifying event such as moving into or within the county.
