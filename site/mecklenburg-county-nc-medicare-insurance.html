Medicare in Mecklenburg County

Mecklenburg County includes Charlotte, the county seat. According to the U.S. Census Bureau, 2023 population estimates, about 143,690 Mecklenburg County residents are age 65 or older, so choosing the right Medicare coverage is a decision many of your neighbors are making too. Whether you are turning 65, retiring, or reviewing your current plan, we can walk through your choices with you on a call at a time that suits you.

Major hospitals and health systems serving the county include Atrium Health Carolinas Medical Center and Novant Health Presbyterian Medical Center in Charlotte. Medicare Advantage and Part D plan availability and provider networks are set county by county, and they can change every year. Original Medicare (Parts A and B) is the same nationwide, but the private plans you can add to it are not. That is why we check the plans offered where you live and confirm that your doctors and pharmacy are included before you enroll.

Free help available in Mecklenburg County

You have several no-cost places to turn for Medicare questions, and we encourage you to use them alongside our help:

  • NC SHIIP (Seniors’ Health Insurance Information Program, NC Department of Insurance): free, unbiased Medicare counseling at 1-855-408-1212.
  • Centralina Area Agency on Aging: local aging services and referrals for Mecklenburg County, 704-372-2416 (toll-free 1-800-508-5777).
  • 1-800-MEDICARE (1-800-633-4227) or Medicare.gov: official Medicare information.

Mecklenburg County Medicare FAQ

Can I get help with Medicare in Mecklenburg County by phone?
Yes. We are an independent agency licensed in North Carolina, and we help Mecklenburg County residents by phone or video call. We review your doctors, prescriptions and budget, then walk you through the options available at your ZIP code.
Will my Mecklenburg County doctors and Atrium Health or Novant Health be covered?
On a Medicare Advantage plan it depends on that plan's network, so before you enroll we check whether your specific doctors and the hospitals you use are in network. Original Medicare with a Medicare Supplement (Medigap) plan lets you see any provider that accepts Medicare.
Do you charge Mecklenburg County residents for help?
No. Our help is free to you. Carriers compensate us when you enroll, and that does not change your premium.
When can I enroll or change plans?
You can enroll during your Initial Enrollment Period around your 65th birthday, during the Annual Enrollment Period from October 15 to December 7, or during a Special Enrollment Period if you have a qualifying event. We can tell you which applies to you.
