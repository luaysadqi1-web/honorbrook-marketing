Essex County ranks 108th of 133 Virginia counties by number of residents age 65 and older, with about 2,646 people in that age group, or 25.0% of residents (statewide: 17.2%). The 934 residents now between 60 and 64 will become Medicare-eligible soon, and our turning 65 guide walks through it. By age, the 65-and-older group splits 60% ages 65 to 74, 32% ages 75 to 84 and 8% age 85 and up (Census 2023). Medicare Advantage enrollment in Essex County is 42.2% of people with Medicare, close to the Virginia average of 39.6%. A county average does not tell you which kind of plan fits you. Whether Medicare Advantage or Original Medicare with a Medigap plan is the better fit depends on your doctors, your prescriptions and how you want to handle costs, so we compare both for your ZIP code.

Who has Medicare in Essex County

Essex County is a rural county; the U.S. Department of Agriculture describes it as: urban population of fewer than 5,000, adjacent to a metro area. Rural counties tend to have fewer local hospitals and doctors, so a plan’s network and the distance to care matter a great deal.

CMS counts 3,331 Medicare beneficiaries in Essex County (2023). Their average age is 74, about the same as the Virginia average of 74. About 9% are “dual eligible,” meaning they have both Medicare and Medicaid, close to the state figure of 9%.

Among people in Original Medicare in Essex County, CMS reported about 660 emergency room visits and 238 inpatient hospital stays per 1,000 beneficiaries in 2023, which is higher than the state average for emergency visits (589) and higher than it for hospital stays (206). About 7% received home health care. These figures describe how people use care under Original Medicare; they do not show what any plan covers or costs.

Sources: CMS Medicare Geographic Variation, 2023; USDA Rural-Urban Continuum Codes, 2023.

Common questions

Essex County Medicare FAQ

Can I get help with Medicare in Essex County by phone?
Yes. We are an independent agency licensed in Virginia, and we help Essex County residents by phone or video call, so you can compare plans from home. There is no pressure to enroll.
Will my Essex County doctors and hospitals be covered?
On a Medicare Advantage plan it depends on that plan’s network, so before you enroll we check whether your doctors and the hospitals you use, such as VCU Health Tappahannock Hospital, are in network. With a Medicare Supplement (Medigap) plan you can see any doctor who accepts Medicare.
How many people in Essex County have Medicare Advantage?
CMS reported 1,434 people enrolled in a Medicare Advantage plan out of 3,400 people with Medicare in Essex County in September 2026, or 42.2%. The Virginia average was 39.6%.
Do you charge Essex County residents for help?
No. You pay nothing to work with us. Carriers compensate us, and the price of your plan is the same as if you enrolled directly.
When can I enroll or change plans?
You can enroll during your Initial Enrollment Period around your 65th birthday, during the Annual Enrollment Period from October 15 to December 7, or during a Special Enrollment Period if you have a qualifying event such as moving into or within the county.
