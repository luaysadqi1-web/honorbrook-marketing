Among Maryland’s 24 counties, Garrett County comes in 21st for residents age 65 and up (7,163). Older adults make up 25.2% of the population here; the state figure is 17.3%. About 2,442 more people are 60 to 64 and are heading toward Medicare; our turning 65 guide explains their first steps. Census 2023 estimates show 57% of the 65-plus population is under 75, 32% is 75 to 84 and 11% is 85 or older. CMS data show 22.1% of Garrett County residents with Medicare chose Medicare Advantage, which is close to the 24.8% average across Maryland. Averages cannot choose a plan for you. Medicare Advantage and Original Medicare with a Medigap plan each suit different people, depending on doctors, medicines and how you prefer to handle costs, and we compare both at your ZIP code.

Who has Medicare in Garrett County

Garrett County is a rural county (USDA: urban population of fewer than 5,000, adjacent to a metro area). Plan choices can be narrower than in a city, and we focus on networks that include the doctors and hospital you actually use.

CMS counts 7,935 Medicare beneficiaries in Garrett County (2023). Their average age is 73, about the same as the Maryland average of 73. About 18% are “dual eligible,” meaning they have both Medicare and Medicaid, close to the state figure of 17%.

Among people in Original Medicare in Garrett County, CMS reported about 655 emergency room visits and 211 inpatient hospital stays per 1,000 beneficiaries in 2023, which is higher than the state average for emergency visits (555) and close to it for hospital stays (217). About 6% received home health care. These figures describe how people use care under Original Medicare; they do not show what any plan covers or costs.

Sources: CMS Medicare Geographic Variation, 2023; USDA Rural-Urban Continuum Codes, 2023.

Common questions

Garrett County Medicare FAQ

Can I get help with Medicare in Garrett County by phone?
Yes. We are an independent agency licensed in Maryland, and we help Garrett County residents by phone or video call, so you can compare plans from home. There is no pressure to enroll.
Will my Garrett County doctors and hospitals be covered?
On a Medicare Advantage plan it depends on that plan’s network, so before you enroll we check whether your doctors and the hospitals you use, such as Garrett Regional Medical Center, are in network. With a Medicare Supplement (Medigap) plan you can see any doctor who accepts Medicare.
How many people in Garrett County have Medicare Advantage?
CMS reported 1,778 people enrolled in a Medicare Advantage plan out of 8,060 people with Medicare in Garrett County in September 2026, or 22.1%. The Maryland average was 24.8%.
Do you charge Garrett County residents for help?
No, there is no fee. The insurance company pays us when you enroll, and your premium does not go up because you used an agent.
When can I enroll or change plans?
You can enroll during your Initial Enrollment Period around your 65th birthday, during the Annual Enrollment Period from October 15 to December 7, or during a Special Enrollment Period if you have a qualifying event such as moving into or within the county.
