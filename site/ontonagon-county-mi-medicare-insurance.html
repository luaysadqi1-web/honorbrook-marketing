With roughly 2,285 residents age 65 and older, Ontonagon County is 80th among 83 Michigan counties for older residents. That group is 38.7% of its population, compared with 19.2% across Michigan. Another 635 residents are age 60 to 64 and will reach Medicare eligibility within about five years (see our turning 65 guide). By age, the 65-and-older group splits 58% ages 65 to 74, 32% ages 75 to 84 and 10% age 85 and up (Census 2023). Medicare Advantage enrollment in Ontonagon County is 46.7% of people with Medicare, lower than the Michigan average of 63.6%. Averages cannot choose a plan for you. Medicare Advantage and Original Medicare with a Medigap plan each suit different people, depending on doctors, medicines and how you prefer to handle costs, and we compare both at your ZIP code.

Who has Medicare in Ontonagon County

Ontonagon County is a rural county; the U.S. Department of Agriculture describes it as: urban population of fewer than 5,000, not adjacent to a metro area. Rural counties tend to have fewer local hospitals and doctors, so a plan’s network and the distance to care matter a great deal.

CMS counts 2,555 Medicare beneficiaries in Ontonagon County (2023). Their average age is 73, about the same as the Michigan average of 72. About 11% are “dual eligible,” meaning they have both Medicare and Medicaid, lower than the state figure of 20%.

Among people in Original Medicare in Ontonagon County, CMS reported about 595 emergency room visits and 126 inpatient hospital stays per 1,000 beneficiaries in 2023, which is close to the state average for emergency visits (645) and lower than it for hospital stays (245). About 6% received home health care. These figures describe how people use care under Original Medicare; they do not show what any plan covers or costs.

Sources: CMS Medicare Geographic Variation, 2023; USDA Rural-Urban Continuum Codes, 2023.

Insurers choose which counties to serve each year, which means the Medicare Advantage and Part D plans you can pick in Ontonagon County can be different from the ones offered next door. We are not based in Ontonagon County; we help Ontonagon County residents by phone or video call.

Hospitals and doctors near Ontonagon County

CMS Hospital General Information lists no acute care, critical access or children’s hospital located inside Ontonagon County. Residents often use hospitals in neighboring counties, which matters for Medicare Advantage because a plan’s network may include or leave out the hospital you use. We check your hospitals and doctors before you enroll.

Common questions

Ontonagon County Medicare FAQ

Can I get help with Medicare in Ontonagon County by phone?
Yes. Phone and video appointments are how we serve Ontonagon County. We hold a Michigan license, compare plans from many carriers and walk you through them from home, with no pressure to enroll.
Will my Ontonagon County doctors and hospitals be covered?
On a Medicare Advantage plan it depends on that plan’s network, so before you enroll we check whether your doctors and the hospitals you use are in network, including hospitals in neighboring counties. With a Medicare Supplement (Medigap) plan you can see any doctor who accepts Medicare.
How many people in Ontonagon County have Medicare Advantage?
CMS reported 1,173 people enrolled in a Medicare Advantage plan out of 2,514 people with Medicare in Ontonagon County in September 2026, or 46.7%. The Michigan average was 63.6%.
Do you charge Ontonagon County residents for help?
No. You pay nothing to work with us. Carriers compensate us, and the price of your plan is the same as if you enrolled directly.
When can I enroll or change plans?
You can enroll during your Initial Enrollment Period around your 65th birthday, during the Annual Enrollment Period from October 15 to December 7, or during a Special Enrollment Period if you have a qualifying event such as moving into or within the county.
