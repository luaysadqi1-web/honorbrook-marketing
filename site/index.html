Common questions

Answers, in plain English

Does it really cost nothing to work with you?
Correct — our help is 100% free. Insurance carriers pay us a commission when we place a policy, and by law your premium is exactly the same whether you use an agent or enroll directly. The difference: with us, someone compares all your options and answers the phone after you enroll.
When can I enroll in or change a Medicare plan?
Most people enroll when they turn 65 (a 7-month window around your birthday) and can change plans each year during the Annual Enrollment Period, October 15 – December 7. Certain life events — moving, losing coverage, qualifying for Extra Help — open special windows year-round. Medicare Supplement and final expense policies can be applied for any time of year.
What's the difference between Medicare Advantage and a Supplement?
Advantage plans bundle everything into one plan with low or $0 premiums but use provider networks and copays. Supplements (Medigap) cost more monthly but let you see any doctor who accepts Medicare with minimal out-of-pocket costs. There's no universally "better" choice — it depends on your health, budget, doctors, and travel habits. That's exactly what we help you weigh.
Can I get final expense coverage with health problems?
Almost always, yes. Simplified-issue policies ask a few health questions but require no exam, and guaranteed-issue options exist for serious conditions. Because we work with many carriers, we match your specific health history to the carrier most likely to approve you at the best rate.
I already have a Medicare plan. Why review it?
Plans change every single year — premiums, drug lists, doctor networks, and benefits all shift. The plan that was perfect two years ago may be costing you money today. We run a free annual review each fall so you're never stuck overpaying.
Which states do you serve?
We're licensed in Virginia, Maryland, Georgia, Texas, Michigan, North Carolina, South Carolina, Alabama, Louisiana, Indiana, West Virginia, and growing. Everything can be handled by phone — no office visit needed.
