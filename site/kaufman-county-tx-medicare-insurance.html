Among Texas’s 254 counties, Kaufman County comes in 37th for residents age 65 and up (19,347). Older adults make up 10.4% of the population here; the state figure is 13.7%. Another 8,931 residents are age 60 to 64 and will reach Medicare eligibility within about five years (see our turning 65 guide). About 54.2% of people with Medicare in Kaufman County are enrolled in a Medicare Advantage plan. That is close to the Texas average of 54.9%. What most people do in a county is less important than what fits you. We weigh Medicare Advantage against Original Medicare with Medigap using your doctors, your drugs and your budget.

Who has Medicare in Kaufman County

Kaufman County sits in a metro area, which the USDA defines as counties in metro areas of 1 million population or more. That usually means more doctors, more hospitals and more plan choices in the area.

CMS counts 25,437 Medicare beneficiaries in Kaufman County (2023). Their average age is 72, about the same as the Texas average of 73. About 11% are “dual eligible,” meaning they have both Medicare and Medicaid, close to the state figure of 11%.

Among people in Original Medicare in Kaufman County, CMS reported about 680 emergency room visits and 233 inpatient hospital stays per 1,000 beneficiaries in 2023, which is higher than the state average for emergency visits (580) and close to it for hospital stays (226). About 10% received home health care. These figures describe how people use care under Original Medicare; they do not show what any plan covers or costs.

Sources: CMS Medicare Geographic Variation, 2023; USDA Rural-Urban Continuum Codes, 2023.

Common questions

Kaufman County Medicare FAQ

Can I get help with Medicare in Kaufman County by phone?
Yes. We are an independent agency licensed in Texas, and we help Kaufman County residents by phone or video call, so you can compare plans from home. There is no pressure to enroll.
Will my Kaufman County doctors and hospitals be covered?
On a Medicare Advantage plan it depends on that plan’s network, so before you enroll we check whether your doctors and the hospitals you use, such as Texas Health Presbyterian Hospital Kaufman, are in network. With a Medicare Supplement (Medigap) plan you can see any doctor who accepts Medicare.
How many people in Kaufman County have Medicare Advantage?
CMS reported 14,814 people enrolled in a Medicare Advantage plan out of 27,334 people with Medicare in Kaufman County in September 2026, or 54.2%. The Texas average was 54.9%.
Do you charge Kaufman County residents for help?
No, there is no fee. The insurance company pays us when you enroll, and your premium does not go up because you used an agent.
When can I enroll or change plans?
You can enroll during your Initial Enrollment Period around your 65th birthday, during the Annual Enrollment Period from October 15 to December 7, or during a Special Enrollment Period if you have a qualifying event such as moving into or within the county.
