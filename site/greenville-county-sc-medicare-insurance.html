Medicare in Greenville County

Greenville County includes communities such as Greenville, Greer, Mauldin, Simpsonville, Fountain Inn and Travelers Rest. The U.S. Census Bureau's 2023 population estimates put the county's 65-and-older population at 95,536 (U.S. Census Bureau, 2023 population estimates). Hospital care for many residents runs through Prisma Health Greenville Memorial Hospital and the Bon Secours St. Francis hospitals (Downtown and Eastside) in Greenville.

Medicare Advantage and Part D plan availability and provider networks are set county by county, so a plan offered in one part of South Carolina may not be offered in Greenville County. We check what is available at your ZIP code and whether your doctors and hospitals are in the plan's network before you enroll. Honorbrook is based in Tysons, Virginia, and works with Greenville County residents by phone or video.

Greenville County ranks 2nd of 46 South Carolina counties by number of residents age 65 and older, with about 95,536 people in that age group, or 17.1% of residents (statewide: 19.3%). Another 34,027 residents are age 60 to 64 and will reach Medicare eligibility within about five years (see our turning 65 guide). Among residents 65 and older, 59% are 65 to 74, 32% are 75 to 84 and 9% are 85 or older (Census 2023 estimates). About 49.4% of people with Medicare in Greenville County are enrolled in a Medicare Advantage plan. That is close to the South Carolina average of 46.7%. A county average does not tell you which kind of plan fits you. Whether Medicare Advantage or Original Medicare with a Medigap plan is the better fit depends on your doctors, your prescriptions and how you want to handle costs, so we compare both for your ZIP code.

Free help available in Greenville County

You do not have to choose a plan alone, and you have several free, unbiased places to turn:

  • I-CARE, South Carolina's State Health Insurance Assistance Program, offers free Medicare counseling through the South Carolina Department on Aging: 1-800-868-9095.
  • Appalachian Area Agency on Aging, run by the SC Appalachian Council of Governments, serves Anderson, Cherokee, Greenville, Oconee, Pickens and Spartanburg counties: (864) 242-9733 or visit its website.
  • 1-800-MEDICARE (1-800-633-4227) answers general Medicare questions at any time.

We are happy to work alongside these resources. Honorbrook is an independent agency, not connected with or endorsed by the government or the federal Medicare program.

Common questions

Greenville County Medicare FAQ

Can I get help with Medicare in Greenville County by phone?
Yes. Honorbrook is an independent agency licensed in South Carolina, and we help Greenville County residents by phone or video call. We review your options, check your doctors and prescriptions, and answer questions without any pressure.
Will my Greenville doctors and hospitals be covered?
On a Medicare Advantage plan it depends on the plan's network, so before you enroll we check that your doctors and the hospitals you use, such as Prisma Health Greenville Memorial and Bon Secours St. Francis, are in network. With a Medicare Supplement (Medigap) plan, you can see any provider who accepts Medicare.
Do you charge Greenville County residents for help?
No. Our help is free to you. Carriers pay us a commission when we place a policy, and your premium is the same whether you use an agent or enroll directly.
When can I enroll or change plans?
You can enroll during your Initial Enrollment Period around your 65th birthday, during the Annual Enrollment Period from October 15 to December 7, or during a Special Enrollment Period if you have a qualifying event, such as moving into or within Greenville County.
