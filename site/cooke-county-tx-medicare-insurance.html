Among Texas’s 254 counties, Cooke County comes in 72nd for residents age 65 and up (8,531). Older adults make up 19.5% of the population here; the state figure is 13.7%. The 3,113 residents now between 60 and 64 will become Medicare-eligible soon, and our turning 65 guide walks through it. By age, the 65-and-older group splits 59% ages 65 to 74, 31% ages 75 to 84 and 10% age 85 and up (Census 2023). About 41.9% of people with Medicare in Cooke County are enrolled in a Medicare Advantage plan. That is lower than the Texas average of 54.9%. What most people do in a county is less important than what fits you. We weigh Medicare Advantage against Original Medicare with Medigap using your doctors, your drugs and your budget.

Who has Medicare in Cooke County

The USDA classifies Cooke County as: urban population of 5,000 to 20,000, adjacent to a metro area. In counties of this type, people often travel to a larger neighboring county for specialists, so we check whether your plan’s network reaches there.

CMS counts 9,588 Medicare beneficiaries in Cooke County (2023). Their average age is 74, about the same as the Texas average of 73. About 7% are “dual eligible,” meaning they have both Medicare and Medicaid, lower than the state figure of 11%.

Among people in Original Medicare in Cooke County, CMS reported about 621 emergency room visits and 259 inpatient hospital stays per 1,000 beneficiaries in 2023, which is close to the state average for emergency visits (580) and higher than it for hospital stays (226). About 12% received home health care. These figures describe how people use care under Original Medicare; they do not show what any plan covers or costs.

Sources: CMS Medicare Geographic Variation, 2023; USDA Rural-Urban Continuum Codes, 2023.

Insurers choose which counties to serve each year, which means the Medicare Advantage and Part D plans you can pick in Cooke County can be different from the ones offered next door. We are not based in Cooke County; we help Cooke County residents by phone or video call.

Hospitals located in Cooke County

The Centers for Medicare & Medicaid Services (CMS) lists these hospitals in Cooke County:

  • Muenster Memorial Hospital — Muenster (critical access; government-owned; emergency services)
  • North Texas Medical Center — Gainesville (acute care; nonprofit; emergency services; CMS overall rating 4 of 5)

Being located in the county is not the same as being in your plan’s network. Each Medicare Advantage plan builds its own network, so we confirm that your hospital and your doctors are included before you choose. Ratings are CMS Care Compare overall hospital star ratings; they describe the hospital, not whether a plan covers it. Source: CMS Hospital General Information.

Where to get free Medicare help in Cooke County

  • Texas’s SHIP program, HICAP, gives free Medicare counseling that is not tied to any insurer: 1-800-252-9240.
  • To reach your local Area Agency on Aging, use the Eldercare Locator or call 1-800-677-1116.
  • 1-800-MEDICARE (1-800-633-4227) is the federal Medicare helpline.

These services are worth using. Where we add to them is comparing specific plans and helping you enroll and stay on track year after year.

Common questions

Cooke County Medicare FAQ

Can I get help with Medicare in Cooke County by phone?
Yes. You do not need to travel: we are licensed in Texas and help Cooke County residents by phone or video, comparing plans from many insurers at no cost to you.
Will my Cooke County doctors and hospitals be covered?
On a Medicare Advantage plan it depends on that plan’s network, so before you enroll we check whether your doctors and the hospitals you use, such as Muenster Memorial Hospital, North Texas Medical Center, are in network. With a Medicare Supplement (Medigap) plan you can see any doctor who accepts Medicare.
How many people in Cooke County have Medicare Advantage?
CMS reported 4,172 people enrolled in a Medicare Advantage plan out of 9,965 people with Medicare in Cooke County in September 2026, or 41.9%. The Texas average was 54.9%.
Do you charge Cooke County residents for help?
No, there is no fee. The insurance company pays us when you enroll, and your premium does not go up because you used an agent.
When can I enroll or change plans?
You can enroll during your Initial Enrollment Period around your 65th birthday, during the Annual Enrollment Period from October 15 to December 7, or during a Special Enrollment Period if you have a qualifying event such as moving into or within the county.
