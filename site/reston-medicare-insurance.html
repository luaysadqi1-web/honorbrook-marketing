Common questions

Reston Medicare FAQ

Can I meet with a Reston Medicare agent in person?
Yes. We meet Reston residents by appointment — our office is just a short drive away in Vienna, or we can come to you. If you'd rather keep it simple, we handle everything by phone. Whatever's easiest for you works for us.
Will my Reston-area doctors and hospitals be covered?
On a Medicare Advantage plan it depends on the plan's network, so before you enroll we verify your specific doctors and hospitals — for example Reston Hospital Center, part of HCA Virginia — are in network. With a Medicare Supplement (Medigap) plan there's no network, so you can see any doctor or hospital that accepts Medicare.
Do you charge Reston residents for help?
No, our help is completely free. Carriers pay us a commission when we place a policy, and by law your premium is exactly the same whether you use an agent or enroll on your own. You get local guidance at no added cost.
When can I enroll or change plans?
During your Initial Enrollment Period around your 65th birthday, during the Annual Enrollment Period (Oct 15 – Dec 7), or during a Special Enrollment Period triggered by a qualifying life event — such as moving to or within Reston.
