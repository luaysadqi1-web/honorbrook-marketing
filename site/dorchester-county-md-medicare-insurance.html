Dorchester County ranks 20th of 24 Maryland counties by number of residents age 65 and older, with about 7,760 people in that age group, or 23.6% of residents (statewide: 17.3%). The 2,626 residents now between 60 and 64 will become Medicare-eligible soon, and our turning 65 guide walks through it. By age, the 65-and-older group splits 58% ages 65 to 74, 32% ages 75 to 84 and 10% age 85 and up (Census 2023). Medicare Advantage enrollment in Dorchester County is 26.2% of people with Medicare, close to the Maryland average of 24.8%. What most people do in a county is less important than what fits you. We weigh Medicare Advantage against Original Medicare with Medigap using your doctors, your drugs and your budget.

Who has Medicare in Dorchester County

Dorchester County is a non-metro county with towns and small cities (USDA: urban population of 5,000 to 20,000, adjacent to a metro area). Care is often split between local providers and a bigger hospital elsewhere, which is worth testing against each plan’s network.

CMS counts 9,107 Medicare beneficiaries in Dorchester County (2023). Their average age is 73, about the same as the Maryland average of 73. About 22% are “dual eligible,” meaning they have both Medicare and Medicaid, higher than the state figure of 17%. Where many people have both, special plans for dual-eligible members can matter; see our Medicaid and dual eligible guide, since availability varies by plan and area.

Among people in Original Medicare in Dorchester County, CMS reported about 648 emergency room visits and 181 inpatient hospital stays per 1,000 beneficiaries in 2023, which is higher than the state average for emergency visits (555) and lower than it for hospital stays (217). About 11% received home health care. These figures describe how people use care under Original Medicare; they do not show what any plan covers or costs.

Sources: CMS Medicare Geographic Variation, 2023; USDA Rural-Urban Continuum Codes, 2023.

Hospitals and doctors near Dorchester County

CMS Hospital General Information lists no acute care, critical access or children’s hospital located inside Dorchester County. Residents often use hospitals in neighboring counties, which matters for Medicare Advantage because a plan’s network may include or leave out the hospital you use. We check your hospitals and doctors before you enroll.

Common questions

Dorchester County Medicare FAQ

Can I get help with Medicare in Dorchester County by phone?
Yes. We are an independent agency licensed in Maryland, and we help Dorchester County residents by phone or video call, so you can compare plans from home. There is no pressure to enroll.
Will my Dorchester County doctors and hospitals be covered?
On a Medicare Advantage plan it depends on that plan’s network, so before you enroll we check whether your doctors and the hospitals you use are in network, including hospitals in neighboring counties. With a Medicare Supplement (Medigap) plan you can see any doctor who accepts Medicare.
How many people in Dorchester County have Medicare Advantage?
CMS reported 2,397 people enrolled in a Medicare Advantage plan out of 9,138 people with Medicare in Dorchester County in September 2026, or 26.2%. The Maryland average was 24.8%.
Do you charge Dorchester County residents for help?
No. You pay nothing to work with us. Carriers compensate us, and the price of your plan is the same as if you enrolled directly.
When can I enroll or change plans?
You can enroll during your Initial Enrollment Period around your 65th birthday, during the Annual Enrollment Period from October 15 to December 7, or during a Special Enrollment Period if you have a qualifying event such as moving into or within the county.
