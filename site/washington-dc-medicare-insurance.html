Insurance help for DC residents

Choosing coverage shouldn't feel like a maze. Whether you're turning 65, already on Medicare and wondering if you're overpaying, retiring early and need coverage until you qualify, or looking to protect your family with life insurance, we explain your options in plain English. Honorbrook works with DC individuals, couples, and families to compare plans honestly and find the fit that matches your doctors, prescriptions, and budget.

There is no fee for our help. Carriers pay us a commission when we place a policy, and your premium is the same whether you use an agent or sign up on your own. We don't work for any one insurance company, so our job is to help you find the right fit, not to sell a particular brand.

Coverage we offer in DC

  • Medicare Advantage: all-in-one Part C plans that can include dental, vision, and drug coverage.
  • Medicare Supplement (Medigap): see any doctor who accepts Medicare, with predictable out-of-pocket costs.
  • Part D drug plans: we look for the lowest true annual cost for your medication list.
  • ACA plans (under 65): individual and family coverage, including subsidy help if you qualify. The District runs its own marketplace, DC Health Link, for under-65 coverage.
  • Private health insurance and HSA-eligible plans for people who buy their own coverage.
  • Final expense: small whole-life coverage to protect your family from funeral and burial costs.
  • Life insurance, including mortgage protection, coverage for veterans, and indexed universal life.
  • Fixed annuities and retirement rollovers.
  • Dental, vision and hearing, hospital indemnity, and cancer coverage.
  • Group health and ICHRA for DC employers.

Common questions

Washington, DC Medicare & insurance FAQ

Do you charge DC residents for help?
No. Our help costs you nothing. Carriers pay us a commission when we place a policy, and your premium is the same whether you use an agent or enroll on your own.
Which DC neighborhoods do you serve?
All of Washington, DC, by phone or video. We help residents in Capitol Hill, Georgetown, Columbia Heights, Petworth, Anacostia, and Tenleytown and everywhere in between. No office visit needed.
What plans can I get in DC?
Availability varies by where you live. We compare Medicare Advantage, Medigap, and Part D drug plans, plus ACA, life and final expense coverage, and we check what is offered at your address before you choose.
Can you help if I'm new to DC or moving?
Yes. Moving to, from, or within DC can open a Special Enrollment Period that lets you pick a new plan outside the usual windows. Call us and we will walk through your options based on your new address.
