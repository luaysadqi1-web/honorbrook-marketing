Among Louisiana’s 64 parishes, Vermilion Parish comes in 18th for residents age 65 and up (9,725). Older adults make up 17.1% of the population here; the state figure is 17.4%. The 3,846 residents now between 60 and 64 will become Medicare-eligible soon, and our turning 65 guide walks through it. Census 2023 estimates show 61% of the 65-plus population is under 75, 28% is 75 to 84 and 11% is 85 or older. CMS data show 49.0% of Vermilion Parish residents with Medicare chose Medicare Advantage, which is lower than the 57.5% average across Louisiana. What most people do in a county is less important than what fits you. We weigh Medicare Advantage against Original Medicare with Medigap using your doctors, your drugs and your budget.

Who has Medicare in Vermilion Parish

The U.S. Department of Agriculture classifies Vermilion Parish as a metro county (counties in metro areas of 250,000 to 1 million population). Counties like this tend to have a wider choice of hospitals and Medicare plans than rural ones, though networks still differ plan by plan.

CMS counts 12,681 Medicare beneficiaries in Vermilion Parish (2023). Their average age is 73, about the same as the Louisiana average of 72. About 19% are “dual eligible,” meaning they have both Medicare and Medicaid, close to the state figure of 20%.

Among people in Original Medicare in Vermilion Parish, CMS reported about 616 emergency room visits and 220 inpatient hospital stays per 1,000 beneficiaries in 2023, which is close to the state average for emergency visits (648) and close to it for hospital stays (237). About 10% received home health care. These figures describe how people use care under Original Medicare; they do not show what any plan covers or costs.

Sources: CMS Medicare Geographic Variation, 2023; USDA Rural-Urban Continuum Codes, 2023.

Insurers choose which counties to serve each year, which means the Medicare Advantage and Part D plans you can pick in Vermilion Parish can be different from the ones offered next door. We are not based in Vermilion Parish; we help Vermilion Parish residents by phone or video call.

Common questions

Vermilion Parish Medicare FAQ

Can I get help with Medicare in Vermilion Parish by phone?
Yes. Phone and video appointments are how we serve Vermilion Parish. We hold a Louisiana license, compare plans from many carriers and walk you through them from home, with no pressure to enroll.
Will my Vermilion Parish doctors and hospitals be covered?
On a Medicare Advantage plan it depends on that plan’s network, so before you enroll we check whether your doctors and the hospitals you use, such as Abbeville General Hospital, Abrom Kaplan Memorial Hospital, are in network. With a Medicare Supplement (Medigap) plan you can see any doctor who accepts Medicare.
How many people in Vermilion Parish have Medicare Advantage?
CMS reported 6,275 people enrolled in a Medicare Advantage plan out of 12,794 people with Medicare in Vermilion Parish in September 2026, or 49.0%. The Louisiana average was 57.5%.
Do you charge Vermilion Parish residents for help?
No. Our help is free to you. Carriers pay us a commission when we place a policy, and your premium is the same whether you use an agent or enroll on your own.
When can I enroll or change plans?
You can enroll during your Initial Enrollment Period around your 65th birthday, during the Annual Enrollment Period from October 15 to December 7, or during a Special Enrollment Period if you have a qualifying event such as moving into or within the county.
