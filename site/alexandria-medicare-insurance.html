Knowing Alexandria's doctors and hospitals

Common questions

Alexandria Medicare FAQ

Can I meet with an Alexandria Medicare agent in person?
Yes. By appointment — our office is nearby in Vienna, or we can come to you in Alexandria or handle everything by phone, whichever is easiest for you.
Will my Alexandria-area doctors and hospitals be covered?
On a Medicare Advantage plan it depends on that plan's provider network, so we verify your specific doctors and hospitals — including Inova Alexandria Hospital — are in network before you enroll. With a Medicare Supplement (Medigap) plan, you can see any doctor or hospital that accepts Medicare.
Do you charge Alexandria residents for help?
No. Our help is free. Carriers pay us a commission when we place a policy, and by law your premium is the same whether you use an agent or enroll on your own.
When can I enroll or change plans?
During your Initial Enrollment Period around your 65th birthday, during the Annual Enrollment Period (Oct 15 – Dec 7), or during a Special Enrollment Period triggered by a qualifying event such as moving to or within Alexandria.
