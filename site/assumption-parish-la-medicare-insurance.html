Assumption Parish ranks 41st of 64 Louisiana parishes by number of residents age 65 and older, with about 4,199 people in that age group, or 20.8% of residents (statewide: 17.4%). About 1,524 more people are 60 to 64 and are heading toward Medicare; our turning 65 guide explains their first steps. CMS data show 62.7% of Assumption Parish residents with Medicare chose Medicare Advantage, which is higher than the 57.5% average across Louisiana. Averages cannot choose a plan for you. Medicare Advantage and Original Medicare with a Medigap plan each suit different people, depending on doctors, medicines and how you prefer to handle costs, and we compare both at your ZIP code.

Who has Medicare in Assumption Parish

Assumption Parish is a metro county; the U.S. Department of Agriculture describes it as: counties in metro areas of 250,000 to 1 million population. Metro counties usually have more hospitals and more insurers competing for Medicare members, so there can be more plans to compare.

CMS counts 5,343 Medicare beneficiaries in Assumption Parish (2023). Their average age is 72, about the same as the Louisiana average of 72. About 19% are “dual eligible,” meaning they have both Medicare and Medicaid, close to the state figure of 20%.

Among people in Original Medicare in Assumption Parish, CMS reported about 650 emergency room visits and 187 inpatient hospital stays per 1,000 beneficiaries in 2023, which is close to the state average for emergency visits (648) and lower than it for hospital stays (237). About 10% received home health care. These figures describe how people use care under Original Medicare; they do not show what any plan covers or costs.

Sources: CMS Medicare Geographic Variation, 2023; USDA Rural-Urban Continuum Codes, 2023.

Insurers choose which counties to serve each year, which means the Medicare Advantage and Part D plans you can pick in Assumption Parish can be different from the ones offered next door. We are not based in Assumption Parish; we help Assumption Parish residents by phone or video call.

Common questions

Assumption Parish Medicare FAQ

Can I get help with Medicare in Assumption Parish by phone?
Yes. Phone and video appointments are how we serve Assumption Parish. We hold a Louisiana license, compare plans from many carriers and walk you through them from home, with no pressure to enroll.
Will my Assumption Parish doctors and hospitals be covered?
On a Medicare Advantage plan it depends on that plan’s network, so before you enroll we check whether your doctors and the hospitals you use, such as Assumption Community Hospital, are in network. With a Medicare Supplement (Medigap) plan you can see any doctor who accepts Medicare.
How many people in Assumption Parish have Medicare Advantage?
CMS reported 3,287 people enrolled in a Medicare Advantage plan out of 5,240 people with Medicare in Assumption Parish in September 2026, or 62.7%. The Louisiana average was 57.5%.
Do you charge Assumption Parish residents for help?
No. Our help is free to you. Carriers pay us a commission when we place a policy, and your premium is the same whether you use an agent or enroll on your own.
When can I enroll or change plans?
You can enroll during your Initial Enrollment Period around your 65th birthday, during the Annual Enrollment Period from October 15 to December 7, or during a Special Enrollment Period if you have a qualifying event such as moving into or within the county.
