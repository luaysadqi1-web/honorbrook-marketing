St. Landry Parish ranks 16th of 64 Louisiana parishes by number of residents age 65 and older, with about 14,069 people in that age group, or 17.3% of residents (statewide: 17.4%). Another 5,349 residents are age 60 to 64 and will reach Medicare eligibility within about five years (see our turning 65 guide). Among residents 65 and older, 61% are 65 to 74, 30% are 75 to 84 and 9% are 85 or older (Census 2023 estimates). About 48.6% of people with Medicare in St. Landry Parish are enrolled in a Medicare Advantage plan. That is lower than the Louisiana average of 57.5%. A county average does not tell you which kind of plan fits you. Whether Medicare Advantage or Original Medicare with a Medigap plan is the better fit depends on your doctors, your prescriptions and how you want to handle costs, so we compare both for your ZIP code.

Medicare Advantage and Part D plan availability and provider networks are set county by county, so the plans open to you in St. Landry Parish may differ from those in a neighboring county. We are not based in St. Landry Parish; we help St. Landry Parish residents by phone or video call.

Hospitals located in St. Landry Parish

The Centers for Medicare & Medicaid Services (CMS) lists these hospitals in St. Landry Parish:

  • Acadian Medical Center — Eunice (acute care; for-profit; emergency services; CMS overall rating 3 of 5)
  • Opelousas General Health System — Opelousas (acute care; government-owned; emergency services; CMS overall rating 2 of 5)

Being located in the county is not the same as being in your plan’s network. Each Medicare Advantage plan builds its own network, so we confirm that your hospital and your doctors are included before you choose. Ratings are CMS Care Compare overall hospital star ratings; they describe the hospital, not whether a plan covers it. Source: CMS Hospital General Information.

Free help available in St. Landry Parish

  • SHIIP, the Louisiana state health insurance assistance program (SHIP), offers free, unbiased Medicare counseling: 1-800-259-5301.
  • Eldercare Locator connects you to your local Area Agency on Aging: 1-800-677-1116 (eldercare.acl.gov).
  • 1-800-MEDICARE (1-800-633-4227) is the federal Medicare helpline.

Start with any of these if you like. We pick up where they leave off: comparing the actual plans for your doctors and drugs, and staying with you each year at renewal.

Common questions

St. Landry Parish Medicare FAQ

Can I get help with Medicare in St. Landry Parish by phone?
Yes. We are an independent agency licensed in Louisiana, and we help St. Landry Parish residents by phone or video call, so you can compare plans from home. There is no pressure to enroll.
Will my St. Landry Parish doctors and hospitals be covered?
On a Medicare Advantage plan it depends on that plan’s network, so before you enroll we check whether your doctors and the hospitals you use, such as Acadian Medical Center, Opelousas General Health System, are in network. With a Medicare Supplement (Medigap) plan you can see any doctor who accepts Medicare.
How many people in St. Landry Parish have Medicare Advantage?
CMS reported 10,093 people enrolled in a Medicare Advantage plan out of 20,770 people with Medicare in St. Landry Parish in September 2026, or 48.6%. The Louisiana average was 57.5%.
Do you charge St. Landry Parish residents for help?
No. You pay nothing to work with us. Carriers compensate us, and the price of your plan is the same as if you enrolled directly.
When can I enroll or change plans?
You can enroll during your Initial Enrollment Period around your 65th birthday, during the Annual Enrollment Period from October 15 to December 7, or during a Special Enrollment Period if you have a qualifying event such as moving into or within the county.
