With roughly 8,296 residents age 65 and older, Roscommon County is 44th among 83 Michigan counties for older residents. That group is 34.8% of its population, compared with 19.2% across Michigan. The 2,587 residents now between 60 and 64 will become Medicare-eligible soon, and our turning 65 guide walks through it. By age, the 65-and-older group splits 62% ages 65 to 74, 31% ages 75 to 84 and 7% age 85 and up (Census 2023). CMS data show 64.9% of Roscommon County residents with Medicare chose Medicare Advantage, which is close to the 63.6% average across Michigan. Averages cannot choose a plan for you. Medicare Advantage and Original Medicare with a Medigap plan each suit different people, depending on doctors, medicines and how you prefer to handle costs, and we compare both at your ZIP code.

Who has Medicare in Roscommon County

Roscommon County is a non-metro county with towns and small cities (USDA: urban population of 5,000 to 20,000, not adjacent to a metro area). Care is often split between local providers and a bigger hospital elsewhere, which is worth testing against each plan’s network.

CMS counts 10,361 Medicare beneficiaries in Roscommon County (2023). Their average age is 72, about the same as the Michigan average of 72. About 18% are “dual eligible,” meaning they have both Medicare and Medicaid, close to the state figure of 20%.

Among people in Original Medicare in Roscommon County, CMS reported about 619 emergency room visits and 197 inpatient hospital stays per 1,000 beneficiaries in 2023, which is close to the state average for emergency visits (645) and lower than it for hospital stays (245). About 8% received home health care. These figures describe how people use care under Original Medicare; they do not show what any plan covers or costs.

Sources: CMS Medicare Geographic Variation, 2023; USDA Rural-Urban Continuum Codes, 2023.

Where to get free Medicare help in Roscommon County

  • The federal Medicare helpline is 1-800-MEDICARE, 1-800-633-4227.

These services are worth using. Where we add to them is comparing specific plans and helping you enroll and stay on track year after year.

Common questions

Roscommon County Medicare FAQ

Can I get help with Medicare in Roscommon County by phone?
Yes. You do not need to travel: we are licensed in Michigan and help Roscommon County residents by phone or video, comparing plans from many insurers at no cost to you.
Will my Roscommon County doctors and hospitals be covered?
On a Medicare Advantage plan it depends on that plan’s network, so before you enroll we check whether your doctors and the hospitals you use are in network, including hospitals in neighboring counties. With a Medicare Supplement (Medigap) plan you can see any doctor who accepts Medicare.
How many people in Roscommon County have Medicare Advantage?
CMS reported 6,786 people enrolled in a Medicare Advantage plan out of 10,455 people with Medicare in Roscommon County in September 2026, or 64.9%. The Michigan average was 63.6%.
Do you charge Roscommon County residents for help?
No, there is no fee. The insurance company pays us when you enroll, and your premium does not go up because you used an agent.
When can I enroll or change plans?
You can enroll during your Initial Enrollment Period around your 65th birthday, during the Annual Enrollment Period from October 15 to December 7, or during a Special Enrollment Period if you have a qualifying event such as moving into or within the county.
