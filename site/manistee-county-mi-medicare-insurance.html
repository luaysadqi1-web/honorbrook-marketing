Manistee County ranks 53rd of 83 Michigan counties by number of residents age 65 and older, with about 7,368 people in that age group, or 28.8% of residents (statewide: 19.2%). The 2,162 residents now between 60 and 64 will become Medicare-eligible soon, and our turning 65 guide walks through it. Census 2023 estimates show 60% of the 65-plus population is under 75, 31% is 75 to 84 and 9% is 85 or older. CMS data show 63.0% of Manistee County residents with Medicare chose Medicare Advantage, which is close to the 63.6% average across Michigan. Averages cannot choose a plan for you. Medicare Advantage and Original Medicare with a Medigap plan each suit different people, depending on doctors, medicines and how you prefer to handle costs, and we compare both at your ZIP code.

Who has Medicare in Manistee County

Manistee County is a non-metro county with towns and small cities (USDA: urban population of 5,000 to 20,000, adjacent to a metro area). Care is often split between local providers and a bigger hospital elsewhere, which is worth testing against each plan’s network.

CMS counts 8,537 Medicare beneficiaries in Manistee County (2023). Their average age is 73, about the same as the Michigan average of 72. About 19% are “dual eligible,” meaning they have both Medicare and Medicaid, close to the state figure of 20%.

Among people in Original Medicare in Manistee County, CMS reported about 696 emergency room visits and 180 inpatient hospital stays per 1,000 beneficiaries in 2023, which is close to the state average for emergency visits (645) and lower than it for hospital stays (245). About 7% received home health care. These figures describe how people use care under Original Medicare; they do not show what any plan covers or costs.

Sources: CMS Medicare Geographic Variation, 2023; USDA Rural-Urban Continuum Codes, 2023.

Common questions

Manistee County Medicare FAQ

Can I get help with Medicare in Manistee County by phone?
Yes. You do not need to travel: we are licensed in Michigan and help Manistee County residents by phone or video, comparing plans from many insurers at no cost to you.
Will my Manistee County doctors and hospitals be covered?
On a Medicare Advantage plan it depends on that plan’s network, so before you enroll we check whether your doctors and the hospitals you use, such as Munson Healthcare Manistee Hospital, are in network. With a Medicare Supplement (Medigap) plan you can see any doctor who accepts Medicare.
How many people in Manistee County have Medicare Advantage?
CMS reported 5,453 people enrolled in a Medicare Advantage plan out of 8,652 people with Medicare in Manistee County in September 2026, or 63.0%. The Michigan average was 63.6%.
Do you charge Manistee County residents for help?
No, there is no fee. The insurance company pays us when you enroll, and your premium does not go up because you used an agent.
When can I enroll or change plans?
You can enroll during your Initial Enrollment Period around your 65th birthday, during the Annual Enrollment Period from October 15 to December 7, or during a Special Enrollment Period if you have a qualifying event such as moving into or within the county.
