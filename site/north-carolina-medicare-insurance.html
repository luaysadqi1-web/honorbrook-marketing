Insurance help for North Carolina residents

Whether you're turning 65, already on Medicare and unsure if your plan still fits, or under 65 and shopping for health or life coverage, Honorbrook is here to make the choice simpler. We work with North Carolinians one-on-one — listening to your situation, reviewing your doctors and prescriptions, and walking you through the options that actually make sense for you.

Our help is always free. Insurance carriers pay us a commission when we place a policy, not you — and by law your premium is exactly the same whether you use an agent or sign up on your own. There's no fee, no obligation, and no pressure. You get an experienced guide in your corner at no cost.

Common questions

North Carolina Medicare & insurance FAQ

Do you charge North Carolina residents for help?
No — our help is completely free to you. Carriers pay us a commission when we place a policy, and by law your premium is identical whether you use an agent or enroll on your own. You get personalized guidance at no added cost.
Which North Carolina cities do you serve?
All of North Carolina, by phone. We regularly help residents in Charlotte, Raleigh, Durham, Greensboro, Winston-Salem, Asheville, and Wilmington, along with smaller towns and rural counties across the state.
What plans can I get in North Carolina?
Plan availability varies by county. We compare what's actually offered at your address — Medicare Advantage, Medigap, Part D drug plans, ACA health plans, ICHRA, group coverage, and life insurance — and show you the options that fit.
Can you help if I'm new to North Carolina or moving?
Yes. Moving to or within North Carolina can open a Special Enrollment Period that lets you change or pick up coverage outside the usual windows. We'll check what's available at your new address and help you enroll without missing a deadline.
