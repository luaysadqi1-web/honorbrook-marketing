Among Georgia’s 159 counties, Bibb County comes in 13th for residents age 65 and up (26,499). Older adults make up 16.9% of the population here; the state figure is 15.4%. About 9,370 more people are 60 to 64 and are heading toward Medicare; our turning 65 guide explains their first steps. By age, the 65-and-older group splits 59% ages 65 to 74, 31% ages 75 to 84 and 10% age 85 and up (Census 2023). Medicare Advantage enrollment in Bibb County is 62.7% of people with Medicare, higher than the Georgia average of 56.2%. Averages cannot choose a plan for you. Medicare Advantage and Original Medicare with a Medigap plan each suit different people, depending on doctors, medicines and how you prefer to handle costs, and we compare both at your ZIP code.

Who has Medicare in Bibb County

Bibb County is a metro county; the U.S. Department of Agriculture describes it as: counties in metro areas of fewer than 250,000 population. Metro counties usually have more hospitals and more insurers competing for Medicare members, so there can be more plans to compare.

CMS counts 33,881 Medicare beneficiaries in Bibb County (2023). Their average age is 73, about the same as the Georgia average of 73. About 14% are “dual eligible,” meaning they have both Medicare and Medicaid, close to the state figure of 12%.

Among people in Original Medicare in Bibb County, CMS reported about 806 emergency room visits and 240 inpatient hospital stays per 1,000 beneficiaries in 2023, which is higher than the state average for emergency visits (560) and close to it for hospital stays (223). About 9% received home health care. These figures describe how people use care under Original Medicare; they do not show what any plan covers or costs.

Sources: CMS Medicare Geographic Variation, 2023; USDA Rural-Urban Continuum Codes, 2023.

Medicare Advantage and Part D plan availability and provider networks are set county by county, so the plans open to you in Bibb County may differ from those in a neighboring county. We are not based in Bibb County; we help Bibb County residents by phone or video call.

Hospitals located in Bibb County

The Centers for Medicare & Medicaid Services (CMS) lists these hospitals in Bibb County:

  • Atrium Health Navicent the Medical Center — Macon (acute care; government-owned; emergency services; CMS overall rating 2 of 5)
  • Coliseum Medical Centers — Macon (acute care; nonprofit; emergency services; CMS overall rating 3 of 5)
  • Piedmont Macon North Hospital — Macon (acute care; for-profit; emergency services; CMS overall rating 4 of 5)

A hospital being in the county does not mean every plan covers it. Medicare Advantage networks differ by plan, so we check the hospitals and doctors you actually use before you enroll. Ratings are CMS Care Compare overall hospital star ratings; they describe the hospital, not whether a plan covers it. Source: CMS Hospital General Information.

Common questions

Bibb County Medicare FAQ

Can I get help with Medicare in Bibb County by phone?
Yes. We are an independent agency licensed in Georgia, and we help Bibb County residents by phone or video call, so you can compare plans from home. There is no pressure to enroll.
Will my Bibb County doctors and hospitals be covered?
On a Medicare Advantage plan it depends on that plan’s network, so before you enroll we check whether your doctors and the hospitals you use, such as Atrium Health Navicent the Medical Center, Coliseum Medical Centers, Piedmont Macon North Hospital, are in network. With a Medicare Supplement (Medigap) plan you can see any doctor who accepts Medicare.
How many people in Bibb County have Medicare Advantage?
CMS reported 20,851 people enrolled in a Medicare Advantage plan out of 33,244 people with Medicare in Bibb County in September 2026, or 62.7%. The Georgia average was 56.2%.
Do you charge Bibb County residents for help?
No. Our help is free to you. Carriers pay us a commission when we place a policy, and your premium is the same whether you use an agent or enroll on your own.
When can I enroll or change plans?
You can enroll during your Initial Enrollment Period around your 65th birthday, during the Annual Enrollment Period from October 15 to December 7, or during a Special Enrollment Period if you have a qualifying event such as moving into or within the county.
