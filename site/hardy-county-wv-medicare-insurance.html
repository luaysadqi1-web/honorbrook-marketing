Hardy County ranks 36th of 55 West Virginia counties by number of residents age 65 and older, with about 3,424 people in that age group, or 24.0% of residents (statewide: 21.5%). The 1,159 residents now between 60 and 64 will become Medicare-eligible soon, and our turning 65 guide walks through it. By age, the 65-and-older group splits 57% ages 65 to 74, 35% ages 75 to 84 and 9% age 85 and up (Census 2023). CMS data show 44.8% of Hardy County residents with Medicare chose Medicare Advantage, which is lower than the 56.6% average across West Virginia. A county average does not tell you which kind of plan fits you. Whether Medicare Advantage or Original Medicare with a Medigap plan is the better fit depends on your doctors, your prescriptions and how you want to handle costs, so we compare both for your ZIP code.

Who has Medicare in Hardy County

The USDA classifies Hardy County as: urban population of fewer than 5,000, adjacent to a metro area. With fewer hospitals and clinics close by, the right Medicare plan here is often the one whose network includes the providers you can really reach.

CMS counts 3,859 Medicare beneficiaries in Hardy County (2023). Their average age is 72, about the same as the West Virginia average of 72. About 19% are “dual eligible,” meaning they have both Medicare and Medicaid, close to the state figure of 20%.

Among people in Original Medicare in Hardy County, CMS reported about 633 emergency room visits and 206 inpatient hospital stays per 1,000 beneficiaries in 2023, which is close to the state average for emergency visits (684) and lower than it for hospital stays (250). About 7% received home health care. These figures describe how people use care under Original Medicare; they do not show what any plan covers or costs.

Sources: CMS Medicare Geographic Variation, 2023; USDA Rural-Urban Continuum Codes, 2023.

Where you live decides which Medicare Advantage and Part D plans you can join, and Hardy County may have a different set of plans than the counties around it. We are not based in Hardy County; we help Hardy County residents by phone or video call.

Hospitals and doctors near Hardy County

CMS Hospital General Information lists no acute care, critical access or children’s hospital located inside Hardy County. Residents often use hospitals in neighboring counties, which matters for Medicare Advantage because a plan’s network may include or leave out the hospital you use. We check your hospitals and doctors before you enroll.

Where people live in Hardy County

Census Bureau 2023 estimates put the biggest communities in Hardy County at Moorefield (2,487), Wardensville (262), counting only the part of each community inside the county.

Common questions

Hardy County Medicare FAQ

Can I get help with Medicare in Hardy County by phone?
Yes. We are an independent agency licensed in West Virginia, and we help Hardy County residents by phone or video call, so you can compare plans from home. There is no pressure to enroll.
Will my Hardy County doctors and hospitals be covered?
On a Medicare Advantage plan it depends on that plan’s network, so before you enroll we check whether your doctors and the hospitals you use are in network, including hospitals in neighboring counties. With a Medicare Supplement (Medigap) plan you can see any doctor who accepts Medicare.
How many people in Hardy County have Medicare Advantage?
CMS reported 1,761 people enrolled in a Medicare Advantage plan out of 3,932 people with Medicare in Hardy County in September 2026, or 44.8%. The West Virginia average was 56.6%.
Do you charge Hardy County residents for help?
No, there is no fee. The insurance company pays us when you enroll, and your premium does not go up because you used an agent.
When can I enroll or change plans?
You can enroll during your Initial Enrollment Period around your 65th birthday, during the Annual Enrollment Period from October 15 to December 7, or during a Special Enrollment Period if you have a qualifying event such as moving into or within the county.
