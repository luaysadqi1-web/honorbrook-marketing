Among West Virginia’s 55 counties, Jefferson County comes in 11th for residents age 65 and up (10,779). Older adults make up 18.0% of the population here; the state figure is 21.5%. Another 4,201 residents are age 60 to 64 and will reach Medicare eligibility within about five years (see our turning 65 guide). Census 2023 estimates show 61% of the 65-plus population is under 75, 32% is 75 to 84 and 8% is 85 or older. Medicare Advantage enrollment in Jefferson County is 41.2% of people with Medicare, lower than the West Virginia average of 56.6%. What most people do in a county is less important than what fits you. We weigh Medicare Advantage against Original Medicare with Medigap using your doctors, your drugs and your budget.

Who has Medicare in Jefferson County

The U.S. Department of Agriculture classifies Jefferson County as a metro county (counties in metro areas of 1 million population or more). Counties like this tend to have a wider choice of hospitals and Medicare plans than rural ones, though networks still differ plan by plan.

CMS counts 12,696 Medicare beneficiaries in Jefferson County (2023). Their average age is 73, older than the West Virginia average of 72. About 11% are “dual eligible,” meaning they have both Medicare and Medicaid, lower than the state figure of 20%.

Among people in Original Medicare in Jefferson County, CMS reported about 640 emergency room visits and 189 inpatient hospital stays per 1,000 beneficiaries in 2023, which is close to the state average for emergency visits (684) and lower than it for hospital stays (250). About 7% received home health care. These figures describe how people use care under Original Medicare; they do not show what any plan covers or costs.

Sources: CMS Medicare Geographic Variation, 2023; USDA Rural-Urban Continuum Codes, 2023.

Common questions

Jefferson County Medicare FAQ

Can I get help with Medicare in Jefferson County by phone?
Yes. You do not need to travel: we are licensed in West Virginia and help Jefferson County residents by phone or video, comparing plans from many insurers at no cost to you.
Will my Jefferson County doctors and hospitals be covered?
On a Medicare Advantage plan it depends on that plan’s network, so before you enroll we check whether your doctors and the hospitals you use, such as Jefferson Medical Center, are in network. With a Medicare Supplement (Medigap) plan you can see any doctor who accepts Medicare.
How many people in Jefferson County have Medicare Advantage?
CMS reported 5,436 people enrolled in a Medicare Advantage plan out of 13,196 people with Medicare in Jefferson County in September 2026, or 41.2%. The West Virginia average was 56.6%.
Do you charge Jefferson County residents for help?
No, there is no fee. The insurance company pays us when you enroll, and your premium does not go up because you used an agent.
When can I enroll or change plans?
You can enroll during your Initial Enrollment Period around your 65th birthday, during the Annual Enrollment Period from October 15 to December 7, or during a Special Enrollment Period if you have a qualifying event such as moving into or within the county.
