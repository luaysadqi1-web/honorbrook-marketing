West Virginia Medicare & insurance FAQ

Do you charge West Virginia residents for help?
No — our help is completely free. Carriers pay us a commission when we place a policy, and by law your premium is identical whether you use an agent or enroll on your own. You get our time, comparisons, and year-round support at no cost.
Which West Virginia cities do you serve?
All of West Virginia, handled by phone — including Charleston, Huntington, Morgantown, Parkersburg, Wheeling, and Beckley, plus every smaller town and county in between. There's no office visit required.
What plans can I get in West Virginia?
Plan availability varies by county in West Virginia. We compare exactly what's offered at your address — Medicare Advantage, Medigap, Part D drug plans, ACA plans for those under 65, ICHRA, group coverage, and life insurance.
Can you help if I'm new to West Virginia or moving?
Yes. Moving into, out of, or within West Virginia can open a Special Enrollment Period, which lets you change plans outside the usual windows. We'll confirm your dates and find the best coverage available at your new address.
