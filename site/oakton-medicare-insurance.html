Oakton Medicare FAQ

Can I meet with an Oakton Medicare agent in person?
Yes. By appointment — our office is nearby in Vienna, just minutes from Oakton, so it's easy to sit down together. If you'd rather, we can come to you or handle everything by phone, whichever you prefer.
Will my Oakton-area doctors and hospitals be covered?
On a Medicare Advantage plan it depends on that plan's provider network, which can change each year. Before you enroll, we verify your specific doctors and hospitals — including the Inova system and Inova Fairfax Medical Campus that many Oakton residents use — are in network. With a Medicare Supplement (Medigap) plan, you can see any doctor that accepts Medicare.
Do you charge Oakton residents for help?
No, our help is completely free. Carriers pay us a commission when we place a policy, and by law your premium is the same whether you use an agent or enroll on your own.
When can I enroll or change plans?
During your Initial Enrollment Period around your 65th birthday, during the Annual Enrollment Period (Oct 15 – Dec 7), or during a Special Enrollment Period triggered by a qualifying event such as moving to or within Oakton.
