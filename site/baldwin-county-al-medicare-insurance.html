Medicare in Baldwin County

Baldwin County's county seat is Bay Minette, on Alabama's Gulf Coast side of the state. The U.S. Census Bureau's 2023 population estimates put the number of Baldwin County residents age 65 and older at 55,923, so many of your neighbors are making the same Medicare decisions. Thomas Hospital in Fairhope, operated by Infirmary Health, is a major community hospital in the county.

Medicare Advantage and Part D plan availability and provider networks are set county by county, and they can change each year. We check what is offered where you live, then confirm your doctors, hospitals and prescriptions before you enroll. Baldwin County ranks 4th of 67 Alabama counties by number of residents age 65 and older, with about 55,923 people in that age group, or 22.1% of residents (statewide: 18.3%). Another 18,982 residents are age 60 to 64 and will reach Medicare eligibility within about five years (see our turning 65 guide). Among residents 65 and older, 59% are 65 to 74, 32% are 75 to 84 and 9% are 85 or older (Census 2023 estimates). About 57.9% of people with Medicare in Baldwin County are enrolled in a Medicare Advantage plan. That is close to the Alabama average of 61.5%. A county average does not tell you which kind of plan fits you. Whether Medicare Advantage or Original Medicare with a Medigap plan is the better fit depends on your doctors, your prescriptions and how you want to handle costs, so we compare both for your ZIP code.

Free help available in Baldwin County

You do not have to rely on us alone. These are official sources of free, unbiased help:

  • Alabama SHIP offers free Medicare counseling: 1-800-243-5463.
  • Area Agency on Aging: AgeSouth, the Area Agency on Aging at the South Alabama Regional Planning Commission, serves Mobile, Baldwin and Escambia counties. Call (251) 706-4680 or visit agingsouthalabama.org. AgeSouth also runs a Medicare assistance program through SHIP.
  • 1-800-MEDICARE (1-800-633-4227) is the federal Medicare helpline.

Common questions

Baldwin County Medicare FAQ

Can I get help with Medicare in Baldwin County by phone?
Yes. We are based in Tysons, Virginia, and help Baldwin County residents by phone or video call, so you never need to travel. We are licensed in Alabama and can review your options, answer questions and help you enroll from home.
Will my Baldwin County doctors and Thomas Hospital be covered?
With a Medicare Advantage plan it depends on that plan's network, so before you enroll we check that your doctors and the hospitals you use are included. A Medicare Supplement (Medigap) plan lets you see any doctor who accepts Medicare.
Do you charge Baldwin County residents for help?
No. Carriers pay us a commission when we place a policy, and by law your premium is the same whether you use an agent or enroll on your own.
When can I enroll or change plans?
You can enroll during your Initial Enrollment Period around your 65th birthday, during the Annual Enrollment Period from October 15 to December 7, or during a Special Enrollment Period if you have a qualifying event, such as moving to or within the county.
