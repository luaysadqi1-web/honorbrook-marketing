Medicare in Baldwin County

Baldwin County's county seat is Bay Minette, on Alabama's Gulf Coast side of the state. The U.S. Census Bureau's 2023 population estimates put the number of Baldwin County residents age 65 and older at 55,923, so many of your neighbors are making the same Medicare decisions. Thomas Hospital in Fairhope, operated by Infirmary Health, is a major community hospital in the county.

Medicare Advantage and Part D plan availability and provider networks are set county by county, and they can change each year. We check what is offered where you live, then confirm your doctors, hospitals and prescriptions before you enroll. With roughly 55,923 residents age 65 and older, Baldwin County is 4th among 67 Alabama counties for older residents. That group is 22.1% of its population, compared with 18.3% across Alabama. About 18,982 more people are 60 to 64 and are heading toward Medicare; our turning 65 guide explains their first steps. Among residents 65 and older, 59% are 65 to 74, 32% are 75 to 84 and 9% are 85 or older (Census 2023 estimates). CMS data show 57.9% of Baldwin County residents with Medicare chose Medicare Advantage, which is close to the 61.5% average across Alabama. What most people do in a county is less important than what fits you. We weigh Medicare Advantage against Original Medicare with Medigap using your doctors, your drugs and your budget.

Who has Medicare in Baldwin County

The U.S. Department of Agriculture classifies Baldwin County as a metro county (counties in metro areas of fewer than 250,000 population). Counties like this tend to have a wider choice of hospitals and Medicare plans than rural ones, though networks still differ plan by plan.

CMS counts 64,196 Medicare beneficiaries in Baldwin County (2023). Their average age is 74, older than the Alabama average of 72. About 6% are “dual eligible,” meaning they have both Medicare and Medicaid, lower than the state figure of 14%.

Among people in Original Medicare in Baldwin County, CMS reported about 624 emergency room visits and 219 inpatient hospital stays per 1,000 beneficiaries in 2023, which is close to the state average for emergency visits (600) and close to it for hospital stays (247). About 11% received home health care. These figures describe how people use care under Original Medicare; they do not show what any plan covers or costs.

Sources: CMS Medicare Geographic Variation, 2023; USDA Rural-Urban Continuum Codes, 2023.

Common questions

Baldwin County Medicare FAQ

Can I get help with Medicare in Baldwin County by phone?
Yes. We are based in Tysons, Virginia, and help Baldwin County residents by phone or video call, so you never need to travel. We are licensed in Alabama and can review your options, answer questions and help you enroll from home.
Will my Baldwin County doctors and Thomas Hospital be covered?
With a Medicare Advantage plan it depends on that plan's network, so before you enroll we check that your doctors and the hospitals you use are included. A Medicare Supplement (Medigap) plan lets you see any doctor who accepts Medicare.
Do you charge Baldwin County residents for help?
No. Carriers pay us a commission when we place a policy, and by law your premium is the same whether you use an agent or enroll on your own.
When can I enroll or change plans?
You can enroll during your Initial Enrollment Period around your 65th birthday, during the Annual Enrollment Period from October 15 to December 7, or during a Special Enrollment Period if you have a qualifying event, such as moving to or within the county.
