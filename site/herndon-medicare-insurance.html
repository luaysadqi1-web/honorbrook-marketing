Knowing Herndon's doctors and hospitals

The Town of Herndon sits in Fairfax County near Washington Dulles International Airport, with its walkable historic downtown and the Washington & Old Dominion (W&OD) Trail running right through town — and Reston is just next door. That proximity matters when it comes to your healthcare, because the Herndon area is served by the Inova and HCA (Reston Hospital Center) systems, and the hospitals and physicians you rely on are often the ones just minutes away.

With Medicare Advantage, the local provider network is everything. A plan that looks great on paper isn't the right plan if your Herndon-area doctor or your preferred hospital isn't in it. That's why, before you enroll, we verify that your specific doctors and the hospitals you'd actually use — whether that's Reston Hospital Center or a nearby Inova facility — are covered under any plan we recommend. Common questions

Herndon Medicare FAQ

Can I meet with a Herndon Medicare agent in person?
Yes — by appointment. Our office is nearby in Vienna, so we can meet in person, come to you, or handle everything by phone, whichever is easiest for you.
Will my Herndon-area doctors and hospitals be covered?
On a Medicare Advantage plan it depends on the plan's network, so we verify your specific providers — such as Reston Hospital Center or a nearby Inova facility — before you enroll. A Medigap plan lets you see any doctor that accepts Medicare.
Do you charge Herndon residents for help?
No, it's completely free. The carriers pay us when we place a policy, and your premium is exactly the same whether you use an agent or enroll on your own.
When can I enroll or change plans?
During your Initial Enrollment Period at 65, during the Annual Enrollment Period (Oct 15 – Dec 7), or during a Special Enrollment Period triggered by a qualifying event such as moving to or within Herndon.
