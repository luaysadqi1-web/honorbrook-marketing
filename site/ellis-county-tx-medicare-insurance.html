With roughly 29,218 residents age 65 and older, Ellis County is 24th among 254 Texas counties for older residents. That group is 13.1% of its population, compared with 13.7% across Texas. Another 12,402 residents are age 60 to 64 and will reach Medicare eligibility within about five years (see our turning 65 guide). Census 2023 estimates show 62% of the 65-plus population is under 75, 30% is 75 to 84 and 8% is 85 or older. CMS data show 53.4% of Ellis County residents with Medicare chose Medicare Advantage, which is close to the 54.9% average across Texas. What most people do in a county is less important than what fits you. We weigh Medicare Advantage against Original Medicare with Medigap using your doctors, your drugs and your budget.

Who has Medicare in Ellis County

Ellis County sits in a metro area, which the USDA defines as counties in metro areas of 1 million population or more. That usually means more doctors, more hospitals and more plan choices in the area.

CMS counts 35,350 Medicare beneficiaries in Ellis County (2023). Their average age is 73, about the same as the Texas average of 73. About 9% are “dual eligible,” meaning they have both Medicare and Medicaid, close to the state figure of 11%.

Among people in Original Medicare in Ellis County, CMS reported about 647 emergency room visits and 239 inpatient hospital stays per 1,000 beneficiaries in 2023, which is close to the state average for emergency visits (580) and close to it for hospital stays (226). About 10% received home health care. These figures describe how people use care under Original Medicare; they do not show what any plan covers or costs.

Sources: CMS Medicare Geographic Variation, 2023; USDA Rural-Urban Continuum Codes, 2023.

Medicare Advantage and Part D plan availability and provider networks are set county by county, so the plans open to you in Ellis County may differ from those in a neighboring county. We are not based in Ellis County; we help Ellis County residents by phone or video call.

Network rules, not geography, decide whether a plan covers a hospital. We look up the hospitals and doctors you use and check them against each plan before you enroll. Ratings are CMS Care Compare overall hospital star ratings; they describe the hospital, not whether a plan covers it. Source: CMS Hospital General Information.

Free Medicare counseling for Ellis County

  • Texas’s SHIP program, HICAP, gives free Medicare counseling that is not tied to any insurer: 1-800-252-9240.
  • The Eldercare Locator, 1-800-677-1116, finds the Area Agency on Aging that serves your address (eldercare.acl.gov).
  • 1-800-MEDICARE (1-800-633-4227) is the federal Medicare helpline.

These services are worth using. Where we add to them is comparing specific plans and helping you enroll and stay on track year after year.

Common questions

Ellis County Medicare FAQ

Can I get help with Medicare in Ellis County by phone?
Yes. You do not need to travel: we are licensed in Texas and help Ellis County residents by phone or video, comparing plans from many insurers at no cost to you.
Will my Ellis County doctors and hospitals be covered?
On a Medicare Advantage plan it depends on that plan’s network, so before you enroll we check whether your doctors and the hospitals you use, such as Baylor Scott & White Medical Center- Waxahachie, Ennis Regional Medical Center, Methodist Midlothian Medical Center, are in network. With a Medicare Supplement (Medigap) plan you can see any doctor who accepts Medicare.
How many people in Ellis County have Medicare Advantage?
CMS reported 20,514 people enrolled in a Medicare Advantage plan out of 38,425 people with Medicare in Ellis County in September 2026, or 53.4%. The Texas average was 54.9%.
Do you charge Ellis County residents for help?
No. Our help is free to you. Carriers pay us a commission when we place a policy, and your premium is the same whether you use an agent or enroll on your own.
When can I enroll or change plans?
You can enroll during your Initial Enrollment Period around your 65th birthday, during the Annual Enrollment Period from October 15 to December 7, or during a Special Enrollment Period if you have a qualifying event such as moving into or within the county.
