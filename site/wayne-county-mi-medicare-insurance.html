Medicare in Wayne County

Wayne County includes Detroit, Dearborn and many surrounding communities. According to the U.S. Census Bureau, 2023 population estimates, about 299,646 Wayne County residents are age 65 or older, so choosing the right Medicare coverage is a decision many of your neighbors are making too. Whether you are turning 65, retiring, or reviewing your current plan, we can walk through your choices with you on a call at a time that suits you.

Major hospitals and health systems serving the county include Henry Ford Hospital and DMC Harper University Hospital in Detroit and Corewell Health Dearborn Hospital. Medicare Advantage and Part D plan availability and provider networks are set county by county, and they can change every year. Original Medicare (Parts A and B) is the same nationwide, but the private plans you can add to it are not. That is why we check the plans offered where you live and confirm that your doctors and pharmacy are included before you enroll.

Wayne County ranks 1st of 83 Michigan counties by number of residents age 65 and older, with about 299,646 people in that age group, or 17.1% of residents (statewide: 19.2%). Another 110,755 residents are age 60 to 64 and will reach Medicare eligibility within about five years (see our turning 65 guide). By age, the 65-and-older group splits 60% ages 65 to 74, 30% ages 75 to 84 and 10% age 85 and up (Census 2023). CMS data show 64.9% of Wayne County residents with Medicare chose Medicare Advantage, which is close to the 63.6% average across Michigan. Averages cannot choose a plan for you. Medicare Advantage and Original Medicare with a Medigap plan each suit different people, depending on doctors, medicines and how you prefer to handle costs, and we compare both at your ZIP code.

Who has Medicare in Wayne County

Wayne County sits in a metro area, which the USDA defines as counties in metro areas of 1 million population or more. That usually means more doctors, more hospitals and more plan choices in the area.

CMS counts 355,133 Medicare beneficiaries in Wayne County (2023). Their average age is 71, about the same as the Michigan average of 72. About 26% are “dual eligible,” meaning they have both Medicare and Medicaid, higher than the state figure of 20%. Where many people have both, special plans for dual-eligible members can matter; see our Medicaid and dual eligible guide, since availability varies by plan and area.

Among people in Original Medicare in Wayne County, CMS reported about 733 emergency room visits and 306 inpatient hospital stays per 1,000 beneficiaries in 2023, which is higher than the state average for emergency visits (645) and higher than it for hospital stays (245). About 11% received home health care. These figures describe how people use care under Original Medicare; they do not show what any plan covers or costs.

Sources: CMS Medicare Geographic Variation, 2023; USDA Rural-Urban Continuum Codes, 2023.

Common questions

Wayne County Medicare FAQ

Can I get help with Medicare in Wayne County by phone?
Yes. We are an independent agency licensed in Michigan, and we help Wayne County residents by phone or video call. We review your doctors, prescriptions and budget, then walk you through the options available at your ZIP code.
Will my Wayne County doctors and Henry Ford, DMC Harper or Corewell Health Dearborn be covered?
On a Medicare Advantage plan it depends on that plan's network, so before you enroll we check whether your specific doctors and the hospitals you use are in network. Original Medicare with a Medicare Supplement (Medigap) plan lets you see any provider that accepts Medicare.
Do you charge Wayne County residents for help?
No. Our help is free to you. Carriers compensate us when you enroll, and that does not change your premium.
When can I enroll or change plans?
You can enroll during your Initial Enrollment Period around your 65th birthday, during the Annual Enrollment Period from October 15 to December 7, or during a Special Enrollment Period if you have a qualifying event. We can tell you which applies to you.
