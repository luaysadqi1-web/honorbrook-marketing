Insurance help for Texas residents

If you live in Texas and you're turning 65, already on Medicare, or shopping for coverage before you qualify, Honorbrook is here to make the decision simpler. We're an independent agency — that means we work for you, not for any single insurance company. Our job is to lay out your real options in plain English, answer your questions honestly, and help you pick coverage that fits your doctors, your prescriptions, and your budget.

Our help is always free. Carriers pay us a commission when we place a policy, and by law your premium is exactly the same whether you work with us or sign up on your own. There's no fee, no pressure, and no obligation — just a knowledgeable person on the phone who will take the time to get it right.

Common questions

Texas Medicare & insurance FAQ

Do you charge Texas residents for help?
No — our help is completely free. Carriers pay us a commission when we place a policy, and by law your premium is identical whether you use an agent or enroll on your own. You get the comparison, guidance, and ongoing service at no cost.
Which Texas cities do you serve?
All of Texas, handled by phone. We regularly help residents in Houston, Dallas, Austin, San Antonio, Fort Worth, El Paso, Arlington, Plano, and every community in between — no office visit needed.
What plans can I get in Texas?
Plan availability varies by county in Texas. We compare what's actually offered at your exact address — Medicare Advantage, Medicare Supplement (Medigap), Part D drug plans, ACA health plans, ICHRA, group coverage, and life insurance.
Can you help if I'm new to Texas or moving?
Yes. Moving to or within Texas can open a Special Enrollment Period that lets you change your Medicare or health coverage outside the normal windows. We'll check what's available at your new address and help you switch smoothly.
