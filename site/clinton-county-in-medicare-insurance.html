Among Indiana’s 92 counties, Clinton County comes in 53rd for residents age 65 and up (5,508). Older adults make up 16.8% of the population here; the state figure is 17.2%. The 2,089 residents now between 60 and 64 will become Medicare-eligible soon, and our turning 65 guide walks through it. Among residents 65 and older, 59% are 65 to 74, 28% are 75 to 84 and 13% are 85 or older (Census 2023 estimates). CMS data show 57.0% of Clinton County residents with Medicare chose Medicare Advantage, which is higher than the 51.1% average across Indiana. A county average does not tell you which kind of plan fits you. Whether Medicare Advantage or Original Medicare with a Medigap plan is the better fit depends on your doctors, your prescriptions and how you want to handle costs, so we compare both for your ZIP code.

Who has Medicare in Clinton County

The USDA classifies Clinton County as: urban population of 5,000 to 20,000, adjacent to a metro area. In counties of this type, people often travel to a larger neighboring county for specialists, so we check whether your plan’s network reaches there.

CMS counts 6,927 Medicare beneficiaries in Clinton County (2023). Their average age is 74, older than the Indiana average of 72. About 16% are “dual eligible,” meaning they have both Medicare and Medicaid, close to the state figure of 16%.

Among people in Original Medicare in Clinton County, CMS reported about 690 emergency room visits and 212 inpatient hospital stays per 1,000 beneficiaries in 2023, which is close to the state average for emergency visits (619) and close to it for hospital stays (235). About 6% received home health care. These figures describe how people use care under Original Medicare; they do not show what any plan covers or costs.

Sources: CMS Medicare Geographic Variation, 2023; USDA Rural-Urban Continuum Codes, 2023.

Common questions

Clinton County Medicare FAQ

Can I get help with Medicare in Clinton County by phone?
Yes. Phone and video appointments are how we serve Clinton County. We hold a Indiana license, compare plans from many carriers and walk you through them from home, with no pressure to enroll.
Will my Clinton County doctors and hospitals be covered?
On a Medicare Advantage plan it depends on that plan’s network, so before you enroll we check whether your doctors and the hospitals you use, such as Indiana University Health Frankfort, are in network. With a Medicare Supplement (Medigap) plan you can see any doctor who accepts Medicare.
How many people in Clinton County have Medicare Advantage?
CMS reported 3,948 people enrolled in a Medicare Advantage plan out of 6,926 people with Medicare in Clinton County in September 2026, or 57.0%. The Indiana average was 51.1%.
Do you charge Clinton County residents for help?
No, there is no fee. The insurance company pays us when you enroll, and your premium does not go up because you used an agent.
When can I enroll or change plans?
You can enroll during your Initial Enrollment Period around your 65th birthday, during the Annual Enrollment Period from October 15 to December 7, or during a Special Enrollment Period if you have a qualifying event such as moving into or within the county.
