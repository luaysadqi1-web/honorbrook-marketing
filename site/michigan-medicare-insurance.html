Insurance help for Michigan residents

If you live in Michigan and you're sorting through Medicare, health, or life insurance, you don't have to do it alone — and you don't have to pay for help. Honorbrook is an independent agency, which means we work for you, not for any one insurance company. Whether you're turning 65, already on Medicare and wondering if there's a better fit, shopping for coverage under 65, or thinking about protecting your family with life insurance, we'll walk you through the options at your own pace.

Our help is always free. Carriers pay us a commission when we place a policy, and by law your premium is identical whether you use an agent or enroll on your own. So you get our time, our comparison, and our follow-up support without ever paying us a dime — and with no pressure to decide before you're ready.

Coverage we offer in Michigan

  • Medicare Advantage — all-in-one Part C plans, many with $0 premiums plus extras.
  • Medicare Supplement (Medigap) — predictable costs and freedom to see any doctor who accepts Medicare.
  • Part D drug plans — we find the lowest true annual cost for your medication list.
  • final expense — affordable coverage for funeral and end-of-life costs.
  • life insurance — term and whole life to protect the people who depend on you.
  • ICHRA — individual coverage HRA options for employers and their teams.
  • ACA plans (under 65) — Marketplace coverage, including subsidy help if you qualify.
  • group / SHOP coverage — small-business health plans for Michigan employers.
Plan availability varies by county in Michigan — what's offered in Wayne County isn't always the same as Kent, Oakland, or Ingham. We check exactly what's available at your address before you decide, so you never waste time on a plan you can't actually enroll in.

Common questions

Michigan Medicare & insurance FAQ

Do you charge Michigan residents for help?
No — our help is completely free. Carriers pay us a commission when we place a policy, and by law your premium is identical whether you use an agent or enroll on your own. You get our comparison, guidance, and year-round support at no cost.
Which Michigan cities do you serve?
All of Michigan, handled by phone. We regularly help residents in Detroit, Grand Rapids, Ann Arbor, Lansing, Sterling Heights, Warren, and Flint, along with every other community across the state — no office visit needed.
What plans can I get in Michigan?
Availability varies by county in Michigan. We compare what's actually offered at your exact address — Medicare Advantage, Medigap, Part D, ACA, ICHRA, group coverage, and life — so you only see plans you can really enroll in.
Can you help if I'm new to Michigan or moving?
Yes. A move can open a Special Enrollment Period that lets you choose or change coverage outside the usual windows. If you're relocating to or within Michigan, call us and we'll check what's available at your new address and help you switch smoothly.
