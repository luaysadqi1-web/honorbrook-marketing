Laurens County ranks 41st of 159 Georgia counties by number of residents age 65 and older, with about 9,242 people in that age group, or 18.5% of residents (statewide: 15.4%). Another 3,116 residents are age 60 to 64 and will reach Medicare eligibility within about five years (see our turning 65 guide). Census 2023 estimates show 57% of the 65-plus population is under 75, 32% is 75 to 84 and 11% is 85 or older. Medicare Advantage enrollment in Laurens County is 59.2% of people with Medicare, close to the Georgia average of 56.2%. A county average does not tell you which kind of plan fits you. Whether Medicare Advantage or Original Medicare with a Medigap plan is the better fit depends on your doctors, your prescriptions and how you want to handle costs, so we compare both for your ZIP code.

Who has Medicare in Laurens County

Laurens County is a non-metro county with towns and small cities (USDA: urban population of 20,000 or more, adjacent to a metro area). Care is often split between local providers and a bigger hospital elsewhere, which is worth testing against each plan’s network.

CMS counts 11,661 Medicare beneficiaries in Laurens County (2023). Their average age is 72, about the same as the Georgia average of 73. About 16% are “dual eligible,” meaning they have both Medicare and Medicaid, higher than the state figure of 12%. Where many people have both, special plans for dual-eligible members can matter; see our Medicaid and dual eligible guide, since availability varies by plan and area.

Among people in Original Medicare in Laurens County, CMS reported about 608 emergency room visits and 251 inpatient hospital stays per 1,000 beneficiaries in 2023, which is close to the state average for emergency visits (560) and higher than it for hospital stays (223). About 7% received home health care. These figures describe how people use care under Original Medicare; they do not show what any plan covers or costs.

Sources: CMS Medicare Geographic Variation, 2023; USDA Rural-Urban Continuum Codes, 2023.

Insurers choose which counties to serve each year, which means the Medicare Advantage and Part D plans you can pick in Laurens County can be different from the ones offered next door. We are not based in Laurens County; we help Laurens County residents by phone or video call.

Communities and ZIP codes in Laurens County

The largest communities in Laurens County by 2023 Census population estimates are Dublin (16,008), East Dublin (2,499), Dexter (663), Dudley (595), Cadwell (419), Rentz (314). Each figure counts only the people living inside the county.

We look up plans by ZIP code. In Laurens County, that means areas such as 30454, 31009, 31019, 31021, 31022, 31027, 31065, 31075, based on Census ZIP Code Tabulation Areas that lie mostly in the county.

Common questions

Laurens County Medicare FAQ

Can I get help with Medicare in Laurens County by phone?
Yes. We are an independent agency licensed in Georgia, and we help Laurens County residents by phone or video call, so you can compare plans from home. There is no pressure to enroll.
Will my Laurens County doctors and hospitals be covered?
On a Medicare Advantage plan it depends on that plan’s network, so before you enroll we check whether your doctors and the hospitals you use, such as Fairview Park Hospital, are in network. With a Medicare Supplement (Medigap) plan you can see any doctor who accepts Medicare.
How many people in Laurens County have Medicare Advantage?
CMS reported 6,862 people enrolled in a Medicare Advantage plan out of 11,601 people with Medicare in Laurens County in September 2026, or 59.2%. The Georgia average was 56.2%.
Do you charge Laurens County residents for help?
No. You pay nothing to work with us. Carriers compensate us, and the price of your plan is the same as if you enrolled directly.
When can I enroll or change plans?
You can enroll during your Initial Enrollment Period around your 65th birthday, during the Annual Enrollment Period from October 15 to December 7, or during a Special Enrollment Period if you have a qualifying event such as moving into or within the county.
