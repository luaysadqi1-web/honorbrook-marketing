Why your county matters more than you'd think

Medicare Advantage and Part D plans are approved, priced, and networked at the county level. That means the set of plans available in Fairfax County isn't necessarily the set available in Arlington County or the City of Alexandria — and premiums for what looks like the same plan can differ across that line.

In a region as tightly packed as ours, that produces some genuinely counterintuitive results. Someone in Vienna and someone in Reston are twenty minutes apart and can be looking at different plan menus. A couple who moves from Falls Church to Fairfax may find their plan works differently. It's not intuitive, and it's the most common thing people are surprised by when we run their comparison.

Checking your doctors is the step people skip. Northern Virginia families are often deeply attached to specific physicians and health systems, and network participation varies by plan and changes year to year. Before you enroll in anything, have your doctors' names in front of you. We check every one against every plan we show you — it takes a few extra minutes and prevents the single most painful surprise in Medicare.

What we help Northern Virginia families with

Medicare is what most people call us about, but it's not the whole picture — and in a region with this many federal retirees, contractors, self-employed consultants, and multigenerational households, the questions vary a lot:

  • Turning 65 — comparing Medicare Advantage against a Medicare Supplement with a Part D plan, and sorting out timing around your Initial Enrollment Period.
  • Retiring later than 65 — common here, and the coordination between employer coverage and Medicare has real penalties if it's done wrong.
  • Annual plan reviews — checking each fall during the Annual Enrollment Period whether your plan is still the right one.
  • Coverage before 65 — ACA marketplace plans for early retirees, the self-employed, and families between jobs, plus Medicaid and dual-eligible guidance.
  • Life insurance — term for families with a mortgage and kids at home, whole life and guaranteed universal life for estate planning, and final expense coverage for seniors.
  • Small business owners — ICHRA and group and SHOP coverage for the region's many small firms and consultancies.

Splitting time between states? That changes the answer.

A meaningful share of the people we work with in Northern Virginia spend real time somewhere else — a place at the beach, months with family in another state, or a plan to relocate in retirement. This isn't a footnote; it should drive the decision.

Medicare Advantage plans are built around local provider networks, and they serve you best in your home county. Medicare Supplement plans let you see any provider nationwide who accepts Medicare, which is why they often suit people who travel or split residences. We're licensed in 11 states — Virginia, Maryland, West Virginia, North Carolina, South Carolina, Georgia, Alabama, Louisiana, Texas, Michigan, and Indiana — so we can look at both ends of your situation rather than only this one.

Common questions

Northern Virginia Medicare FAQ

Do Medicare plans differ between Northern Virginia counties?
Yes. Medicare Advantage and Part D availability, pricing, and networks are set at the county level, so plans in Fairfax County can differ from Arlington County or the City of Alexandria. Someone in Vienna and someone in Reston may see different options despite living twenty minutes apart — which is why a county-specific comparison matters.
Can I meet with an agent in person?
Yes. Our office is at 8609 Westwood Center Drive, Suite 110 in Tysons, and we meet clients throughout Northern Virginia. Many people prefer phone or video, which works equally well — but if you'd rather sit down and go through the paperwork together, that's available.
Does working with a local broker cost anything?
No. Carriers pay agent commissions, and by law your premium is exactly the same whether you enroll through a broker, directly with the carrier, or on Medicare.gov. It costs you nothing and gets you someone who compares across carriers and answers the phone after you enroll.
What if I split time between Virginia and another state?
Common here, and it should drive your decision. Medicare Advantage plans are built around local networks and serve you best in your home county; Medicare Supplement plans let you see any provider nationwide who accepts Medicare. If you spend months elsewhere, that difference matters. We're licensed in 11 states and can coordinate across them.
