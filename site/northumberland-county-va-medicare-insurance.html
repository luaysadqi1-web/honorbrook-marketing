Northumberland County ranks 77th of 133 Virginia counties by number of residents age 65 and older, with about 4,646 people in that age group, or 37.5% of residents (statewide: 17.2%). Another 1,358 residents are age 60 to 64 and will reach Medicare eligibility within about five years (see our turning 65 guide). Census 2023 estimates show 55% of the 65-plus population is under 75, 36% is 75 to 84 and 9% is 85 or older. About 32.8% of people with Medicare in Northumberland County are enrolled in a Medicare Advantage plan. That is lower than the Virginia average of 39.6%. A county average does not tell you which kind of plan fits you. Whether Medicare Advantage or Original Medicare with a Medigap plan is the better fit depends on your doctors, your prescriptions and how you want to handle costs, so we compare both for your ZIP code.

Who has Medicare in Northumberland County

Northumberland County is a rural county (USDA: urban population of fewer than 5,000, not adjacent to a metro area). Plan choices can be narrower than in a city, and we focus on networks that include the doctors and hospital you actually use.

CMS counts 5,143 Medicare beneficiaries in Northumberland County (2023). Their average age is 75, about the same as the Virginia average of 74. About 6% are “dual eligible,” meaning they have both Medicare and Medicaid, lower than the state figure of 9%.

Among people in Original Medicare in Northumberland County, CMS reported about 583 emergency room visits and 204 inpatient hospital stays per 1,000 beneficiaries in 2023, which is close to the state average for emergency visits (589) and close to it for hospital stays (206). About 8% received home health care. These figures describe how people use care under Original Medicare; they do not show what any plan covers or costs.

Sources: CMS Medicare Geographic Variation, 2023; USDA Rural-Urban Continuum Codes, 2023.

Where you live decides which Medicare Advantage and Part D plans you can join, and Northumberland County may have a different set of plans than the counties around it. We are not based in Northumberland County; we help Northumberland County residents by phone or video call.

Hospitals and doctors near Northumberland County

CMS Hospital General Information lists no acute care, critical access or children’s hospital located inside Northumberland County. Residents often use hospitals in neighboring counties, which matters for Medicare Advantage because a plan’s network may include or leave out the hospital you use. Common questions

Northumberland County Medicare FAQ

Can I get help with Medicare in Northumberland County by phone?
Yes. You do not need to travel: we are licensed in Virginia and help Northumberland County residents by phone or video, comparing plans from many insurers at no cost to you.
Will my Northumberland County doctors and hospitals be covered?
On a Medicare Advantage plan it depends on that plan’s network, so before you enroll we check whether your doctors and the hospitals you use are in network, including hospitals in neighboring counties. With a Medicare Supplement (Medigap) plan you can see any doctor who accepts Medicare.
How many people in Northumberland County have Medicare Advantage?
CMS reported 1,701 people enrolled in a Medicare Advantage plan out of 5,183 people with Medicare in Northumberland County in September 2026, or 32.8%. The Virginia average was 39.6%.
Do you charge Northumberland County residents for help?
No, there is no fee. The insurance company pays us when you enroll, and your premium does not go up because you used an agent.
When can I enroll or change plans?
You can enroll during your Initial Enrollment Period around your 65th birthday, during the Annual Enrollment Period from October 15 to December 7, or during a Special Enrollment Period if you have a qualifying event such as moving into or within the county.
