City of Salem ranks 70th of 133 Virginia counties by number of residents age 65 and older, with about 5,170 people in that age group, or 20.2% of residents (statewide: 17.2%). Another 1,689 residents are age 60 to 64 and will reach Medicare eligibility within about five years (see our turning 65 guide). Among residents 65 and older, 57% are 65 to 74, 31% are 75 to 84 and 12% are 85 or older (Census 2023 estimates). About 42.1% of people with Medicare in City of Salem are enrolled in a Medicare Advantage plan. That is close to the Virginia average of 39.6%. A county average does not tell you which kind of plan fits you. Whether Medicare Advantage or Original Medicare with a Medigap plan is the better fit depends on your doctors, your prescriptions and how you want to handle costs, so we compare both for your ZIP code.

Insurers choose which counties to serve each year, which means the Medicare Advantage and Part D plans you can pick in City of Salem can be different from the ones offered next door. We are not based in City of Salem; we help City of Salem residents by phone or video call.

Free help available in City of Salem

Start with any of these if you like. We pick up where they leave off: comparing the actual plans for your doctors and drugs, and staying with you each year at renewal.

Common questions

City of Salem Medicare FAQ

Can I get help with Medicare in City of Salem by phone?
Yes. You do not need to travel: we are licensed in Virginia and help City of Salem residents by phone or video, comparing plans from many insurers at no cost to you.
Will my City of Salem doctors and hospitals be covered?
On a Medicare Advantage plan it depends on that plan’s network, so before you enroll we check whether your doctors and the hospitals you use, such as Lewisgale Medical Center, are in network. With a Medicare Supplement (Medigap) plan you can see any doctor who accepts Medicare.
How many people in City of Salem have Medicare Advantage?
CMS reported 2,770 people enrolled in a Medicare Advantage plan out of 6,583 people with Medicare in City of Salem in September 2026, or 42.1%. The Virginia average was 39.6%.
Do you charge City of Salem residents for help?
No. You pay nothing to work with us. Carriers compensate us, and the price of your plan is the same as if you enrolled directly.
When can I enroll or change plans?
You can enroll during your Initial Enrollment Period around your 65th birthday, during the Annual Enrollment Period from October 15 to December 7, or during a Special Enrollment Period if you have a qualifying event such as moving into or within the county.
