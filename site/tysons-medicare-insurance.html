Tysons Medicare FAQ

Can I meet with a Tysons Medicare agent in person?
Yes. We meet Tysons residents by appointment — our office is just minutes away in Vienna, or we can come to you. If you'd rather not travel, we handle everything by phone. Whatever's easiest for you.
Will my Tysons-area doctors and hospitals be covered?
On a Medicare Advantage plan it depends on that plan's network, so before you enroll we verify your specific doctors and hospitals — including Inova facilities serving the Tysons area — are in network. If you choose a Medicare Supplement (Medigap) plan instead, you can see any doctor who accepts Medicare nationwide.
Do you charge Tysons residents for help?
No. Our help is completely free. Carriers pay us a commission when we place a policy, and by law your premium is exactly the same whether you use an agent or enroll on your own.
When can I enroll or change plans?
During your Initial Enrollment Period around your 65th birthday, during the Annual Enrollment Period (Oct 15 – Dec 7), or during a Special Enrollment Period triggered by a qualifying event such as moving to or within Tysons.
