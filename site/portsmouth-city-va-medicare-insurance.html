Who has Medicare in City of Portsmouth

City of Portsmouth sits in a metro area, which the USDA defines as counties in metro areas of 1 million population or more. That usually means more doctors, more hospitals and more plan choices in the area.

CMS counts 19,940 Medicare beneficiaries in City of Portsmouth (2023). Their average age is 72, younger than the Virginia average of 74. About 13% are “dual eligible,” meaning they have both Medicare and Medicaid, higher than the state figure of 9%. Where many people have both, special plans for dual-eligible members can matter; see our Medicaid and dual eligible guide, since availability varies by plan and area.

Among people in Original Medicare in City of Portsmouth, CMS reported about 809 emergency room visits and 236 inpatient hospital stays per 1,000 beneficiaries in 2023, which is higher than the state average for emergency visits (589) and higher than it for hospital stays (206). About 10% received home health care. These figures describe how people use care under Original Medicare; they do not show what any plan covers or costs.

City of Portsmouth Medicare FAQ

Can I get help with Medicare in City of Portsmouth by phone?
Yes. You do not need to travel: we are licensed in Virginia and help City of Portsmouth residents by phone or video, comparing plans from many insurers at no cost to you.
Will my City of Portsmouth doctors and hospitals be covered?
On a Medicare Advantage plan it depends on that plan’s network, so before you enroll we check whether your doctors and the hospitals you use, such as Bon Secours Maryview Medical Center, are in network. With a Medicare Supplement (Medigap) plan you can see any doctor who accepts Medicare.
How many people in City of Portsmouth have Medicare Advantage?
CMS reported 10,061 people enrolled in a Medicare Advantage plan out of 19,333 people with Medicare in City of Portsmouth in September 2026, or 52.0%. The Virginia average was 39.6%.
Do you charge City of Portsmouth residents for help?
No, there is no fee. The insurance company pays us when you enroll, and your premium does not go up because you used an agent.
When can I enroll or change plans?
You can enroll during your Initial Enrollment Period around your 65th birthday, during the Annual Enrollment Period from October 15 to December 7, or during a Special Enrollment Period if you have a qualifying event such as moving into or within the county.
