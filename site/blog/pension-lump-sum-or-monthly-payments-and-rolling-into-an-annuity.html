Pension Lump Sum or Monthly Payments? Rolling It Into an Annuity

If your pension plan offers a lump sum, that lump sum can generally be rolled over to an IRA, and an annuity can then be bought inside the IRA. Monthly lifetime payments cannot be rolled over, and the choice is a plan election that may be hard or impossible to undo. Ask the plan for the numbers before you decide.

By Luay Sadqi, Licensed Agent (NPN 21370662) · October 2026

Important: this article is educational, not personalized advice. Annuity guarantees depend on the issuing company's claims-paying ability, and annuities can carry surrender charges and fees. They are not bank products or investments. Speak with a licensed professional and your tax advisor before moving money.

What can be rolled over

The IRS rollover chart treats defined benefit pension plans as qualified plans, so an eligible lump-sum distribution can be rolled to a traditional IRA (IRS rollover chart). But a distribution that is one of a series of substantially equal payments, such as a monthly pension for life, is not an eligible rollover distribution, and neither is a required minimum distribution (IRS: Rollovers of retirement plan and IRA distributions). In plain terms: you can roll a lump sum, but you cannot roll monthly pension checks.

The traps

  • Withholding. A taxable lump sum paid to you is subject to mandatory withholding, generally 20%, and you must replace it from other funds to roll over the full amount (IRS Topic 413). Choose a direct rollover.
  • An irreversible choice. Once payments begin or the money is rolled, you may not be able to go back. Ask in writing whether the election can be changed and until when.
  • Comparing unlike things. A pension’s monthly amount and an annuity’s payout are not the same kind of promise. Ask for the plan’s lump-sum figure and the monthly figure side by side, and for an annuity quote with all fees and surrender charges stated.
  • Someone else’s sales pitch. Be cautious of anyone who says you will “get more” without showing the comparison on paper.

Where an annuity fits

Some retirees use an annuity inside the IRA to rebuild a stream of payments after taking a lump sum. Whether that is better than the pension depends on the survivor benefit, the interest-rate environment, the carrier and your health, and an annuity’s guarantees depend on the issuing company’s claims-paying ability. Questions to ask the pension plan

  • What is the lump-sum amount, and how is it calculated? Does it change with the date I elect?

  • Does my spouse have to consent to a lump sum?
  • Can the lump sum be sent as a direct rollover, and by what date must I elect?

Common questions

Pension Lump Sum FAQ

Can I roll my pension into an annuity?
If the plan offers a lump sum, that lump sum can generally be rolled to an IRA, which can then buy an annuity. Monthly lifetime pension payments cannot be rolled over.
Will taxes be withheld on a pension lump sum?
If it is paid to you, mandatory withholding of generally 20% applies. A direct rollover to an IRA avoids the withholding.
Can I change my mind after choosing monthly payments?
Usually not once payments begin, but the rules are set by your plan, so ask in writing before you elect.
