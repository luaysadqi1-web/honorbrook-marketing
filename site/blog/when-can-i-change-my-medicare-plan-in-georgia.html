When Can I Change My Medicare Advantage or Medigap Plan in Georgia?

The Medicare windows are federal, but Medigap switching rules vary by state. Here is what applies in Georgia.

By Luay Sadqi, Licensed Agent (NPN 21370662) · October 2026

If you live in Georgia and you have been searching for how and when to change your Medicare Advantage or Medigap plan, you have probably found confusing answers, because the rules depend on which type of plan you have. Here is a clear Georgia-specific guide that separates the two, with the federal windows that apply everywhere and what to check for Georgia in particular.

The quick answer for Georgia

  • Medicare Advantage: you can generally change during the Annual Enrollment Period (October 15 to December 7), during the Medicare Advantage Open Enrollment Period (January 1 to March 31) if you are already in an Advantage plan, or during a Special Enrollment Period if you qualify. These windows are set by Medicare, so they are the same in Georgia as in every state.
  • Medigap: there is no annual deadline, but whether you can switch without health questions depends on protections that include some state rules. See below for what we found for Georgia.

Medicare Advantage changes in Georgia

  1. Annual Enrollment Period (Oct 15 – Dec 7). Change plans for the following January 1. See our Annual Enrollment guide.
  2. Medicare Advantage Open Enrollment (Jan 1 – Mar 31). If you are already in an Advantage plan, you get one change. Read the full guide.
  3. Special Enrollment Periods. Triggered by moving to a new area, losing other coverage, your plan ending, or qualifying for Extra Help or Medicaid, among others. See when you can switch plans.

Medigap changes in Georgia

In the recent 2026 sources we reviewed, Georgia was not listed as having a Medigap birthday rule. That generally means that outside your Medigap Open Enrollment Period and federal guaranteed-issue situations, an insurer may ask health questions when you apply to change plans. Rules can change, so confirm with the Georgia insurance commissioner's office, and do not cancel your current plan until a new one is approved.

Regardless of state, you generally have protection in your six-month Medigap Open Enrollment Period and in certain federal guaranteed-issue situations. Our national guide, when can I change my Medigap plan, explains each one.

Apply first, cancel second. Whatever the rule in Georgia, wait for written approval of your new plan before ending the old one so you never have a gap.

What to think about in Georgia

Georgia has a large Atlanta metro area with many health systems, and smaller communities and rural counties where networks can be narrower. Where you live inside the state can change which Medicare Advantage plans are offered, so compare options at your exact address. If you use specialists in Atlanta but live elsewhere, check that those doctors are in network. We serve people in and around Atlanta, Savannah, Augusta, Columbus and Macon, and we handle everything by phone.

Free help in Georgia

Georgia's State Health Insurance Assistance Program is GeorgiaCares, which offers free, unbiased Medicare counseling. For questions about Medigap rules and consumer protections, you can also contact the Georgia Office of Insurance and Safety Fire Commissioner. Program names and contact details can change, so confirm through Medicare.gov or 1-800-MEDICARE.

Common situations people ask about

  • "My doctor left my plan's network." You may qualify for a Special Enrollment Period in some cases, and you can compare plans at the next window. See what to do when a plan drops your doctor.
  • "My premium or drug costs went up." Review your Annual Notice of Change and compare during the next open window.
  • "I moved within Georgia." A move to a different county can change which plans you can choose, and may open a Special Enrollment Period.
  • "I want to move from Advantage back to Original Medicare." Check your Medigap options before you cancel. Read leaving Medicare Advantage.
  • "I have both Medicare and Medicaid." You may have more frequent change opportunities. See Medicaid and dual-eligible options.
  • "I live outside Atlanta but see specialists there." Check whether those specialists and hospitals are in your plan's network.

A checklist before you change anything in Georgia

  1. Identify which window you are in (AEP, MA-OEP, SEP, or Medigap rules).
  2. List your doctors, hospitals, pharmacies, and medications.
  3. Check each against the plan you are considering at your Georgia address.
  4. If it is Medigap, find out whether underwriting applies and apply before you cancel.
  5. Confirm the effective date in writing.

Changing Medicare Plans in Georgia FAQ

When can I change my Medicare Advantage plan in Georgia?
You can generally change during the Annual Enrollment Period (October 15 to December 7), during the Medicare Advantage Open Enrollment Period (January 1 to March 31) if you are already in an Advantage plan, or during a Special Enrollment Period if you qualify. These windows are the same in Georgia as in every state.
Can I change my Medigap plan any time in Georgia?
You can generally apply any time of year, but whether you can switch without health questions depends on protections such as your Medigap Open Enrollment Period, federal guaranteed-issue rights, and any state rule. Confirm current Georgia rules with the Georgia Office of Insurance and Safety Fire Commissioner.
Where can I get free Medicare help in Georgia?
Georgia's State Health Insurance Assistance Program, GeorgiaCares, offers free, unbiased counseling, and an independent agent like Honorbrook can compare plans available at your address at no cost to you.
