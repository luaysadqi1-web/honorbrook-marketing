Medicare in Fulton County

Fulton County is in Georgia, and its county seat is Atlanta. The U.S. Census Bureau's 2023 population estimates count 141,433 residents age 65 and older in Fulton County. Many of them are choosing between Original Medicare, Medicare Advantage, Medigap and Part D coverage each year.

Grady Memorial Hospital in Atlanta and Northside Hospital Atlanta are among the hospitals that serve Fulton County residents. Medicare Advantage and Part D plan availability, and the doctor and hospital networks that come with them, are set county by county, so we check the options for your ZIP code and your providers rather than guessing. We work with Fulton County residents by phone or video call.

Fulton County ranks 1st of 159 Georgia counties by number of residents age 65 and older, with about 141,433 people in that age group, or 13.1% of residents (statewide: 15.4%). Another 58,488 residents are age 60 to 64 and will reach Medicare eligibility within about five years (see our turning 65 guide). Among residents 65 and older, 60% are 65 to 74, 30% are 75 to 84 and 10% are 85 or older (Census 2023 estimates). About 55.1% of people with Medicare in Fulton County are enrolled in a Medicare Advantage plan. That is close to the Georgia average of 56.2%. A county average does not tell you which kind of plan fits you. Whether Medicare Advantage or Original Medicare with a Medigap plan is the better fit depends on your doctors, your prescriptions and how you want to handle costs, so we compare both for your ZIP code.

Common questions

Fulton County Medicare FAQ

Can I get help with Medicare in Fulton County by phone?
Yes. We are an independent agency licensed in Georgia, and we help Fulton County residents by phone or video call. We compare options, check your doctors and prescriptions, and can help you enroll without you having to travel.
Will my Fulton County doctors and Grady Memorial Hospital be covered?
It depends on the plan. Medicare Advantage networks are set plan by plan and county by county, so before you enroll we check whether your doctors and the hospital you prefer, such as Grady Memorial Hospital, are in the plan's network. With a Medicare Supplement (Medigap) plan you can see any doctor who accepts Medicare.
Do you charge Fulton County residents for help?
No. Our help is free to you. Carriers pay us a commission when we place a policy, and your premium is the same whether you use an agent or enroll on your own.
When can I enroll or change plans?
You can enroll during your Initial Enrollment Period around your 65th birthday, during the Annual Enrollment Period from October 15 to December 7, or during a Special Enrollment Period if you have a qualifying event, such as moving to or within Fulton County.
