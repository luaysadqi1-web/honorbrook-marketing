Why work here

We're independent, which means our agents work for their clients, not for one insurance company's sales targets. It also means our team isn't a call center — no scripts, no cold-call quotas. We're growing because clients stay with us for years, and that only works if the people doing the work actually care about getting it right. If that sounds like the kind of place you want to work, we'd like to hear from you.

A note on licensing: some roles below require an active state insurance license (life and/or health line of authority); others don't. Each listing says which. If you're studying for a license or considering one, reach out anyway — we're happy to talk timing.

Client Support Specialist

No license required

What you'll do: answer phones, schedule client reviews, process enrollment paperwork, and keep client files organized so our licensed agents can focus on advising.

What we're looking for: comfortable on the phone, organized, detail-oriented, and able to keep sensitive client information confidential. Insurance experience is a plus, not a requirement.

Agency Administrator

No license required

What you'll do: keep the agency's operations running — carrier paperwork, license and appointment renewals, CRM upkeep, and day-to-day office management.

What we're looking for: strong organizational skills, comfort with office software and CRM tools, and someone who can juggle multiple carriers' paperwork without dropping a thread. Prior agency or office-management experience preferred.

Licensed Health Insurance Agent (Medicare Specialist)

Health license required

What you'll do: help Medicare-eligible clients compare Medicare Advantage, Medigap, and Part D plans across 90+ carriers — verifying doctors and prescriptions before anyone enrolls.

What we're looking for: an active health insurance license (or actively obtaining one), comfort explaining coverage in plain English, and a no-pressure approach with clients who are often making a confusing decision for the first time.

Licensed Life Insurance Agent

Life license required

What you'll do: help clients compare term, whole, and universal life policies and choose the coverage that fits their family and budget.

What we're looking for: an active life insurance license (or actively obtaining one), and someone who treats life insurance as a family-protection conversation, not a transaction.

Licensed Insurance Agent — Annuities & Retirement Income

Life license required

What you'll do: help clients turn savings into predictable retirement income through fixed and fixed-indexed annuities.

What we're looking for: an active life insurance license, comfort with retirement-income conversations, and clear communication about how annuities work — this role currently covers fixed and fixed-indexed products only, not variable annuities or other securities-registered products.
