Common questions

Vienna Medicare FAQ

Can I meet with a Vienna Medicare agent in person?
Yes — by appointment. Our home office is right here in Vienna at 8609 Westwood Center Drive, so we can meet in person, come to you, or handle everything by phone — whatever is easiest for you.
Will my Vienna-area doctors and hospitals be covered?
On a Medicare Advantage plan it depends on the plan's network, so before you enroll we verify your specific providers — including Inova facilities like the nearby Inova Fairfax Medical Campus — are in network. With a Medigap plan, you can see any doctor or hospital that accepts Medicare.
Do you charge Vienna residents for help?
No, our help is free. Carriers pay us a commission when we place a policy, and by law your premium is the same whether you use an agent or enroll on your own.
When can I enroll or change plans?
During your Initial Enrollment Period around your 65th birthday, during the Annual Enrollment Period (Oct 15 – Dec 7), or during a Special Enrollment Period — for example, when you move to or within Vienna.
