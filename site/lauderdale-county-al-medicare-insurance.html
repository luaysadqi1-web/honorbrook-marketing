Lauderdale County ranks 13th of 67 Alabama counties by number of residents age 65 and older, with about 20,189 people in that age group, or 20.9% of residents (statewide: 18.3%). Another 6,356 residents are age 60 to 64 and will reach Medicare eligibility within about five years (see our turning 65 guide). Among residents 65 and older, 57% are 65 to 74, 33% are 75 to 84 and 10% are 85 or older (Census 2023 estimates). About 54.8% of people with Medicare in Lauderdale County are enrolled in a Medicare Advantage plan. That is lower than the Alabama average of 61.5%. A county average does not tell you which kind of plan fits you. Whether Medicare Advantage or Original Medicare with a Medigap plan is the better fit depends on your doctors, your prescriptions and how you want to handle costs, so we compare both for your ZIP code.

Insurers choose which counties to serve each year, which means the Medicare Advantage and Part D plans you can pick in Lauderdale County can be different from the ones offered next door. We are not based in Lauderdale County; we help Lauderdale County residents by phone or video call.

Free help available in Lauderdale County

  • Alabama SHIP, the Alabama state health insurance assistance program (SHIP), offers free, unbiased Medicare counseling: 1-800-243-5463.
  • Eldercare Locator connects you to your local Area Agency on Aging: 1-800-677-1116 (eldercare.acl.gov).
  • 1-800-MEDICARE (1-800-633-4227) is the federal Medicare helpline.

Start with any of these if you like. We pick up where they leave off: comparing the actual plans for your doctors and drugs, and staying with you each year at renewal.

Common questions

Lauderdale County Medicare FAQ

Can I get help with Medicare in Lauderdale County by phone?
Yes. Phone and video appointments are how we serve Lauderdale County. We hold a Alabama license, compare plans from many carriers and walk you through them from home, with no pressure to enroll.
Will my Lauderdale County doctors and hospitals be covered?
On a Medicare Advantage plan it depends on that plan’s network, so before you enroll we check whether your doctors and the hospitals you use, such as North Alabama Medical Center, are in network. With a Medicare Supplement (Medigap) plan you can see any doctor who accepts Medicare.
How many people in Lauderdale County have Medicare Advantage?
CMS reported 13,085 people enrolled in a Medicare Advantage plan out of 23,882 people with Medicare in Lauderdale County in September 2026, or 54.8%. The Alabama average was 61.5%.
Do you charge Lauderdale County residents for help?
No. Our help is free to you. Carriers pay us a commission when we place a policy, and your premium is the same whether you use an agent or enroll on your own.
When can I enroll or change plans?
You can enroll during your Initial Enrollment Period around your 65th birthday, during the Annual Enrollment Period from October 15 to December 7, or during a Special Enrollment Period if you have a qualifying event such as moving into or within the county.
