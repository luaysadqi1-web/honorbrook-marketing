Medicare in St. Tammany Parish

The parish seat is Covington, and the parish includes the cities of Mandeville and Slidell and the towns of Abita Springs and Madisonville. The U.S. Census Bureau's 2023 population estimates count 52,409 residents age 65 and older in St. Tammany Parish (source: U.S. Census Bureau, 2023 population estimates).

Health systems serving the parish include St. Tammany Health System, with locations in Covington and Mandeville, and Slidell Memorial Hospital in Slidell, which partners with Ochsner Health. Medicare Advantage and Part D plan availability, and the provider networks behind them, are set county by county, which in Louisiana means parish by parish. That is why we check the plans offered where you live and confirm your doctors before you choose.

Who has Medicare in St. Tammany Parish

St. Tammany Parish is a metro county; the U.S. Department of Agriculture describes it as: counties in metro areas of 250,000 to 1 million population. Metro counties usually have more hospitals and more insurers competing for Medicare members, so there can be more plans to compare.

CMS counts 61,207 Medicare beneficiaries in St. Tammany Parish (2023). Their average age is 72, about the same as the Louisiana average of 72. About 13% are “dual eligible,” meaning they have both Medicare and Medicaid, lower than the state figure of 20%.

Among people in Original Medicare in St. Tammany Parish, CMS reported about 624 emergency room visits and 230 inpatient hospital stays per 1,000 beneficiaries in 2023, which is close to the state average for emergency visits (648) and close to it for hospital stays (237). About 11% received home health care. These figures describe how people use care under Original Medicare; they do not show what any plan covers or costs.

Sources: CMS Medicare Geographic Variation, 2023; USDA Rural-Urban Continuum Codes, 2023.

Common questions

St. Tammany Parish Medicare FAQ

Can I get help with Medicare in St. Tammany Parish by phone?
Yes. We are an independent agency licensed in Louisiana, and we help St. Tammany Parish residents by phone or video call. We review your doctors, prescriptions and budget, compare the plans available at your ZIP code, and walk you through enrollment.
Will my St. Tammany Parish doctors and hospitals be covered?
With a Medicare Advantage plan it depends on that plan's network, so before you enroll we check whether your doctors and the hospital you prefer, such as St. Tammany Health System or Slidell Memorial Hospital, are in network. With a Medicare Supplement (Medigap) plan you can generally see any provider who accepts Medicare.
Do you charge St. Tammany Parish residents for help?
No. Our help is free to you. Carriers pay us a commission when we place a policy, and your premium is the same whether you use an agent or enroll on your own.
When can I enroll or change plans?
You can enroll during your Initial Enrollment Period around your 65th birthday, or during the Annual Enrollment Period from October 15 to December 7. A Special Enrollment Period may apply after a qualifying event, such as moving to or within Louisiana.
