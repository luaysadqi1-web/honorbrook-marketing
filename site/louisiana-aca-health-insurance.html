Help with the cost of coverage in Louisiana

Premium tax credits lower your monthly Marketplace premium based on your household income and the cost of local plans. The enhanced federal credits that applied through 2025 have expired, so many households pay more than before. To know your number, run it with your real income. Our subsidy guide explains what happens if your income changes during the year.

Medicaid in Louisiana

Louisiana has expanded Medicaid for adults age 19 to 64. The Louisiana Department of Health’s 2026 table, effective March 1, 2026, lists monthly income up to $1,836 for one person and $3,142 for a household of three. Whether you qualify depends on your household and income, so we check Medicaid and Marketplace eligibility together before you pick a plan.

Plan choices and networks in Louisiana

Marketplace plans come in bronze, silver, gold and platinum levels, and networks differ by insurer and by where you live. Compare the metal tiers, check that your doctors and hospitals are in network, and look at the deductible and out-of-pocket maximum, not just the premium.

Business owners and self-employed in Louisiana

If you run a business, you may have better options than the individual Marketplace. See ICHRA (an employer allowance for employees’ individual plans), group health and SHOP, and self-employed health insurance.

Common questions

Louisiana ACA FAQ

When is ACA open enrollment in Louisiana?
Open enrollment for 2027 coverage in Louisiana runs from November 1, 2026 to January 15, 2027, according to HealthCare.gov. Dates can change, so confirm on the Marketplace site before you plan around them.
Did the enhanced ACA tax credits end?
The enhanced federal premium tax credits expired at the end of 2025 and have not been extended. Your subsidy depends on your income and the plans available where you live, so we check it for you.
Can I get help choosing an ACA plan in Louisiana?
Yes. We are an independent agency licensed in Louisiana. We compare plans, check your premium tax credit and Medicaid eligibility, and help you enroll by phone or video. Our help is free to you.
What if I miss open enrollment?
A qualifying life event, such as losing job-based coverage, moving or a change in household, can open a Special Enrollment Period. See our guide to losing health coverage or call us.
