With roughly 4,493 residents age 65 and older, Meriwether County is 83rd among 159 Georgia counties for older residents. That group is 21.5% of its population, compared with 15.4% across Georgia. The 1,658 residents now between 60 and 64 will become Medicare-eligible soon, and our turning 65 guide walks through it. Among residents 65 and older, 59% are 65 to 74, 32% are 75 to 84 and 9% are 85 or older (Census 2023 estimates). Medicare Advantage enrollment in Meriwether County is 66.6% of people with Medicare, higher than the Georgia average of 56.2%. A county average does not tell you which kind of plan fits you. Whether Medicare Advantage or Original Medicare with a Medigap plan is the better fit depends on your doctors, your prescriptions and how you want to handle costs, so we compare both for your ZIP code.

Who has Medicare in Meriwether County

The U.S. Department of Agriculture classifies Meriwether County as a metro county (counties in metro areas of 1 million population or more). Counties like this tend to have a wider choice of hospitals and Medicare plans than rural ones, though networks still differ plan by plan.

CMS counts 5,890 Medicare beneficiaries in Meriwether County (2023). Their average age is 72, about the same as the Georgia average of 73. About 20% are “dual eligible,” meaning they have both Medicare and Medicaid, higher than the state figure of 12%. Where many people have both, special plans for dual-eligible members can matter; see our Medicaid and dual eligible guide, since availability varies by plan and area.

Among people in Original Medicare in Meriwether County, CMS reported about 651 emergency room visits and 239 inpatient hospital stays per 1,000 beneficiaries in 2023, which is higher than the state average for emergency visits (560) and close to it for hospital stays (223). About 9% received home health care. These figures describe how people use care under Original Medicare; they do not show what any plan covers or costs.

Sources: CMS Medicare Geographic Variation, 2023; USDA Rural-Urban Continuum Codes, 2023.

Common questions

Meriwether County Medicare FAQ

Can I get help with Medicare in Meriwether County by phone?
Yes. We are an independent agency licensed in Georgia, and we help Meriwether County residents by phone or video call, so you can compare plans from home. There is no pressure to enroll.
Will my Meriwether County doctors and hospitals be covered?
On a Medicare Advantage plan it depends on that plan’s network, so before you enroll we check whether your doctors and the hospitals you use, such as Warm Springs Medical Center, are in network. With a Medicare Supplement (Medigap) plan you can see any doctor who accepts Medicare.
How many people in Meriwether County have Medicare Advantage?
CMS reported 3,933 people enrolled in a Medicare Advantage plan out of 5,905 people with Medicare in Meriwether County in September 2026, or 66.6%. The Georgia average was 56.2%.
Do you charge Meriwether County residents for help?
No, there is no fee. The insurance company pays us when you enroll, and your premium does not go up because you used an agent.
When can I enroll or change plans?
You can enroll during your Initial Enrollment Period around your 65th birthday, during the Annual Enrollment Period from October 15 to December 7, or during a Special Enrollment Period if you have a qualifying event such as moving into or within the county.
