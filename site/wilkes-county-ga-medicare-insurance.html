Wilkes County ranks 116th of 159 Georgia counties by number of residents age 65 and older, with about 2,314 people in that age group, or 24.3% of residents (statewide: 15.4%). The 740 residents now between 60 and 64 will become Medicare-eligible soon, and our turning 65 guide walks through it. Census 2023 estimates show 55% of the 65-plus population is under 75, 34% is 75 to 84 and 12% is 85 or older. Medicare Advantage enrollment in Wilkes County is 63.4% of people with Medicare, higher than the Georgia average of 56.2%. A county average does not tell you which kind of plan fits you. Whether Medicare Advantage or Original Medicare with a Medigap plan is the better fit depends on your doctors, your prescriptions and how you want to handle costs, so we compare both for your ZIP code.

Who has Medicare in Wilkes County

The USDA classifies Wilkes County as: urban population of fewer than 5,000, adjacent to a metro area. With fewer hospitals and clinics close by, the right Medicare plan here is often the one whose network includes the providers you can really reach.

CMS counts 2,939 Medicare beneficiaries in Wilkes County (2023). Their average age is 73, about the same as the Georgia average of 73. About 18% are “dual eligible,” meaning they have both Medicare and Medicaid, higher than the state figure of 12%. Where many people have both, special plans for dual-eligible members can matter; see our Medicaid and dual eligible guide, since availability varies by plan and area.

Among people in Original Medicare in Wilkes County, CMS reported about 656 emergency room visits and 207 inpatient hospital stays per 1,000 beneficiaries in 2023, which is higher than the state average for emergency visits (560) and close to it for hospital stays (223). About 10% received home health care. These figures describe how people use care under Original Medicare; they do not show what any plan covers or costs.

Free Medicare counseling for Wilkes County

Common questions

Wilkes County Medicare FAQ

Can I get help with Medicare in Wilkes County by phone?
Yes. You do not need to travel: we are licensed in Georgia and help Wilkes County residents by phone or video, comparing plans from many insurers at no cost to you.
Will my Wilkes County doctors and hospitals be covered?
On a Medicare Advantage plan it depends on that plan’s network, so before you enroll we check whether your doctors and the hospitals you use, such as Wills Memorial Hospital, are in network. With a Medicare Supplement (Medigap) plan you can see any doctor who accepts Medicare.
How many people in Wilkes County have Medicare Advantage?
CMS reported 1,816 people enrolled in a Medicare Advantage plan out of 2,864 people with Medicare in Wilkes County in September 2026, or 63.4%. The Georgia average was 56.2%.
Do you charge Wilkes County residents for help?
No. Our help is free to you. Carriers pay us a commission when we place a policy, and your premium is the same whether you use an agent or enroll on your own.
When can I enroll or change plans?
You can enroll during your Initial Enrollment Period around your 65th birthday, during the Annual Enrollment Period from October 15 to December 7, or during a Special Enrollment Period if you have a qualifying event such as moving into or within the county.
