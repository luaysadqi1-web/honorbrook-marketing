Camden County ranks 43rd of 159 Georgia counties by number of residents age 65 and older, with about 9,093 people in that age group, or 15.6% of residents (statewide: 15.4%). Another 3,522 residents are age 60 to 64 and will reach Medicare eligibility within about five years (see our turning 65 guide). Census 2023 estimates show 61% of the 65-plus population is under 75, 32% is 75 to 84 and 7% is 85 or older. CMS data show 43.1% of Camden County residents with Medicare chose Medicare Advantage, which is lower than the 56.2% average across Georgia. What most people do in a county is less important than what fits you. We weigh Medicare Advantage against Original Medicare with Medigap using your doctors, your drugs and your budget.

Who has Medicare in Camden County

Camden County is a smaller-city and town county; the U.S. Department of Agriculture describes it as: urban population of 20,000 or more, adjacent to a metro area. Counties like this often sit between a metro area and rural communities, so some residents use hospitals and specialists in a neighboring county and a plan’s network across the county line matters.

CMS counts 10,957 Medicare beneficiaries in Camden County (2023). Their average age is 72, about the same as the Georgia average of 73. About 8% are “dual eligible,” meaning they have both Medicare and Medicaid, lower than the state figure of 12%.

Among people in Original Medicare in Camden County, CMS reported about 648 emergency room visits and 202 inpatient hospital stays per 1,000 beneficiaries in 2023, which is higher than the state average for emergency visits (560) and close to it for hospital stays (223). About 7% received home health care. These figures describe how people use care under Original Medicare; they do not show what any plan covers or costs.

Sources: CMS Medicare Geographic Variation, 2023; USDA Rural-Urban Continuum Codes, 2023.

Common questions

Camden County Medicare FAQ

Can I get help with Medicare in Camden County by phone?
Yes. You do not need to travel: we are licensed in Georgia and help Camden County residents by phone or video, comparing plans from many insurers at no cost to you.
Will my Camden County doctors and hospitals be covered?
On a Medicare Advantage plan it depends on that plan’s network, so before you enroll we check whether your doctors and the hospitals you use, such as Southeast Georgia Health System -- Camden Campus, are in network. With a Medicare Supplement (Medigap) plan you can see any doctor who accepts Medicare.
How many people in Camden County have Medicare Advantage?
CMS reported 5,141 people enrolled in a Medicare Advantage plan out of 11,941 people with Medicare in Camden County in September 2026, or 43.1%. The Georgia average was 56.2%.
Do you charge Camden County residents for help?
No, there is no fee. The insurance company pays us when you enroll, and your premium does not go up because you used an agent.
When can I enroll or change plans?
You can enroll during your Initial Enrollment Period around your 65th birthday, during the Annual Enrollment Period from October 15 to December 7, or during a Special Enrollment Period if you have a qualifying event such as moving into or within the county.
