How this estimate works

We grow your current savings and monthly contributions at the yearly rate you enter until your retirement age, using growth after inflation so results are in today’s dollars. We then apply a 4% yearly withdrawal rule of thumb to estimate monthly income, add the Social Security and pension you expect, and compare the total with your goal. If there is a gap, we show the extra monthly saving that would close it. Real results vary with markets, taxes and how long you live.

Frequently asked questions

How much do I need to retire?
It depends on the income you want, what Social Security and any pension will cover, and how long you expect retirement to last. This calculator projects your savings and shows the monthly income they could support using a 4% yearly withdrawal as a rule of thumb.
Why use a return after inflation?
Using a growth rate after inflation keeps all the results in today’s dollars, so you can compare them directly with what things cost now.
Can an annuity change the result?
Some retirees use annuities to turn savings into guaranteed income. Guarantees depend on the insurer’s claims-paying ability and the contract terms. This tool is a projection, not an annuity quote.
