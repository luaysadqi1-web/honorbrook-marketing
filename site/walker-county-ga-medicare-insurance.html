Walker County ranks 31st of 159 Georgia counties by number of residents age 65 and older, with about 13,877 people in that age group, or 20.0% of residents (statewide: 15.4%). Another 4,653 residents are age 60 to 64 and will reach Medicare eligibility within about five years (see our turning 65 guide). Among residents 65 and older, 58% are 65 to 74, 32% are 75 to 84 and 10% are 85 or older (Census 2023 estimates). About 60.4% of people with Medicare in Walker County are enrolled in a Medicare Advantage plan. That is close to the Georgia average of 56.2%. A county average does not tell you which kind of plan fits you. Whether Medicare Advantage or Original Medicare with a Medigap plan is the better fit depends on your doctors, your prescriptions and how you want to handle costs, so we compare both for your ZIP code.

Medicare Advantage and Part D plan availability and provider networks are set county by county, so the plans open to you in Walker County may differ from those in a neighboring county. We are not based in Walker County; we help Walker County residents by phone or video call.

Hospitals and doctors near Walker County

CMS Hospital General Information lists no acute care, critical access or children’s hospital located inside Walker County. Residents often use hospitals in neighboring counties, which matters for Medicare Advantage because a plan’s network may include or leave out the hospital you use. We check your hospitals and doctors before you enroll.

Free help available in Walker County

  • GeorgiaCares, the Georgia state health insurance assistance program (SHIP), offers free, unbiased Medicare counseling: 1-866-552-4464.

  • 1-800-MEDICARE (1-800-633-4227) is the federal Medicare helpline.

Common questions

Walker County Medicare FAQ

Can I get help with Medicare in Walker County by phone?
Yes. You do not need to travel: we are licensed in Georgia and help Walker County residents by phone or video, comparing plans from many insurers at no cost to you.
Will my Walker County doctors and hospitals be covered?
On a Medicare Advantage plan it depends on that plan’s network, so before you enroll we check whether your doctors and the hospitals you use are in network, including hospitals in neighboring counties. With a Medicare Supplement (Medigap) plan you can see any doctor who accepts Medicare.
How many people in Walker County have Medicare Advantage?
CMS reported 9,678 people enrolled in a Medicare Advantage plan out of 16,035 people with Medicare in Walker County in September 2026, or 60.4%. The Georgia average was 56.2%.
Do you charge Walker County residents for help?
No. Our help is free to you. Carriers pay us a commission when we place a policy, and your premium is the same whether you use an agent or enroll on your own.
When can I enroll or change plans?
You can enroll during your Initial Enrollment Period around your 65th birthday, during the Annual Enrollment Period from October 15 to December 7, or during a Special Enrollment Period if you have a qualifying event such as moving into or within the county.
