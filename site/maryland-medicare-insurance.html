Insurance help for Maryland residents

If you live in Maryland and you're sorting through Medicare, a health plan, or life insurance, you don't have to do it alone — and you don't have to pay for guidance. Honorbrook is an independent agency that works for you, not for a single insurance company. We help people turning 65, people already on Medicare who want to make sure they're still in the right plan, families shopping for coverage under 65, and small-business owners trying to take care of their team.

Our help is free because of how the system works: carriers pay us a commission when we place a policy, and by law your premium is exactly the same whether you enroll with us or sign up on your own. So you get an experienced person comparing your options, checking the fine print, and answering the phone after you enroll — at no extra cost. We'd rather earn your trust and your referrals than push you into something that doesn't fit.

Common questions

Maryland Medicare & insurance FAQ

Do you charge Maryland residents for help?
No — our help is completely free. Carriers pay us a commission when we place a policy, and by law your premium is identical whether you use an agent or enroll on your own. You get our comparison, guidance, and year-round support at no added cost.
Which Maryland cities do you serve?
All of Maryland — by phone. We regularly help residents in Bethesda, Rockville, Silver Spring, Baltimore, Annapolis, Frederick, Columbia, and Gaithersburg, and every community in between. No office visit is needed.
What plans can I get in Maryland?
Availability varies by county in Maryland. We compare what's actually offered at your exact address — Medicare Advantage, Medigap, Part D, ACA, ICHRA, group/SHOP coverage, and life insurance — so you see real options rather than a generic list.
Can you help if I'm new to Maryland or moving?
Yes. Moving to a new area — or moving within Maryland — can open a Special Enrollment Period that lets you change or pick up coverage outside the usual windows. Call us and we'll check what's available at your new address.
