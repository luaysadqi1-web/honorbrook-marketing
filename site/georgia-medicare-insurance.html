Common questions

Georgia Medicare & insurance FAQ

Do you charge Georgia residents for help?
No — our help is completely free. Carriers pay us a commission when we place a policy, and by law your premium is identical whether you use an agent or enroll on your own. You get our comparison, guidance, and year-round service at no added cost.
Which Georgia cities do you serve?
All of Georgia, handled by phone — including Atlanta, Savannah, Augusta, Macon, and Marietta, plus every county and small town in between. There's no office visit needed; we help wherever you live in the state.
What plans can I get in Georgia?
Plan availability varies by county in Georgia. We compare what's actually offered at your exact address — Medicare Advantage, Medigap, Part D, ACA plans for those under 65, ICHRA, group coverage, and life insurance — so you only see options you can truly enroll in.
Can you help if I'm new to Georgia or moving?
Yes. Moving to, from, or within Georgia can open a Special Enrollment Period that lets you change or pick up coverage outside the usual windows. Give us a call and we'll confirm your options and the deadlines that apply to your move.
