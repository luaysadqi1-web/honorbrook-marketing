Medicare in Anne Arundel County

Anne Arundel County includes Annapolis, Glen Burnie and communities across the county. According to the U.S. Census Bureau, 2023 population estimates, about 97,887 county residents are age 65 or older, so choosing the right Medicare coverage is a decision many of your neighbors are making too.

Residents are served by hospitals and health systems including Luminis Health Anne Arundel Medical Center in Annapolis and University of Maryland Baltimore Washington Medical Center in Glen Burnie. If you have Medicare Advantage, whether those hospitals and your doctors are in network depends on the plan you pick.

Medicare Advantage and Part D plan availability, premiums and provider networks are set county by county, and they can change every year. That is why we check the plans offered where you live instead of assuming one option fits everyone. Anne Arundel County ranks 4th of 24 Maryland counties by number of residents age 65 and older, with about 97,887 people in that age group, or 16.5% of residents (statewide: 17.3%). Another 38,240 residents are age 60 to 64 and will reach Medicare eligibility within about five years (see our turning 65 guide). Among residents 65 and older, 58% are 65 to 74, 32% are 75 to 84 and 10% are 85 or older (Census 2023 estimates). About 19.8% of people with Medicare in Anne Arundel County are enrolled in a Medicare Advantage plan. That is close to the Maryland average of 24.8%. A county average does not tell you which kind of plan fits you. Whether Medicare Advantage or Original Medicare with a Medigap plan is the better fit depends on your doctors, your prescriptions and how you want to handle costs, so we compare both for your ZIP code.

Free help available in Anne Arundel County

You do not have to rely on us alone. These free resources are also available to Anne Arundel County residents:

  • Maryland SHIP (State Health Insurance Assistance Program): free, unbiased Medicare counseling, 1-800-243-3425.

Common questions

Anne Arundel County Medicare FAQ

Can I get help with Medicare in Anne Arundel County by phone?
Yes. We are an independent agency licensed in Maryland, and we help Anne Arundel County residents by phone or video call. We can review your options, check your doctors and prescriptions, and walk you through enrollment without you needing to travel.
Will my Anne Arundel County doctors and hospital be covered?
On a Medicare Advantage plan it depends on that plan's network, so before you enroll we check whether your doctors and the hospital you would use, such as Luminis Health Anne Arundel and UM Baltimore Washington, are in network. With a Medicare Supplement (Medigap) plan you can see any doctor who accepts Medicare nationwide.
Do you charge Anne Arundel County residents for help?
No. Our help is free to you. Carriers pay us a commission when we place a policy, and by law your premium is the same whether you use an agent or enroll on your own.
When can I enroll or change plans?
You can enroll during your Initial Enrollment Period around your 65th birthday, during the Annual Enrollment Period from October 15 to December 7, or during a Special Enrollment Period triggered by a qualifying event such as moving to a new county.
