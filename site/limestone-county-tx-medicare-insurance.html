With roughly 4,707 residents age 65 and older, Limestone County is 106th among 254 Texas counties for older residents. That group is 21.2% of its population, compared with 13.7% across Texas. About 1,614 more people are 60 to 64 and are heading toward Medicare; our turning 65 guide explains their first steps. Census 2023 estimates show 58% of the 65-plus population is under 75, 31% is 75 to 84 and 11% is 85 or older. About 52.9% of people with Medicare in Limestone County are enrolled in a Medicare Advantage plan. That is close to the Texas average of 54.9%. Averages cannot choose a plan for you. Medicare Advantage and Original Medicare with a Medigap plan each suit different people, depending on doctors, medicines and how you prefer to handle costs, and we compare both at your ZIP code.

Who has Medicare in Limestone County

The USDA classifies Limestone County as: urban population of 5,000 to 20,000, adjacent to a metro area. In counties of this type, people often travel to a larger neighboring county for specialists, so we check whether your plan’s network reaches there.

CMS counts 5,609 Medicare beneficiaries in Limestone County (2023). Their average age is 71, younger than the Texas average of 73. About 18% are “dual eligible,” meaning they have both Medicare and Medicaid, higher than the state figure of 11%. Where many people have both, special plans for dual-eligible members can matter; see our Medicaid and dual eligible guide, since availability varies by plan and area.

Among people in Original Medicare in Limestone County, CMS reported about 645 emergency room visits and 202 inpatient hospital stays per 1,000 beneficiaries in 2023, which is close to the state average for emergency visits (580) and close to it for hospital stays (226). About 8% received home health care. These figures describe how people use care under Original Medicare; they do not show what any plan covers or costs.

Sources: CMS Medicare Geographic Variation, 2023; USDA Rural-Urban Continuum Codes, 2023.

Medicare Advantage and Part D plan availability and provider networks are set county by county, so the plans open to you in Limestone County may differ from those in a neighboring county. We are not based in Limestone County; we help Limestone County residents by phone or video call.

Common questions

Limestone County Medicare FAQ

Can I get help with Medicare in Limestone County by phone?
Yes. We are an independent agency licensed in Texas, and we help Limestone County residents by phone or video call, so you can compare plans from home. There is no pressure to enroll.
Will my Limestone County doctors and hospitals be covered?
On a Medicare Advantage plan it depends on that plan’s network, so before you enroll we check whether your doctors and the hospitals you use, such as Limestone Medical Center, Parkview Regional Hospital, are in network. With a Medicare Supplement (Medigap) plan you can see any doctor who accepts Medicare.
How many people in Limestone County have Medicare Advantage?
CMS reported 2,930 people enrolled in a Medicare Advantage plan out of 5,540 people with Medicare in Limestone County in September 2026, or 52.9%. The Texas average was 54.9%.
Do you charge Limestone County residents for help?
No, there is no fee. The insurance company pays us when you enroll, and your premium does not go up because you used an agent.
When can I enroll or change plans?
You can enroll during your Initial Enrollment Period around your 65th birthday, during the Annual Enrollment Period from October 15 to December 7, or during a Special Enrollment Period if you have a qualifying event such as moving into or within the county.
