Common questions

McLean Medicare FAQ

Can I meet with a McLean Medicare agent in person?
Yes. We meet by appointment — our office is nearby in Vienna, just a short drive from McLean — or we can come to you. If it's easier, we handle everything by phone. Whatever works for you.
Will my McLean-area doctors and hospitals be covered?
On a Medicare Advantage plan it depends on the plan's network, so before you enroll we verify your specific doctors and hospitals — including the Inova facilities serving the McLean area — are in network. With a Medigap plan, you can see any doctor or hospital that accepts Medicare.
Do you charge McLean residents for help?
No, our help is free. Carriers pay us a commission when we place a policy, and by law your premium is the same whether you use an agent or enroll on your own. You get local guidance at no extra cost.
When can I enroll or change plans?
During your Initial Enrollment Period around your 65th birthday, during the Annual Enrollment Period (Oct 15 – Dec 7), or during a Special Enrollment Period triggered by a qualifying event such as moving to or within McLean.
