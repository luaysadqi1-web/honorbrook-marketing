Among North Carolina’s 100 counties, Perquimans County comes in 88th for residents age 65 and up (3,841). Older adults make up 28.7% of the population here; the state figure is 17.6%. About 1,150 more people are 60 to 64 and are heading toward Medicare; our turning 65 guide explains their first steps. Among residents 65 and older, 57% are 65 to 74, 34% are 75 to 84 and 9% are 85 or older (Census 2023 estimates). About 44.0% of people with Medicare in Perquimans County are enrolled in a Medicare Advantage plan. That is lower than the North Carolina average of 57.9%. What most people do in a county is less important than what fits you. We weigh Medicare Advantage against Original Medicare with Medigap using your doctors, your drugs and your budget.

Who has Medicare in Perquimans County

The USDA classifies Perquimans County as: urban population of fewer than 5,000, adjacent to a metro area. With fewer hospitals and clinics close by, the right Medicare plan here is often the one whose network includes the providers you can really reach.

CMS counts 4,436 Medicare beneficiaries in Perquimans County (2023). Their average age is 73, about the same as the North Carolina average of 73. About 12% are “dual eligible,” meaning they have both Medicare and Medicaid, close to the state figure of 13%.

Among people in Original Medicare in Perquimans County, CMS reported about 709 emergency room visits and 212 inpatient hospital stays per 1,000 beneficiaries in 2023, which is higher than the state average for emergency visits (572) and close to it for hospital stays (210). About 7% received home health care. These figures describe how people use care under Original Medicare; they do not show what any plan covers or costs.

Sources: CMS Medicare Geographic Variation, 2023; USDA Rural-Urban Continuum Codes, 2023.

Where to get free Medicare help in Perquimans County

  • North Carolina’s SHIP program, SHIIP, gives free Medicare counseling that is not tied to any insurer: 1-855-408-1212.
  • Eldercare Locator connects you to your local Area Agency on Aging: 1-800-677-1116 (eldercare.acl.gov).
  • The federal Medicare helpline is 1-800-MEDICARE, 1-800-633-4227.

They are good places to ask questions. What we add is a side-by-side comparison of the plans you can really join, plus help with enrollment and yearly reviews.

Common questions

Perquimans County Medicare FAQ

Can I get help with Medicare in Perquimans County by phone?
Yes. Phone and video appointments are how we serve Perquimans County. We hold a North Carolina license, compare plans from many carriers and walk you through them from home, with no pressure to enroll.
Will my Perquimans County doctors and hospitals be covered?
On a Medicare Advantage plan it depends on that plan’s network, so before you enroll we check whether your doctors and the hospitals you use are in network, including hospitals in neighboring counties. With a Medicare Supplement (Medigap) plan you can see any doctor who accepts Medicare.
How many people in Perquimans County have Medicare Advantage?
CMS reported 1,970 people enrolled in a Medicare Advantage plan out of 4,475 people with Medicare in Perquimans County in September 2026, or 44.0%. The North Carolina average was 57.9%.
Do you charge Perquimans County residents for help?
No. Our help is free to you. Carriers pay us a commission when we place a policy, and your premium is the same whether you use an agent or enroll on your own.
When can I enroll or change plans?
You can enroll during your Initial Enrollment Period around your 65th birthday, during the Annual Enrollment Period from October 15 to December 7, or during a Special Enrollment Period if you have a qualifying event such as moving into or within the county.
