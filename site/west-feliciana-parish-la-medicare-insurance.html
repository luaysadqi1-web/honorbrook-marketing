Who has Medicare in West Feliciana Parish

West Feliciana Parish sits in a metro area, which the USDA defines as counties in metro areas of 250,000 to 1 million population. That usually means more doctors, more hospitals and more plan choices in the area.

CMS counts 2,451 Medicare beneficiaries in West Feliciana Parish (2023). Their average age is 72, about the same as the Louisiana average of 72. About 17% are “dual eligible,” meaning they have both Medicare and Medicaid, close to the state figure of 20%.

Among people in Original Medicare in West Feliciana Parish, CMS reported about 519 emergency room visits and 185 inpatient hospital stays per 1,000 beneficiaries in 2023, which is lower than the state average for emergency visits (648) and lower than it for hospital stays (237). About 9% received home health care. These figures describe how people use care under Original Medicare; they do not show what any plan covers or costs.

Sources: CMS Medicare Geographic Variation, 2023; USDA Rural-Urban Continuum Codes, 2023.

Common questions

West Feliciana Parish Medicare FAQ

Can I get help with Medicare in West Feliciana Parish by phone?
Yes. We are an independent agency licensed in Louisiana, and we help West Feliciana Parish residents by phone or video call, so you can compare plans from home. There is no pressure to enroll.
Will my West Feliciana Parish doctors and hospitals be covered?
On a Medicare Advantage plan it depends on that plan’s network, so before you enroll we check whether your doctors and the hospitals you use, such as West Feliciana Parish Hospital, are in network. With a Medicare Supplement (Medigap) plan you can see any doctor who accepts Medicare.
How many people in West Feliciana Parish have Medicare Advantage?
CMS reported 1,319 people enrolled in a Medicare Advantage plan out of 2,545 people with Medicare in West Feliciana Parish in September 2026, or 51.8%. The Louisiana average was 57.5%.
Do you charge West Feliciana Parish residents for help?
No, there is no fee. The insurance company pays us when you enroll, and your premium does not go up because you used an agent.
When can I enroll or change plans?
You can enroll during your Initial Enrollment Period around your 65th birthday, during the Annual Enrollment Period from October 15 to December 7, or during a Special Enrollment Period if you have a qualifying event such as moving into or within the county.
