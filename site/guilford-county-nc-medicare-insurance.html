Medicare in Guilford County

Guilford County includes Greensboro, the county seat, and High Point. According to the U.S. Census Bureau, 2023 population estimates, about 89,625 Guilford County residents are age 65 or older, so choosing the right Medicare coverage is a decision many of your neighbors are making too. Whether you are turning 65, retiring, or reviewing your current plan, we can walk through your choices with you on a call at a time that suits you.

Major hospitals and health systems serving the county include Cone Health’s Moses Cone Hospital in Greensboro. Medicare Advantage and Part D plan availability and provider networks are set county by county, and they can change every year. Original Medicare (Parts A and B) is the same nationwide, but the private plans you can add to it are not. That is why we check the plans offered where you live and confirm that your doctors and pharmacy are included before you enroll.

Who has Medicare in Guilford County

Guilford County sits in a metro area, which the USDA defines as counties in metro areas of 250,000 to 1 million population. That usually means more doctors, more hospitals and more plan choices in the area.

CMS counts 108,103 Medicare beneficiaries in Guilford County (2023). Their average age is 73, about the same as the North Carolina average of 73. About 14% are “dual eligible,” meaning they have both Medicare and Medicaid, close to the state figure of 13%.

Among people in Original Medicare in Guilford County, CMS reported about 568 emergency room visits and 200 inpatient hospital stays per 1,000 beneficiaries in 2023, which is close to the state average for emergency visits (572) and close to it for hospital stays (210). About 8% received home health care. These figures describe how people use care under Original Medicare; they do not show what any plan covers or costs.

Sources: CMS Medicare Geographic Variation, 2023; USDA Rural-Urban Continuum Codes, 2023.

Common questions

Guilford County Medicare FAQ

Can I get help with Medicare in Guilford County by phone?
Yes. We are an independent agency licensed in North Carolina, and we help Guilford County residents by phone or video call. We review your doctors, prescriptions and budget, then walk you through the options available at your ZIP code.
Will my Guilford County doctors and Moses Cone Hospital be covered?
On a Medicare Advantage plan it depends on that plan's network, so before you enroll we check whether your specific doctors and the hospitals you use are in network. Original Medicare with a Medicare Supplement (Medigap) plan lets you see any provider that accepts Medicare.
Do you charge Guilford County residents for help?
No. Our help is free to you. Carriers compensate us when you enroll, and that does not change your premium.
When can I enroll or change plans?
You can enroll during your Initial Enrollment Period around your 65th birthday, during the Annual Enrollment Period from October 15 to December 7, or during a Special Enrollment Period if you have a qualifying event. We can tell you which applies to you.
