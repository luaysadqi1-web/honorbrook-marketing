Medicare in Forsyth County

Forsyth County includes Winston-Salem, the county seat, along with Kernersville, Clemmons and Lewisville. According to the U.S. Census Bureau, 2023 population estimates, about 68,335 Forsyth County residents are age 65 or older, so choosing the right Medicare coverage is a decision many of your neighbors are making too. Whether you are turning 65, retiring, or reviewing your current plan, we can walk through your choices with you on a call at a time that suits you.

Major hospitals and health systems serving the county include Atrium Health Wake Forest Baptist Medical Center and Novant Health Forsyth Medical Center in Winston-Salem. Medicare Advantage and Part D plan availability and provider networks are set county by county, and they can change every year. Original Medicare (Parts A and B) is the same nationwide, but the private plans you can add to it are not. That is why we check the plans offered where you live and confirm that your doctors and pharmacy are included before you enroll.

Forsyth County ranks 4th of 100 North Carolina counties by number of residents age 65 and older, with about 68,335 people in that age group, or 17.4% of residents (statewide: 17.6%). Another 25,130 residents are age 60 to 64 and will reach Medicare eligibility within about five years (see our turning 65 guide). Among residents 65 and older, 59% are 65 to 74, 31% are 75 to 84 and 10% are 85 or older (Census 2023 estimates). About 69.3% of people with Medicare in Forsyth County are enrolled in a Medicare Advantage plan. That is higher than the North Carolina average of 57.9%. A county average does not tell you which kind of plan fits you. Whether Medicare Advantage or Original Medicare with a Medigap plan is the better fit depends on your doctors, your prescriptions and how you want to handle costs, so we compare both for your ZIP code.

Common questions

Forsyth County Medicare FAQ

Can I get help with Medicare in Forsyth County by phone?
Yes. We are an independent agency licensed in North Carolina, and we help Forsyth County residents by phone or video call. We review your doctors, prescriptions and budget, then walk you through the options available at your ZIP code.
Will my Forsyth County doctors and Atrium Health Wake Forest Baptist or Novant Health Forsyth be covered?
On a Medicare Advantage plan it depends on that plan's network, so before you enroll we check whether your specific doctors and the hospitals you use are in network. Original Medicare with a Medicare Supplement (Medigap) plan lets you see any provider that accepts Medicare.
Do you charge Forsyth County residents for help?
No. Our help is free to you. Carriers compensate us when you enroll, and that does not change your premium.
When can I enroll or change plans?
You can enroll during your Initial Enrollment Period around your 65th birthday, during the Annual Enrollment Period from October 15 to December 7, or during a Special Enrollment Period if you have a qualifying event. We can tell you which applies to you.
