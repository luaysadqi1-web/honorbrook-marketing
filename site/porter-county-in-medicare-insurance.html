With roughly 33,101 residents age 65 and older, Porter County is 7th among 92 Indiana counties for older residents. That group is 18.9% of its population, compared with 17.2% across Indiana. The 11,703 residents now between 60 and 64 will become Medicare-eligible soon, and our turning 65 guide walks through it. Among residents 65 and older, 61% are 65 to 74, 29% are 75 to 84 and 10% are 85 or older (Census 2023 estimates). Medicare Advantage enrollment in Porter County is 39.9% of people with Medicare, lower than the Indiana average of 51.1%. A county average does not tell you which kind of plan fits you. Whether Medicare Advantage or Original Medicare with a Medigap plan is the better fit depends on your doctors, your prescriptions and how you want to handle costs, so we compare both for your ZIP code.

Who has Medicare in Porter County

Porter County sits in a metro area, which the USDA defines as counties in metro areas of 1 million population or more. That usually means more doctors, more hospitals and more plan choices in the area.

CMS counts 38,533 Medicare beneficiaries in Porter County (2023). Their average age is 73, about the same as the Indiana average of 72. About 12% are “dual eligible,” meaning they have both Medicare and Medicaid, lower than the state figure of 16%.

Among people in Original Medicare in Porter County, CMS reported about 648 emergency room visits and 249 inpatient hospital stays per 1,000 beneficiaries in 2023, which is close to the state average for emergency visits (619) and close to it for hospital stays (235). About 7% received home health care. These figures describe how people use care under Original Medicare; they do not show what any plan covers or costs.

Sources: CMS Medicare Geographic Variation, 2023; USDA Rural-Urban Continuum Codes, 2023.

Insurers choose which counties to serve each year, which means the Medicare Advantage and Part D plans you can pick in Porter County can be different from the ones offered next door. We are not based in Porter County; we help Porter County residents by phone or video call.

Common questions

Porter County Medicare FAQ

Can I get help with Medicare in Porter County by phone?
Yes. We are an independent agency licensed in Indiana, and we help Porter County residents by phone or video call, so you can compare plans from home. There is no pressure to enroll.
Will my Porter County doctors and hospitals be covered?
On a Medicare Advantage plan it depends on that plan’s network, so before you enroll we check whether your doctors and the hospitals you use, such as Northwest Health - Porter, are in network. With a Medicare Supplement (Medigap) plan you can see any doctor who accepts Medicare.
How many people in Porter County have Medicare Advantage?
CMS reported 16,019 people enrolled in a Medicare Advantage plan out of 40,126 people with Medicare in Porter County in September 2026, or 39.9%. The Indiana average was 51.1%.
Do you charge Porter County residents for help?
No. You pay nothing to work with us. Carriers compensate us, and the price of your plan is the same as if you enrolled directly.
When can I enroll or change plans?
You can enroll during your Initial Enrollment Period around your 65th birthday, during the Annual Enrollment Period from October 15 to December 7, or during a Special Enrollment Period if you have a qualifying event such as moving into or within the county.
