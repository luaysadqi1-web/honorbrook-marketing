Medicare in East Baton Rouge Parish

The parish seat is Baton Rouge, the state capital, and the parish also includes Baker and Zachary. The U.S. Census Bureau's 2023 population estimates count 70,903 residents age 65 and older in East Baton Rouge Parish (source: U.S. Census Bureau, 2023 population estimates).

Hospitals and health systems serving the parish include Our Lady of the Lake Regional Medical Center in Baton Rouge and Baton Rouge General, which has Mid City and Bluebonnet campuses. Medicare Advantage and Part D plan availability, and the provider networks behind them, are set county by county, which in Louisiana means parish by parish. That is why we check the plans offered where you live and confirm your doctors before you choose.

With roughly 70,903 residents age 65 and older, East Baton Rouge Parish is 2nd among 64 Louisiana parishes for older residents. That group is 15.8% of its population, compared with 17.4% across Louisiana. About 24,991 more people are 60 to 64 and are heading toward Medicare; our turning 65 guide explains their first steps. About 65.3% of people with Medicare in East Baton Rouge Parish are enrolled in a Medicare Advantage plan. That is higher than the Louisiana average of 57.5%. What most people do in a county is less important than what fits you. We weigh Medicare Advantage against Original Medicare with Medigap using your doctors, your drugs and your budget.

Who has Medicare in East Baton Rouge Parish

East Baton Rouge Parish is a metro county; the U.S. Department of Agriculture describes it as: counties in metro areas of 250,000 to 1 million population. Metro counties usually have more hospitals and more insurers competing for Medicare members, so there can be more plans to compare.

CMS counts 83,977 Medicare beneficiaries in East Baton Rouge Parish (2023). Their average age is 72, about the same as the Louisiana average of 72. About 18% are “dual eligible,” meaning they have both Medicare and Medicaid, close to the state figure of 20%.

Among people in Original Medicare in East Baton Rouge Parish, CMS reported about 536 emergency room visits and 213 inpatient hospital stays per 1,000 beneficiaries in 2023, which is lower than the state average for emergency visits (648) and close to it for hospital stays (237). About 10% received home health care. These figures describe how people use care under Original Medicare; they do not show what any plan covers or costs.

Sources: CMS Medicare Geographic Variation, 2023; USDA Rural-Urban Continuum Codes, 2023.

Common questions

East Baton Rouge Parish Medicare FAQ

Can I get help with Medicare in East Baton Rouge Parish by phone?
Yes. We are an independent agency licensed in Louisiana, and we help East Baton Rouge Parish residents by phone or video call. We review your doctors, prescriptions and budget, compare the plans available at your ZIP code, and walk you through enrollment.
Will my East Baton Rouge Parish doctors and hospitals be covered?
With a Medicare Advantage plan it depends on that plan's network, so before you enroll we check whether your doctors and the hospital you prefer, such as Our Lady of the Lake or Baton Rouge General, are in network. With a Medicare Supplement (Medigap) plan you can generally see any provider who accepts Medicare.
Do you charge East Baton Rouge Parish residents for help?
No. Our help is free to you. Carriers pay us a commission when we place a policy, and your premium is the same whether you use an agent or enroll on your own.
When can I enroll or change plans?
You can enroll during your Initial Enrollment Period around your 65th birthday, or during the Annual Enrollment Period from October 15 to December 7. A Special Enrollment Period may apply after a qualifying event, such as moving to or within Louisiana.
