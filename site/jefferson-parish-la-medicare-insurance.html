Medicare in Jefferson Parish

The parish seat is Gretna, and the parish includes Kenner, Harahan, Westwego and Metairie, along with communities such as Marrero, Harvey and River Ridge. The U.S. Census Bureau's 2023 population estimates count 82,284 residents age 65 and older in Jefferson Parish (source: U.S. Census Bureau, 2023 population estimates).

Hospitals serving the parish include East Jefferson General Hospital in Metairie, part of LCMC Health, and Ochsner Medical Center on Jefferson Highway. Medicare Advantage and Part D plan availability, and the provider networks behind them, are set county by county, which in Louisiana means parish by parish. That is why we check the plans offered where you live and confirm your doctors before you choose.

With roughly 82,284 residents age 65 and older, Jefferson Parish is 1st among 64 Louisiana parishes for older residents. That group is 19.5% of its population, compared with 17.4% across Louisiana. About 28,744 more people are 60 to 64 and are heading toward Medicare; our turning 65 guide explains their first steps. Census 2023 estimates show 59% of the 65-plus population is under 75, 31% is 75 to 84 and 10% is 85 or older. About 71.2% of people with Medicare in Jefferson Parish are enrolled in a Medicare Advantage plan. That is higher than the Louisiana average of 57.5%. A county average does not tell you which kind of plan fits you. Whether Medicare Advantage or Original Medicare with a Medigap plan is the better fit depends on your doctors, your prescriptions and how you want to handle costs, so we compare both for your ZIP code.

Who has Medicare in Jefferson Parish

Jefferson Parish is a metro county; the U.S. Department of Agriculture describes it as: counties in metro areas of 1 million population or more. Metro counties usually have more hospitals and more insurers competing for Medicare members, so there can be more plans to compare.

CMS counts 95,478 Medicare beneficiaries in Jefferson Parish (2023). Their average age is 72, about the same as the Louisiana average of 72. About 20% are “dual eligible,” meaning they have both Medicare and Medicaid, close to the state figure of 20%.

Among people in Original Medicare in Jefferson Parish, CMS reported about 620 emergency room visits and 212 inpatient hospital stays per 1,000 beneficiaries in 2023, which is close to the state average for emergency visits (648) and close to it for hospital stays (237). About 10% received home health care. These figures describe how people use care under Original Medicare; they do not show what any plan covers or costs.

Sources: CMS Medicare Geographic Variation, 2023; USDA Rural-Urban Continuum Codes, 2023.

Common questions

Jefferson Parish Medicare FAQ

Can I get help with Medicare in Jefferson Parish by phone?
Yes. We are an independent agency licensed in Louisiana, and we help Jefferson Parish residents by phone or video call. We review your doctors, prescriptions and budget, compare the plans available at your ZIP code, and walk you through enrollment.
Will my Jefferson Parish doctors and hospitals be covered?
With a Medicare Advantage plan it depends on that plan's network, so before you enroll we check whether your doctors and the hospital you prefer, such as East Jefferson General or Ochsner Medical Center, are in network. With a Medicare Supplement (Medigap) plan you can generally see any provider who accepts Medicare.
Do you charge Jefferson Parish residents for help?
No. Our help is free to you. Carriers pay us a commission when we place a policy, and your premium is the same whether you use an agent or enroll on your own.
When can I enroll or change plans?
You can enroll during your Initial Enrollment Period around your 65th birthday, or during the Annual Enrollment Period from October 15 to December 7. A Special Enrollment Period may apply after a qualifying event, such as moving to or within Louisiana.
