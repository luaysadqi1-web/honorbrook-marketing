Owen County ranks 67th of 92 Indiana counties by number of residents age 65 and older, with about 4,642 people in that age group, or 21.6% of residents (statewide: 17.2%). Another 1,849 residents are age 60 to 64 and will reach Medicare eligibility within about five years (see our turning 65 guide). Among residents 65 and older, 62% are 65 to 74, 29% are 75 to 84 and 8% are 85 or older (Census 2023 estimates). CMS data show 54.6% of Owen County residents with Medicare chose Medicare Advantage, which is close to the 51.1% average across Indiana. A county average does not tell you which kind of plan fits you. Whether Medicare Advantage or Original Medicare with a Medigap plan is the better fit depends on your doctors, your prescriptions and how you want to handle costs, so we compare both for your ZIP code.

Who has Medicare in Owen County

Owen County sits in a metro area, which the USDA defines as counties in metro areas of fewer than 250,000 population. That usually means more doctors, more hospitals and more plan choices in the area.

CMS counts 5,562 Medicare beneficiaries in Owen County (2023). Their average age is 72, about the same as the Indiana average of 72. About 17% are “dual eligible,” meaning they have both Medicare and Medicaid, close to the state figure of 16%.

Among people in Original Medicare in Owen County, CMS reported about 580 emergency room visits and 202 inpatient hospital stays per 1,000 beneficiaries in 2023, which is close to the state average for emergency visits (619) and lower than it for hospital stays (235). About 6% received home health care. These figures describe how people use care under Original Medicare; they do not show what any plan covers or costs.

Sources: CMS Medicare Geographic Variation, 2023; USDA Rural-Urban Continuum Codes, 2023.

Where you live decides which Medicare Advantage and Part D plans you can join, and Owen County may have a different set of plans than the counties around it. We are not based in Owen County; we help Owen County residents by phone or video call.

Hospitals and doctors near Owen County

CMS Hospital General Information lists no acute care, critical access or children’s hospital located inside Owen County. Residents often use hospitals in neighboring counties, which matters for Medicare Advantage because a plan’s network may include or leave out the hospital you use. Common questions

Owen County Medicare FAQ

Can I get help with Medicare in Owen County by phone?
Yes. Phone and video appointments are how we serve Owen County. We hold a Indiana license, compare plans from many carriers and walk you through them from home, with no pressure to enroll.
Will my Owen County doctors and hospitals be covered?
On a Medicare Advantage plan it depends on that plan’s network, so before you enroll we check whether your doctors and the hospitals you use are in network, including hospitals in neighboring counties. With a Medicare Supplement (Medigap) plan you can see any doctor who accepts Medicare.
How many people in Owen County have Medicare Advantage?
CMS reported 3,074 people enrolled in a Medicare Advantage plan out of 5,626 people with Medicare in Owen County in September 2026, or 54.6%. The Indiana average was 51.1%.
Do you charge Owen County residents for help?
No, there is no fee. The insurance company pays us when you enroll, and your premium does not go up because you used an agent.
When can I enroll or change plans?
You can enroll during your Initial Enrollment Period around your 65th birthday, during the Annual Enrollment Period from October 15 to December 7, or during a Special Enrollment Period if you have a qualifying event such as moving into or within the county.
