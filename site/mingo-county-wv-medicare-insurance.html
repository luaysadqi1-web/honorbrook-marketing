Among West Virginia’s 55 counties, Mingo County comes in 29th for residents age 65 and up (4,822). Older adults make up 21.9% of the population here; the state figure is 21.5%. The 1,571 residents now between 60 and 64 will become Medicare-eligible soon, and our turning 65 guide walks through it. Census 2023 estimates show 63% of the 65-plus population is under 75, 29% is 75 to 84 and 8% is 85 or older. About 69.5% of people with Medicare in Mingo County are enrolled in a Medicare Advantage plan. That is higher than the West Virginia average of 56.6%. Averages cannot choose a plan for you. Medicare Advantage and Original Medicare with a Medigap plan each suit different people, depending on doctors, medicines and how you prefer to handle costs, and we compare both at your ZIP code.

Who has Medicare in Mingo County

Mingo County is a rural county (USDA: urban population of fewer than 5,000, adjacent to a metro area). Plan choices can be narrower than in a city, and we focus on networks that include the doctors and hospital you actually use.

CMS counts 6,791 Medicare beneficiaries in Mingo County (2023). Their average age is 68, younger than the West Virginia average of 72. About 35% are “dual eligible,” meaning they have both Medicare and Medicaid, higher than the state figure of 20%. Where many people have both, special plans for dual-eligible members can matter; see our Medicaid and dual eligible guide, since availability varies by plan and area.

Among people in Original Medicare in Mingo County, CMS reported about 808 emergency room visits and 303 inpatient hospital stays per 1,000 beneficiaries in 2023, which is higher than the state average for emergency visits (684) and higher than it for hospital stays (250). About 6% received home health care. These figures describe how people use care under Original Medicare; they do not show what any plan covers or costs.

Common questions

Mingo County Medicare FAQ

Can I get help with Medicare in Mingo County by phone?
Yes. We are an independent agency licensed in West Virginia, and we help Mingo County residents by phone or video call, so you can compare plans from home. There is no pressure to enroll.
Will my Mingo County doctors and hospitals be covered?
On a Medicare Advantage plan it depends on that plan’s network, so before you enroll we check whether your doctors and the hospitals you use, such as Williamson Memorial, are in network. With a Medicare Supplement (Medigap) plan you can see any doctor who accepts Medicare.
How many people in Mingo County have Medicare Advantage?
CMS reported 4,510 people enrolled in a Medicare Advantage plan out of 6,486 people with Medicare in Mingo County in September 2026, or 69.5%. The West Virginia average was 56.6%.
Do you charge Mingo County residents for help?
No. You pay nothing to work with us. Carriers compensate us, and the price of your plan is the same as if you enrolled directly.
When can I enroll or change plans?
You can enroll during your Initial Enrollment Period around your 65th birthday, during the Annual Enrollment Period from October 15 to December 7, or during a Special Enrollment Period if you have a qualifying event such as moving into or within the county.
