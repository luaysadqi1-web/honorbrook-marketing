With roughly 5,995 residents age 65 and older, Caroline County is 22nd among 24 Maryland counties for older residents. That group is 17.8% of its population, compared with 17.3% across Maryland. About 2,453 more people are 60 to 64 and are heading toward Medicare; our turning 65 guide explains their first steps. By age, the 65-and-older group splits 60% ages 65 to 74, 31% ages 75 to 84 and 9% age 85 and up (Census 2023). About 23.7% of people with Medicare in Caroline County are enrolled in a Medicare Advantage plan. That is close to the Maryland average of 24.8%. What most people do in a county is less important than what fits you. We weigh Medicare Advantage against Original Medicare with Medigap using your doctors, your drugs and your budget.

Who has Medicare in Caroline County

Caroline County is a non-metro county with towns and small cities (USDA: urban population of 5,000 to 20,000, adjacent to a metro area). Care is often split between local providers and a bigger hospital elsewhere, which is worth testing against each plan’s network.

CMS counts 7,388 Medicare beneficiaries in Caroline County (2023). Their average age is 72, about the same as the Maryland average of 73. About 23% are “dual eligible,” meaning they have both Medicare and Medicaid, higher than the state figure of 17%. Where many people have both, special plans for dual-eligible members can matter; see our Medicaid and dual eligible guide, since availability varies by plan and area.

Among people in Original Medicare in Caroline County, CMS reported about 595 emergency room visits and 187 inpatient hospital stays per 1,000 beneficiaries in 2023, which is close to the state average for emergency visits (555) and lower than it for hospital stays (217). About 10% received home health care. These figures describe how people use care under Original Medicare; they do not show what any plan covers or costs.

Sources: CMS Medicare Geographic Variation, 2023; USDA Rural-Urban Continuum Codes, 2023.

Medicare Advantage and Part D plan availability and provider networks are set county by county, so the plans open to you in Caroline County may differ from those in a neighboring county. We are not based in Caroline County; we help Caroline County residents by phone or video call.

Hospitals and doctors near Caroline County

CMS Hospital General Information lists no acute care, critical access or children’s hospital located inside Caroline County. Residents often use hospitals in neighboring counties, which matters for Medicare Advantage because a plan’s network may include or leave out the hospital you use. We check your hospitals and doctors before you enroll.

Caroline County: communities and ZIP codes

Census Bureau 2023 estimates put the biggest communities in Caroline County at Denton (4,979), Federalsburg (2,804), Greensboro (1,925), Ridgely (1,611), Preston (676), Goldsboro (210), counting only the part of each community inside the county.

We look up plans by ZIP code. In Caroline County, that means areas such as 21629, 21632, 21636, 21639, 21640, 21641, 21655, 21660, based on Census ZIP Code Tabulation Areas that lie mostly in the county.

Common questions

Caroline County Medicare FAQ

Can I get help with Medicare in Caroline County by phone?
Yes. Phone and video appointments are how we serve Caroline County. We hold a Maryland license, compare plans from many carriers and walk you through them from home, with no pressure to enroll.
Will my Caroline County doctors and hospitals be covered?
On a Medicare Advantage plan it depends on that plan’s network, so before you enroll we check whether your doctors and the hospitals you use are in network, including hospitals in neighboring counties. With a Medicare Supplement (Medigap) plan you can see any doctor who accepts Medicare.
How many people in Caroline County have Medicare Advantage?
CMS reported 1,825 people enrolled in a Medicare Advantage plan out of 7,695 people with Medicare in Caroline County in September 2026, or 23.7%. The Maryland average was 24.8%.
Do you charge Caroline County residents for help?
No. You pay nothing to work with us. Carriers compensate us, and the price of your plan is the same as if you enrolled directly.
When can I enroll or change plans?
You can enroll during your Initial Enrollment Period around your 65th birthday, during the Annual Enrollment Period from October 15 to December 7, or during a Special Enrollment Period if you have a qualifying event such as moving into or within the county.
