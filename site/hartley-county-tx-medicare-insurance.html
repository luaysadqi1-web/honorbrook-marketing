Among Texas’s 254 counties, Hartley County comes in 205th for residents age 65 and up (858). Older adults make up 16.7% of the population here; the state figure is 13.7%. About 267 more people are 60 to 64 and are heading toward Medicare; our turning 65 guide explains their first steps. Census 2023 estimates show 50% of the 65-plus population is under 75, 38% is 75 to 84 and 13% is 85 or older. Medicare Advantage enrollment in Hartley County is 31.0% of people with Medicare, lower than the Texas average of 54.9%. A county average does not tell you which kind of plan fits you. Whether Medicare Advantage or Original Medicare with a Medigap plan is the better fit depends on your doctors, your prescriptions and how you want to handle costs, so we compare both for your ZIP code.

Who has Medicare in Hartley County

The USDA classifies Hartley County as: urban population of fewer than 5,000, not adjacent to a metro area. With fewer hospitals and clinics close by, the right Medicare plan here is often the one whose network includes the providers you can really reach.

CMS counts 637 Medicare beneficiaries in Hartley County (2023). Their average age is 76, older than the Texas average of 73. About 9% are “dual eligible,” meaning they have both Medicare and Medicaid, close to the state figure of 11%.

Among people in Original Medicare in Hartley County, CMS reported about 500 emergency room visits and 141 inpatient hospital stays per 1,000 beneficiaries in 2023, which is lower than the state average for emergency visits (580) and lower than it for hospital stays (226). About 5% received home health care. These figures describe how people use care under Original Medicare; they do not show what any plan covers or costs.

Sources: CMS Medicare Geographic Variation, 2023; USDA Rural-Urban Continuum Codes, 2023.

Hospitals and doctors near Hartley County

CMS Hospital General Information lists no acute care, critical access or children’s hospital located inside Hartley County. Residents often use hospitals in neighboring counties, which matters for Medicare Advantage because a plan’s network may include or leave out the hospital you use. Free Medicare counseling for Hartley County

  • 1-800-MEDICARE (1-800-633-4227) is the federal Medicare helpline.
  • Call HICAP, Texas’s SHIP, for free and unbiased Medicare counseling at 1-800-252-9240.
  • Eldercare Locator connects you to your local Area Agency on Aging: 1-800-677-1116 (eldercare.acl.gov).

These services are worth using. Where we add to them is comparing specific plans and helping you enroll and stay on track year after year.

Common questions

Hartley County Medicare FAQ

Can I get help with Medicare in Hartley County by phone?
Yes. You do not need to travel: we are licensed in Texas and help Hartley County residents by phone or video, comparing plans from many insurers at no cost to you.
Will my Hartley County doctors and hospitals be covered?
On a Medicare Advantage plan it depends on that plan’s network, so before you enroll we check whether your doctors and the hospitals you use are in network, including hospitals in neighboring counties. With a Medicare Supplement (Medigap) plan you can see any doctor who accepts Medicare.
How many people in Hartley County have Medicare Advantage?
CMS reported 206 people enrolled in a Medicare Advantage plan out of 665 people with Medicare in Hartley County in September 2026, or 31.0%. The Texas average was 54.9%.
Do you charge Hartley County residents for help?
No. You pay nothing to work with us. Carriers compensate us, and the price of your plan is the same as if you enrolled directly.
When can I enroll or change plans?
You can enroll during your Initial Enrollment Period around your 65th birthday, during the Annual Enrollment Period from October 15 to December 7, or during a Special Enrollment Period if you have a qualifying event such as moving into or within the county.
