Among Michigan’s 83 counties, Berrien County comes in 12th for residents age 65 and up (33,509). Older adults make up 22.0% of the population here; the state figure is 19.2%. Another 11,287 residents are age 60 to 64 and will reach Medicare eligibility within about five years (see our turning 65 guide). Census 2023 estimates show 59% of the 65-plus population is under 75, 31% is 75 to 84 and 10% is 85 or older. About 57.0% of people with Medicare in Berrien County are enrolled in a Medicare Advantage plan. That is lower than the Michigan average of 63.6%. What most people do in a county is less important than what fits you. We weigh Medicare Advantage against Original Medicare with Medigap using your doctors, your drugs and your budget.

Who has Medicare in Berrien County

The U.S. Department of Agriculture classifies Berrien County as a metro county (counties in metro areas of fewer than 250,000 population). Counties like this tend to have a wider choice of hospitals and Medicare plans than rural ones, though networks still differ plan by plan.

CMS counts 39,739 Medicare beneficiaries in Berrien County (2023). Their average age is 74, older than the Michigan average of 72. About 12% are “dual eligible,” meaning they have both Medicare and Medicaid, lower than the state figure of 20%.

Among people in Original Medicare in Berrien County, CMS reported about 608 emergency room visits and 191 inpatient hospital stays per 1,000 beneficiaries in 2023, which is close to the state average for emergency visits (645) and lower than it for hospital stays (245). About 7% received home health care. These figures describe how people use care under Original Medicare; they do not show what any plan covers or costs.

Sources: CMS Medicare Geographic Variation, 2023; USDA Rural-Urban Continuum Codes, 2023.

Insurers choose which counties to serve each year, which means the Medicare Advantage and Part D plans you can pick in Berrien County can be different from the ones offered next door. We are not based in Berrien County; we help Berrien County residents by phone or video call.

Common questions

Berrien County Medicare FAQ

Can I get help with Medicare in Berrien County by phone?
Yes. You do not need to travel: we are licensed in Michigan and help Berrien County residents by phone or video, comparing plans from many insurers at no cost to you.
Will my Berrien County doctors and hospitals be covered?
On a Medicare Advantage plan it depends on that plan’s network, so before you enroll we check whether your doctors and the hospitals you use, such as Corewell Health Watervliet Hospital, Lakeland Hospital, St. Joseph, are in network. With a Medicare Supplement (Medigap) plan you can see any doctor who accepts Medicare.
How many people in Berrien County have Medicare Advantage?
CMS reported 22,711 people enrolled in a Medicare Advantage plan out of 39,812 people with Medicare in Berrien County in September 2026, or 57.0%. The Michigan average was 63.6%.
Do you charge Berrien County residents for help?
No. You pay nothing to work with us. Carriers compensate us, and the price of your plan is the same as if you enrolled directly.
When can I enroll or change plans?
You can enroll during your Initial Enrollment Period around your 65th birthday, during the Annual Enrollment Period from October 15 to December 7, or during a Special Enrollment Period if you have a qualifying event such as moving into or within the county.
