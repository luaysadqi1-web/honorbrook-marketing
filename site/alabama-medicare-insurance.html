Insurance help for Alabama residents

Choosing coverage shouldn't feel like a maze. Whether you're turning 65, already on Medicare and wondering if you're overpaying, retiring early and need coverage until you qualify, or just want someone to explain your options in plain English, we're here for that. Honorbrook works with Alabama individuals, couples, and families to compare plans honestly and find the fit that actually matches your doctors, prescriptions, and budget.

Our help is always free. Carriers pay us a commission when we place a policy, and by law your premium is exactly the same whether you use an agent or sign up on your own. So you get a side-by-side comparison, plain-English answers, and a real person to call later — at no cost to you. We don't work for any one insurance company, which means our only job is to help you find the right plan, not to sell you a particular brand.

Coverage we offer in Alabama

  • Medicare Advantage — all-in-one Part C plans, many with $0 premiums plus dental, vision, and drug coverage.
  • Medicare Supplement (Medigap) — see any doctor who accepts Medicare, with predictable out-of-pocket costs.
  • Part D drug plans — we find the lowest true annual cost for your exact medication list.

  • group / SHOP coverage — small-business health plans for Alabama teams.

Common questions

Alabama Medicare & insurance FAQ

Do you charge Alabama residents for help?
No — our help is completely free. Carriers pay us a commission when we place a policy, and by law your premium is identical whether you use an agent or enroll on your own. You get our comparison, guidance, and ongoing support at no added cost.
Which Alabama cities do you serve?
All of Alabama, entirely by phone. We regularly help residents in Birmingham, Montgomery, Huntsville, Mobile, Tuscaloosa, and Hoover — and every town in between. No office visit needed.
What plans can I get in Alabama?
Availability varies by county in Alabama. We compare Medicare Advantage, Medigap, and Part D drug plans, plus ICHRA, ACA, and life coverage — and we check exactly what's offered at your specific address before you choose.
Can you help if I'm new to Alabama or moving?
Yes. Moving to, from, or within Alabama can open a Special Enrollment Period that lets you change or pick a new plan outside the usual windows. Call us and we'll walk through your options based on your new address.
