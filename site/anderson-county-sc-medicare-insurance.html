With roughly 39,704 residents age 65 and older, Anderson County is 9th among 46 South Carolina counties for older residents. That group is 18.6% of its population, compared with 19.3% across South Carolina. About 14,377 more people are 60 to 64 and are heading toward Medicare; our turning 65 guide explains their first steps. Among residents 65 and older, 58% are 65 to 74, 33% are 75 to 84 and 9% are 85 or older (Census 2023 estimates). CMS data show 53.7% of Anderson County residents with Medicare chose Medicare Advantage, which is higher than the 46.7% average across South Carolina. A county average does not tell you which kind of plan fits you. Whether Medicare Advantage or Original Medicare with a Medigap plan is the better fit depends on your doctors, your prescriptions and how you want to handle costs, so we compare both for your ZIP code.

Who has Medicare in Anderson County

Anderson County is a metro county; the U.S. Department of Agriculture describes it as: counties in metro areas of 250,000 to 1 million population. Metro counties usually have more hospitals and more insurers competing for Medicare members, so there can be more plans to compare.

CMS counts 49,764 Medicare beneficiaries in Anderson County (2023). Their average age is 73, about the same as the South Carolina average of 73. About 7% are “dual eligible,” meaning they have both Medicare and Medicaid, close to the state figure of 7%.

Among people in Original Medicare in Anderson County, CMS reported about 485 emergency room visits and 224 inpatient hospital stays per 1,000 beneficiaries in 2023, which is lower than the state average for emergency visits (563) and close to it for hospital stays (202). About 9% received home health care. These figures describe how people use care under Original Medicare; they do not show what any plan covers or costs.

Sources: CMS Medicare Geographic Variation, 2023; USDA Rural-Urban Continuum Codes, 2023.

Common questions

Anderson County Medicare FAQ

Can I get help with Medicare in Anderson County by phone?
Yes. We are an independent agency licensed in South Carolina, and we help Anderson County residents by phone or video call, so you can compare plans from home. There is no pressure to enroll.
Will my Anderson County doctors and hospitals be covered?
On a Medicare Advantage plan it depends on that plan’s network, so before you enroll we check whether your doctors and the hospitals you use, such as Anmed Health, are in network. With a Medicare Supplement (Medigap) plan you can see any doctor who accepts Medicare.
How many people in Anderson County have Medicare Advantage?
CMS reported 27,366 people enrolled in a Medicare Advantage plan out of 50,965 people with Medicare in Anderson County in September 2026, or 53.7%. The South Carolina average was 46.7%.
Do you charge Anderson County residents for help?
No, there is no fee. The insurance company pays us when you enroll, and your premium does not go up because you used an agent.
When can I enroll or change plans?
You can enroll during your Initial Enrollment Period around your 65th birthday, during the Annual Enrollment Period from October 15 to December 7, or during a Special Enrollment Period if you have a qualifying event such as moving into or within the county.
