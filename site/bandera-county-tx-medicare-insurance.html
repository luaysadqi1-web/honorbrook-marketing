Bandera County ranks 90th of 254 Texas counties by number of residents age 65 and older, with about 6,726 people in that age group, or 29.7% of residents (statewide: 13.7%). Another 2,220 residents are age 60 to 64 and will reach Medicare eligibility within about five years (see our turning 65 guide). Among residents 65 and older, 59% are 65 to 74, 32% are 75 to 84 and 8% are 85 or older (Census 2023 estimates). About 48.8% of people with Medicare in Bandera County are enrolled in a Medicare Advantage plan. That is lower than the Texas average of 54.9%. A county average does not tell you which kind of plan fits you. Whether Medicare Advantage or Original Medicare with a Medigap plan is the better fit depends on your doctors, your prescriptions and how you want to handle costs, so we compare both for your ZIP code.

Where you live decides which Medicare Advantage and Part D plans you can join, and Bandera County may have a different set of plans than the counties around it. We are not based in Bandera County; we help Bandera County residents by phone or video call.

Hospitals and doctors near Bandera County

CMS Hospital General Information lists no acute care, critical access or children’s hospital located inside Bandera County. Residents often use hospitals in neighboring counties, which matters for Medicare Advantage because a plan’s network may include or leave out the hospital you use. We check your hospitals and doctors before you enroll.

Free help available in Bandera County

  • HICAP, the Texas state health insurance assistance program (SHIP), offers free, unbiased Medicare counseling: 1-800-252-9240.
  • Eldercare Locator connects you to your local Area Agency on Aging: 1-800-677-1116 (eldercare.acl.gov).
  • 1-800-MEDICARE (1-800-633-4227) is the federal Medicare helpline.

Start with any of these if you like. We pick up where they leave off: comparing the actual plans for your doctors and drugs, and staying with you each year at renewal.

Common questions

Bandera County Medicare FAQ

Can I get help with Medicare in Bandera County by phone?
Yes. You do not need to travel: we are licensed in Texas and help Bandera County residents by phone or video, comparing plans from many insurers at no cost to you.
Will my Bandera County doctors and hospitals be covered?
On a Medicare Advantage plan it depends on that plan’s network, so before you enroll we check whether your doctors and the hospitals you use are in network, including hospitals in neighboring counties. With a Medicare Supplement (Medigap) plan you can see any doctor who accepts Medicare.
How many people in Bandera County have Medicare Advantage?
CMS reported 4,051 people enrolled in a Medicare Advantage plan out of 8,304 people with Medicare in Bandera County in September 2026, or 48.8%. The Texas average was 54.9%.
Do you charge Bandera County residents for help?
No, there is no fee. The insurance company pays us when you enroll, and your premium does not go up because you used an agent.
When can I enroll or change plans?
You can enroll during your Initial Enrollment Period around your 65th birthday, during the Annual Enrollment Period from October 15 to December 7, or during a Special Enrollment Period if you have a qualifying event such as moving into or within the county.
