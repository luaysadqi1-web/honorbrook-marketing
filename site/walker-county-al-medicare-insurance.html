Walker County ranks 21st of 67 Alabama counties by number of residents age 65 and older, with about 12,984 people in that age group, or 20.1% of residents (statewide: 18.3%). Another 4,448 residents are age 60 to 64 and will reach Medicare eligibility within about five years (see our turning 65 guide). By age, the 65-and-older group splits 58% ages 65 to 74, 33% ages 75 to 84 and 9% age 85 and up (Census 2023). Medicare Advantage enrollment in Walker County is 68.0% of people with Medicare, higher than the Alabama average of 61.5%. A county average does not tell you which kind of plan fits you. Whether Medicare Advantage or Original Medicare with a Medigap plan is the better fit depends on your doctors, your prescriptions and how you want to handle costs, so we compare both for your ZIP code.

Who has Medicare in Walker County

Walker County sits in a metro area, which the USDA defines as counties in metro areas of 1 million population or more. That usually means more doctors, more hospitals and more plan choices in the area.

CMS counts 18,012 Medicare beneficiaries in Walker County (2023). Their average age is 72, about the same as the Alabama average of 72. About 15% are “dual eligible,” meaning they have both Medicare and Medicaid, close to the state figure of 14%.

Among people in Original Medicare in Walker County, CMS reported about 598 emergency room visits and 271 inpatient hospital stays per 1,000 beneficiaries in 2023, which is close to the state average for emergency visits (600) and close to it for hospital stays (247). About 11% received home health care. These figures describe how people use care under Original Medicare; they do not show what any plan covers or costs.

Sources: CMS Medicare Geographic Variation, 2023; USDA Rural-Urban Continuum Codes, 2023.

Common questions

Walker County Medicare FAQ

Can I get help with Medicare in Walker County by phone?
Yes. We are an independent agency licensed in Alabama, and we help Walker County residents by phone or video call, so you can compare plans from home. There is no pressure to enroll.
Will my Walker County doctors and hospitals be covered?
On a Medicare Advantage plan it depends on that plan’s network, so before you enroll we check whether your doctors and the hospitals you use, such as Walker Baptist Medical Center, are in network. With a Medicare Supplement (Medigap) plan you can see any doctor who accepts Medicare.
How many people in Walker County have Medicare Advantage?
CMS reported 11,553 people enrolled in a Medicare Advantage plan out of 16,996 people with Medicare in Walker County in September 2026, or 68.0%. The Alabama average was 61.5%.
Do you charge Walker County residents for help?
No, there is no fee. The insurance company pays us when you enroll, and your premium does not go up because you used an agent.
When can I enroll or change plans?
You can enroll during your Initial Enrollment Period around your 65th birthday, during the Annual Enrollment Period from October 15 to December 7, or during a Special Enrollment Period if you have a qualifying event such as moving into or within the county.
