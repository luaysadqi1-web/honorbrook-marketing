Among Louisiana’s 64 parishes, Acadia Parish comes in 19th for residents age 65 and up (9,578). Older adults make up 17.0% of the population here; the state figure is 17.4%. About 3,724 more people are 60 to 64 and are heading toward Medicare; our turning 65 guide explains their first steps. By age, the 65-and-older group splits 62% ages 65 to 74, 28% ages 75 to 84 and 10% age 85 and up (Census 2023). About 48.7% of people with Medicare in Acadia Parish are enrolled in a Medicare Advantage plan. That is lower than the Louisiana average of 57.5%. A county average does not tell you which kind of plan fits you. Whether Medicare Advantage or Original Medicare with a Medigap plan is the better fit depends on your doctors, your prescriptions and how you want to handle costs, so we compare both for your ZIP code.

Who has Medicare in Acadia Parish

Acadia Parish is a metro county; the U.S. Department of Agriculture describes it as: counties in metro areas of 250,000 to 1 million population. Metro counties usually have more hospitals and more insurers competing for Medicare members, so there can be more plans to compare.

CMS counts 13,173 Medicare beneficiaries in Acadia Parish (2023). Their average age is 73, about the same as the Louisiana average of 72. About 24% are “dual eligible,” meaning they have both Medicare and Medicaid, close to the state figure of 20%.

Among people in Original Medicare in Acadia Parish, CMS reported about 670 emergency room visits and 253 inpatient hospital stays per 1,000 beneficiaries in 2023, which is close to the state average for emergency visits (648) and close to it for hospital stays (237). About 11% received home health care. These figures describe how people use care under Original Medicare; they do not show what any plan covers or costs.

Sources: CMS Medicare Geographic Variation, 2023; USDA Rural-Urban Continuum Codes, 2023.

Common questions

Acadia Parish Medicare FAQ

Can I get help with Medicare in Acadia Parish by phone?
Yes. We are an independent agency licensed in Louisiana, and we help Acadia Parish residents by phone or video call, so you can compare plans from home. There is no pressure to enroll.
Will my Acadia Parish doctors and hospitals be covered?
On a Medicare Advantage plan it depends on that plan’s network, so before you enroll we check whether your doctors and the hospitals you use, such as Acadia General Hospital, Acadia St. Landry, are in network. With a Medicare Supplement (Medigap) plan you can see any doctor who accepts Medicare.
How many people in Acadia Parish have Medicare Advantage?
CMS reported 6,159 people enrolled in a Medicare Advantage plan out of 12,645 people with Medicare in Acadia Parish in September 2026, or 48.7%. The Louisiana average was 57.5%.
Do you charge Acadia Parish residents for help?
No. You pay nothing to work with us. Carriers compensate us, and the price of your plan is the same as if you enrolled directly.
When can I enroll or change plans?
You can enroll during your Initial Enrollment Period around your 65th birthday, during the Annual Enrollment Period from October 15 to December 7, or during a Special Enrollment Period if you have a qualifying event such as moving into or within the county.
