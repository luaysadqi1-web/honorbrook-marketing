What most people do in a county is less important than what fits you. We weigh Medicare Advantage against Original Medicare with Medigap using your doctors, your drugs and your budget.

Who has Medicare in Putnam County

Putnam County is a metro county; the U.S. Department of Agriculture describes it as: counties in metro areas of 250,000 to 1 million population. Metro counties usually have more hospitals and more insurers competing for Medicare members, so there can be more plans to compare.

CMS counts 13,948 Medicare beneficiaries in Putnam County (2023). Their average age is 72, about the same as the West Virginia average of 72. About 16% are “dual eligible,” meaning they have both Medicare and Medicaid, lower than the state figure of 20%.

Among people in Original Medicare in Putnam County, CMS reported about 629 emergency room visits and 224 inpatient hospital stays per 1,000 beneficiaries in 2023, which is close to the state average for emergency visits (684) and close to it for hospital stays (250). About 8% received home health care. These figures describe how people use care under Original Medicare; they do not show what any plan covers or costs.

Sources: CMS Medicare Geographic Variation, 2023; USDA Rural-Urban Continuum Codes, 2023.

Common questions

Putnam County Medicare FAQ

Can I get help with Medicare in Putnam County by phone?
Yes. We are an independent agency licensed in West Virginia, and we help Putnam County residents by phone or video call, so you can compare plans from home. There is no pressure to enroll.
Will my Putnam County doctors and hospitals be covered?
On a Medicare Advantage plan it depends on that plan’s network, so before you enroll we check whether your doctors and the hospitals you use are in network, including hospitals in neighboring counties. With a Medicare Supplement (Medigap) plan you can see any doctor who accepts Medicare.
How many people in Putnam County have Medicare Advantage?
CMS reported 8,261 people enrolled in a Medicare Advantage plan out of 14,048 people with Medicare in Putnam County in September 2026, or 58.8%. The West Virginia average was 56.6%.
Do you charge Putnam County residents for help?
No, there is no fee. The insurance company pays us when you enroll, and your premium does not go up because you used an agent.
When can I enroll or change plans?
You can enroll during your Initial Enrollment Period around your 65th birthday, during the Annual Enrollment Period from October 15 to December 7, or during a Special Enrollment Period if you have a qualifying event such as moving into or within the county.
