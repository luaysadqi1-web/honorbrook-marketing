Common questions

Fairfax Medicare FAQ

Can I meet with a Fairfax Medicare agent in person?
Yes. We meet Fairfax residents by appointment — our office is nearby in Vienna, just minutes away, or we can come to you or handle everything by phone, whichever is easiest for you.
Will my Fairfax-area doctors and hospitals be covered?
On a Medicare Advantage plan it depends on that plan's provider network, which can change each year. Before you enroll, we verify your specific doctors and facilities — including Inova Fair Oaks Hospital — are in network. With Medicare Supplement (Medigap), you can see any doctor or hospital that accepts Medicare.
Do you charge Fairfax residents for help?
No. Our help is completely free. Carriers pay us a commission when we place a policy, and by law your premium is the same whether you use an agent or enroll on your own.
When can I enroll or change plans?
During your Initial Enrollment Period around your 65th birthday, during the Annual Enrollment Period (Oct 15 – Dec 7), or during a Special Enrollment Period triggered by a qualifying life event such as moving to or within Fairfax.
