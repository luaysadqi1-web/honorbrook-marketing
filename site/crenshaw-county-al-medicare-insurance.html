Crenshaw County ranks 61st of 67 Alabama counties by number of residents age 65 and older, with about 2,633 people in that age group, or 20.1% of residents (statewide: 18.3%). Another 931 residents are age 60 to 64 and will reach Medicare eligibility within about five years (see our turning 65 guide). Among residents 65 and older, 59% are 65 to 74, 32% are 75 to 84 and 10% are 85 or older (Census 2023 estimates). About 65.2% of people with Medicare in Crenshaw County are enrolled in a Medicare Advantage plan. That is close to the Alabama average of 61.5%. A county average does not tell you which kind of plan fits you. Whether Medicare Advantage or Original Medicare with a Medigap plan is the better fit depends on your doctors, your prescriptions and how you want to handle costs, so we compare both for your ZIP code.

Insurers choose which counties to serve each year, which means the Medicare Advantage and Part D plans you can pick in Crenshaw County can be different from the ones offered next door. We are not based in Crenshaw County; we help Crenshaw County residents by phone or video call.

Free help available in Crenshaw County

  • Alabama SHIP, the Alabama state health insurance assistance program (SHIP), offers free, unbiased Medicare counseling: 1-800-243-5463.
  • Eldercare Locator connects you to your local Area Agency on Aging: 1-800-677-1116 (eldercare.acl.gov).
  • 1-800-MEDICARE (1-800-633-4227) is the federal Medicare helpline.

Start with any of these if you like. We pick up where they leave off: comparing the actual plans for your doctors and drugs, and staying with you each year at renewal.

Common questions

Crenshaw County Medicare FAQ

Can I get help with Medicare in Crenshaw County by phone?
Yes. You do not need to travel: we are licensed in Alabama and help Crenshaw County residents by phone or video, comparing plans from many insurers at no cost to you.
Will my Crenshaw County doctors and hospitals be covered?
On a Medicare Advantage plan it depends on that plan’s network, so before you enroll we check whether your doctors and the hospitals you use, such as Crenshaw Community Hospital, are in network. With a Medicare Supplement (Medigap) plan you can see any doctor who accepts Medicare.
How many people in Crenshaw County have Medicare Advantage?
CMS reported 2,476 people enrolled in a Medicare Advantage plan out of 3,795 people with Medicare in Crenshaw County in September 2026, or 65.2%. The Alabama average was 61.5%.
Do you charge Crenshaw County residents for help?
No. You pay nothing to work with us. Carriers compensate us, and the price of your plan is the same as if you enrolled directly.
When can I enroll or change plans?
You can enroll during your Initial Enrollment Period around your 65th birthday, during the Annual Enrollment Period from October 15 to December 7, or during a Special Enrollment Period if you have a qualifying event such as moving into or within the county.
