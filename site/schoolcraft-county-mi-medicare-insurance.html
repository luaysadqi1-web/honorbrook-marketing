Among Michigan’s 83 counties, Schoolcraft County comes in 79th for residents age 65 and up (2,386). Older adults make up 29.3% of the population here; the state figure is 19.2%. Medicare Advantage and Original Medicare with a Medigap plan each suit different people, depending on doctors, medicines and how you prefer to handle costs, and we compare both at your ZIP code.

Who has Medicare in Schoolcraft County

The USDA classifies Schoolcraft County as: urban population of fewer than 5,000, not adjacent to a metro area. With fewer hospitals and clinics close by, the right Medicare plan here is often the one whose network includes the providers you can really reach.

CMS counts 2,958 Medicare beneficiaries in Schoolcraft County (2023). Their average age is 72, about the same as the Michigan average of 72. About 13% are “dual eligible,” meaning they have both Medicare and Medicaid, lower than the state figure of 20%.

Among people in Original Medicare in Schoolcraft County, CMS reported about 522 emergency room visits and 141 inpatient hospital stays per 1,000 beneficiaries in 2023, which is lower than the state average for emergency visits (645) and lower than it for hospital stays (245). About 7% received home health care. These figures describe how people use care under Original Medicare; they do not show what any plan covers or costs.

Sources: CMS Medicare Geographic Variation, 2023; USDA Rural-Urban Continuum Codes, 2023.

Common questions

Schoolcraft County Medicare FAQ

Can I get help with Medicare in Schoolcraft County by phone?
Yes. You do not need to travel: we are licensed in Michigan and help Schoolcraft County residents by phone or video, comparing plans from many insurers at no cost to you.
Will my Schoolcraft County doctors and hospitals be covered?
On a Medicare Advantage plan it depends on that plan’s network, so before you enroll we check whether your doctors and the hospitals you use, such as Schoolcraft Memorial Hospital, are in network. With a Medicare Supplement (Medigap) plan you can see any doctor who accepts Medicare.
How many people in Schoolcraft County have Medicare Advantage?
CMS reported 1,631 people enrolled in a Medicare Advantage plan out of 2,808 people with Medicare in Schoolcraft County in September 2026, or 58.1%. The Michigan average was 63.6%.
Do you charge Schoolcraft County residents for help?
No, there is no fee. The insurance company pays us when you enroll, and your premium does not go up because you used an agent.
When can I enroll or change plans?
You can enroll during your Initial Enrollment Period around your 65th birthday, during the Annual Enrollment Period from October 15 to December 7, or during a Special Enrollment Period if you have a qualifying event such as moving into or within the county.
