With roughly 1,450 residents age 65 and older, Pleasants County is 53rd among 55 West Virginia counties for older residents. That group is 19.5% of its population, compared with 21.5% across West Virginia. About 545 more people are 60 to 64 and are heading toward Medicare; our turning 65 guide explains their first steps. Among residents 65 and older, 58% are 65 to 74, 34% are 75 to 84 and 8% are 85 or older (Census 2023 estimates). Medicare Advantage enrollment in Pleasants County is 56.4% of people with Medicare, close to the West Virginia average of 56.6%. Averages cannot choose a plan for you. Medicare Advantage and Original Medicare with a Medigap plan each suit different people, depending on doctors, medicines and how you prefer to handle costs, and we compare both at your ZIP code.

Who has Medicare in Pleasants County

Pleasants County is a rural county (USDA: urban population of fewer than 5,000, adjacent to a metro area). Plan choices can be narrower than in a city, and we focus on networks that include the doctors and hospital you actually use.

CMS counts 1,961 Medicare beneficiaries in Pleasants County (2023). Their average age is 71, about the same as the West Virginia average of 72. About 20% are “dual eligible,” meaning they have both Medicare and Medicaid, close to the state figure of 20%.

Among people in Original Medicare in Pleasants County, CMS reported about 624 emergency room visits and 275 inpatient hospital stays per 1,000 beneficiaries in 2023, which is close to the state average for emergency visits (684) and close to it for hospital stays (250). About 8% received home health care. These figures describe how people use care under Original Medicare; they do not show what any plan covers or costs.

Sources: CMS Medicare Geographic Variation, 2023; USDA Rural-Urban Continuum Codes, 2023.

Common questions

Pleasants County Medicare FAQ

Can I get help with Medicare in Pleasants County by phone?
Yes. You do not need to travel: we are licensed in West Virginia and help Pleasants County residents by phone or video, comparing plans from many insurers at no cost to you.
Will my Pleasants County doctors and hospitals be covered?
On a Medicare Advantage plan it depends on that plan’s network, so before you enroll we check whether your doctors and the hospitals you use are in network, including hospitals in neighboring counties. With a Medicare Supplement (Medigap) plan you can see any doctor who accepts Medicare.
How many people in Pleasants County have Medicare Advantage?
CMS reported 1,066 people enrolled in a Medicare Advantage plan out of 1,891 people with Medicare in Pleasants County in September 2026, or 56.4%. The West Virginia average was 56.6%.
Do you charge Pleasants County residents for help?
No. Our help is free to you. Carriers pay us a commission when we place a policy, and your premium is the same whether you use an agent or enroll on your own.
When can I enroll or change plans?
You can enroll during your Initial Enrollment Period around your 65th birthday, during the Annual Enrollment Period from October 15 to December 7, or during a Special Enrollment Period if you have a qualifying event such as moving into or within the county.
