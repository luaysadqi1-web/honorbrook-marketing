Henry County ranks 45th of 67 Alabama counties by number of residents age 65 and older, with about 4,329 people in that age group, or 24.2% of residents (statewide: 18.3%). About 1,188 more people are 60 to 64 and are heading toward Medicare; our turning 65 guide explains their first steps. Among residents 65 and older, 58% are 65 to 74, 32% are 75 to 84 and 10% are 85 or older (Census 2023 estimates). Medicare Advantage enrollment in Henry County is 66.3% of people with Medicare, close to the Alabama average of 61.5%. Averages cannot choose a plan for you. Medicare Advantage and Original Medicare with a Medigap plan each suit different people, depending on doctors, medicines and how you prefer to handle costs, and we compare both at your ZIP code.

Who has Medicare in Henry County

The U.S. Department of Agriculture classifies Henry County as a metro county (counties in metro areas of fewer than 250,000 population). Counties like this tend to have a wider choice of hospitals and Medicare plans than rural ones, though networks still differ plan by plan.

CMS counts 5,341 Medicare beneficiaries in Henry County (2023). Their average age is 73, about the same as the Alabama average of 72. About 15% are “dual eligible,” meaning they have both Medicare and Medicaid, close to the state figure of 14%.

Among people in Original Medicare in Henry County, CMS reported about 542 emergency room visits and 235 inpatient hospital stays per 1,000 beneficiaries in 2023, which is close to the state average for emergency visits (600) and close to it for hospital stays (247). About 11% received home health care. These figures describe how people use care under Original Medicare; they do not show what any plan covers or costs.

Sources: CMS Medicare Geographic Variation, 2023; USDA Rural-Urban Continuum Codes, 2023.

Where you live decides which Medicare Advantage and Part D plans you can join, and Henry County may have a different set of plans than the counties around it. We are not based in Henry County; we help Henry County residents by phone or video call.

Hospitals and doctors near Henry County

CMS Hospital General Information lists no acute care, critical access or children’s hospital located inside Henry County. Residents often use hospitals in neighboring counties, which matters for Medicare Advantage because a plan’s network may include or leave out the hospital you use. We check your hospitals and doctors before you enroll.

Where people live in Henry County

Census Bureau 2023 estimates put the biggest communities in Henry County at Headland (5,333), Abbeville (2,377), Newville (558), Haleburg (116), Dothan (0), counting only the part of each community inside the county.

We look up plans by ZIP code. In Henry County, that means areas such as 36310, 36317, 36319, 36345, 36353, 36373, based on Census ZIP Code Tabulation Areas that lie mostly in the county.

Common questions

Henry County Medicare FAQ

Can I get help with Medicare in Henry County by phone?
Yes. You do not need to travel: we are licensed in Alabama and help Henry County residents by phone or video, comparing plans from many insurers at no cost to you.
Will my Henry County doctors and hospitals be covered?
On a Medicare Advantage plan it depends on that plan’s network, so before you enroll we check whether your doctors and the hospitals you use are in network, including hospitals in neighboring counties. With a Medicare Supplement (Medigap) plan you can see any doctor who accepts Medicare.
How many people in Henry County have Medicare Advantage?
CMS reported 3,473 people enrolled in a Medicare Advantage plan out of 5,239 people with Medicare in Henry County in September 2026, or 66.3%. The Alabama average was 61.5%.
Do you charge Henry County residents for help?
No. You pay nothing to work with us. Carriers compensate us, and the price of your plan is the same as if you enrolled directly.
When can I enroll or change plans?
You can enroll during your Initial Enrollment Period around your 65th birthday, during the Annual Enrollment Period from October 15 to December 7, or during a Special Enrollment Period if you have a qualifying event such as moving into or within the county.
