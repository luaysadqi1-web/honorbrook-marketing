Jasper County ranks 48th of 92 Indiana counties by number of residents age 65 and older, with about 6,446 people in that age group, or 19.2% of residents (statewide: 17.2%). About 2,440 more people are 60 to 64 and are heading toward Medicare; our turning 65 guide explains their first steps. Among residents 65 and older, 59% are 65 to 74, 31% are 75 to 84 and 9% are 85 or older (Census 2023 estimates). About 39.0% of people with Medicare in Jasper County are enrolled in a Medicare Advantage plan. That is lower than the Indiana average of 51.1%. A county average does not tell you which kind of plan fits you. Whether Medicare Advantage or Original Medicare with a Medigap plan is the better fit depends on your doctors, your prescriptions and how you want to handle costs, so we compare both for your ZIP code.

Who has Medicare in Jasper County

Jasper County sits in a metro area, which the USDA defines as counties in metro areas of 1 million population or more. That usually means more doctors, more hospitals and more plan choices in the area.

CMS counts 7,861 Medicare beneficiaries in Jasper County (2023). Their average age is 73, about the same as the Indiana average of 72. About 12% are “dual eligible,” meaning they have both Medicare and Medicaid, lower than the state figure of 16%.

Among people in Original Medicare in Jasper County, CMS reported about 564 emergency room visits and 238 inpatient hospital stays per 1,000 beneficiaries in 2023, which is close to the state average for emergency visits (619) and close to it for hospital stays (235). About 5% received home health care. These figures describe how people use care under Original Medicare; they do not show what any plan covers or costs.

Sources: CMS Medicare Geographic Variation, 2023; USDA Rural-Urban Continuum Codes, 2023.

Where to get free Medicare help in Jasper County

  • Indiana’s SHIP program, Indiana SHIP, gives free Medicare counseling that is not tied to any insurer: 1-800-452-4800.
  • The Eldercare Locator, 1-800-677-1116, finds the Area Agency on Aging that serves your address (eldercare.acl.gov).
  • 1-800-MEDICARE (1-800-633-4227) is the federal Medicare helpline.

Start with any of these if you like. We pick up where they leave off: comparing the actual plans for your doctors and drugs, and staying with you each year at renewal.

Common questions

Jasper County Medicare FAQ

Can I get help with Medicare in Jasper County by phone?
Yes. You do not need to travel: we are licensed in Indiana and help Jasper County residents by phone or video, comparing plans from many insurers at no cost to you.
Will my Jasper County doctors and hospitals be covered?
On a Medicare Advantage plan it depends on that plan’s network, so before you enroll we check whether your doctors and the hospitals you use, such as Franciscan Health Rensselaer, are in network. With a Medicare Supplement (Medigap) plan you can see any doctor who accepts Medicare.
How many people in Jasper County have Medicare Advantage?
CMS reported 3,196 people enrolled in a Medicare Advantage plan out of 8,189 people with Medicare in Jasper County in September 2026, or 39.0%. The Indiana average was 51.1%.
Do you charge Jasper County residents for help?
No, there is no fee. The insurance company pays us when you enroll, and your premium does not go up because you used an agent.
When can I enroll or change plans?
You can enroll during your Initial Enrollment Period around your 65th birthday, during the Annual Enrollment Period from October 15 to December 7, or during a Special Enrollment Period if you have a qualifying event such as moving into or within the county.
