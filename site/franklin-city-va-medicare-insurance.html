Among Virginia’s 133 counties, City of Franklin comes in 124th for residents age 65 and up (1,573). Older adults make up 18.9% of the population here; the state figure is 17.2%. Another 570 residents are age 60 to 64 and will reach Medicare eligibility within about five years (see our turning 65 guide). By age, the 65-and-older group splits 56% ages 65 to 74, 33% ages 75 to 84 and 10% age 85 and up (Census 2023). Medicare Advantage enrollment in City of Franklin is 59.1% of people with Medicare, higher than the Virginia average of 39.6%. A county average does not tell you which kind of plan fits you. Whether Medicare Advantage or Original Medicare with a Medigap plan is the better fit depends on your doctors, your prescriptions and how you want to handle costs, so we compare both for your ZIP code.

Who has Medicare in City of Franklin

City of Franklin is a non-metro county with towns and small cities (USDA: urban population of 5,000 to 20,000, adjacent to a metro area). Care is often split between local providers and a bigger hospital elsewhere, which is worth testing against each plan’s network.

CMS counts 2,410 Medicare beneficiaries in City of Franklin (2023). Their average age is 74, about the same as the Virginia average of 74. About 12% are “dual eligible,” meaning they have both Medicare and Medicaid, higher than the state figure of 9%. Where many people have both, special plans for dual-eligible members can matter; see our Medicaid and dual eligible guide, since availability varies by plan and area.

Among people in Original Medicare in City of Franklin, CMS reported about 799 emergency room visits and 252 inpatient hospital stays per 1,000 beneficiaries in 2023, which is higher than the state average for emergency visits (589) and higher than it for hospital stays (206). About 8% received home health care. These figures describe how people use care under Original Medicare; they do not show what any plan covers or costs.

City of Franklin Medicare FAQ

Can I get help with Medicare in City of Franklin by phone?
Yes. We are an independent agency licensed in Virginia, and we help City of Franklin residents by phone or video call, so you can compare plans from home. There is no pressure to enroll.
Will my City of Franklin doctors and hospitals be covered?
On a Medicare Advantage plan it depends on that plan’s network, so before you enroll we check whether your doctors and the hospitals you use, such as Bon Secours Southampton Memorial Hospital, are in network. With a Medicare Supplement (Medigap) plan you can see any doctor who accepts Medicare.
How many people in City of Franklin have Medicare Advantage?
CMS reported 1,295 people enrolled in a Medicare Advantage plan out of 2,190 people with Medicare in City of Franklin in September 2026, or 59.1%. The Virginia average was 39.6%.
Do you charge City of Franklin residents for help?
No, there is no fee. The insurance company pays us when you enroll, and your premium does not go up because you used an agent.
When can I enroll or change plans?
You can enroll during your Initial Enrollment Period around your 65th birthday, during the Annual Enrollment Period from October 15 to December 7, or during a Special Enrollment Period if you have a qualifying event such as moving into or within the county.
