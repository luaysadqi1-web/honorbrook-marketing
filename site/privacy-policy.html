Privacy Policy

How Honorbrook Insurance collects, uses, and protects your information.

Last updated: June 18, 2026

Honorbrook Insurance ("Honorbrook," "we," "us," or "our"), a registered trade name of Luay's Lifeline Inc., respects your privacy. This policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and the choices you have. By using this website or submitting a form, you agree to this policy.

2. Information we collect

  • Information you give us through our forms or by phone/email: your name, phone number, email address, ZIP code, date of birth or age, and details you choose to share about your coverage needs, doctors, medications, and general health (for example, when you ask us to compare plans).
  • Information collected automatically when you visit: IP address, browser type, pages viewed, and similar analytics data via cookies and tools such as Google Analytics.

We do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 18.

3. How we use your information

  • To respond to your request, compare plans, and provide quotes and guidance.
  • To contact you about coverage by phone, text (SMS), or email — see Section 4.
  • To submit applications to, and coordinate enrollment with, the insurance carriers you choose.
  • To meet legal, regulatory, and carrier compliance obligations, including applicable Medicare (CMS) marketing rules.

4. Calls, texts, and your consent (TCPA)

When you provide your phone number and submit a form or otherwise ask us to contact you, you authorize Honorbrook Insurance to call and/or text you at that number regarding insurance products and services, including through automated dialing technology or prerecorded messages. Consent is not a condition of purchase. Message and data rates may apply. You can opt out of texts at any time by replying STOP, and you may ask to be placed on our internal Do-Not-Call list by calling (571) 354-0146.

5. How we share information

We do not sell your personal information. We share it only:

  • With insurance carriers and their underwriting/enrollment systems, as needed to provide quotes and process the coverage you request.
  • With service providers that help us operate (for example, hosting, scheduling, or CRM tools) under confidentiality obligations.
  • When required by law, regulation, subpoena, or to protect our legal rights.

6. Medicare disclaimer

We do not offer every plan available in your area. Any information we provide is limited to those plans we do offer in your area. Please contact Medicare.gov or 1-800-MEDICARE to get information on all of your options. Honorbrook Insurance is not connected with or endorsed by the United States government or the federal Medicare program.

7. Cookies & analytics

We use cookies and analytics to understand site usage and improve our service. You can disable cookies in your browser settings; some features may not work as well.

8. Data security & retention

We use reasonable administrative and technical safeguards to protect your information and retain it only as long as needed for the purposes above or as required by law and carrier recordkeeping rules. No method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure.

9. Your privacy rights

Depending on where you live (for example, under the Virginia Consumer Data Protection Act), you may have the right to access, correct, or delete your personal information, or to opt out of certain processing. To make a request, contact us at info@honorbrook-insurance.com or (571) 354-0146. We will verify your identity before acting on your request.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date above reflects the latest version.

11. Contact us

Questions about this policy? Email info@honorbrook-insurance.com or call (571) 354-0146.

Note: This privacy policy is a strong starting template, not legal advice. Have an attorney familiar with TCPA and CMS Medicare marketing rules review it before relying on it.
