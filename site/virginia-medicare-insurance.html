Insurance help for Virginia residents

If you live in Virginia and you're trying to make sense of Medicare or health coverage, you don't have to figure it out alone. Honorbrook is a local, independent agency based in Vienna — Virginia is our home state — and we help people across the Commonwealth understand their options and choose a plan that actually fits their life. Whether you're turning 65, already on Medicare and unhappy with your current plan, or shopping for coverage before age 65, we'll walk through it with you in plain English.

Our help is free. We're paid a commission by the insurance carriers when we place a policy, so you never pay us a fee — and by law your premium is exactly the same whether you work with us or enroll on your own. That means you get an experienced advocate in your corner at no extra cost, and no pressure to choose anything you're not comfortable with.

Common questions

Virginia Medicare & insurance FAQ

Do you charge Virginia residents for help?
No — our help is completely free. Carriers pay us a commission when we place a policy, and by law your premium is identical whether you use an agent or enroll on your own. You get expert, independent guidance at no added cost.
Which Virginia cities do you serve?
All of Virginia, handled entirely by phone — including Vienna, Tysons, McLean, Fairfax, Arlington, Alexandria, Richmond, Virginia Beach, Norfolk, and Roanoke. No office visit is needed; we help wherever you live in the state.
What plans can I get in Virginia?
It depends on where you live — plan availability and pricing vary by county in Virginia. We compare what's actually offered at your exact address, including Medicare Advantage, Medigap, Part D, ACA, ICHRA, and life coverage, so you see real options rather than a generic list.
Can you help if I'm new to Virginia or moving?
Yes. Moving into Virginia, out of Virginia, or to a new county can open a Special Enrollment Period that lets you change plans outside the usual windows. We'll confirm your eligibility and compare the plans available at your new address.
