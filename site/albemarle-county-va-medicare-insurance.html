Among Virginia’s 133 counties, Albemarle County comes in 12th for residents age 65 and up (24,602). Older adults make up 21.3% of the population here; the state figure is 17.2%. About 7,261 more people are 60 to 64 and are heading toward Medicare; our turning 65 guide explains their first steps. Medicare Advantage enrollment in Albemarle County is 22.4% of people with Medicare, lower than the Virginia average of 39.6%. Averages cannot choose a plan for you. Medicare Advantage and Original Medicare with a Medigap plan each suit different people, depending on doctors, medicines and how you prefer to handle costs, and we compare both at your ZIP code.

Who has Medicare in Albemarle County

Albemarle County is a metro county; the U.S. Department of Agriculture describes it as: counties in metro areas of fewer than 250,000 population. Metro counties usually have more hospitals and more insurers competing for Medicare members, so there can be more plans to compare.

CMS counts 25,154 Medicare beneficiaries in Albemarle County (2023). Their average age is 75, about the same as the Virginia average of 74. About 5% are “dual eligible,” meaning they have both Medicare and Medicaid, lower than the state figure of 9%.

Among people in Original Medicare in Albemarle County, CMS reported about 474 emergency room visits and 136 inpatient hospital stays per 1,000 beneficiaries in 2023, which is lower than the state average for emergency visits (589) and lower than it for hospital stays (206). About 7% received home health care. These figures describe how people use care under Original Medicare; they do not show what any plan covers or costs.

Sources: CMS Medicare Geographic Variation, 2023; USDA Rural-Urban Continuum Codes, 2023.

Common questions

Albemarle County Medicare FAQ

Can I get help with Medicare in Albemarle County by phone?
Yes. Phone and video appointments are how we serve Albemarle County. We hold a Virginia license, compare plans from many carriers and walk you through them from home, with no pressure to enroll.
Will my Albemarle County doctors and hospitals be covered?
On a Medicare Advantage plan it depends on that plan’s network, so before you enroll we check whether your doctors and the hospitals you use, such as Sentara Martha Jefferson Hospital, are in network. With a Medicare Supplement (Medigap) plan you can see any doctor who accepts Medicare.
How many people in Albemarle County have Medicare Advantage?
CMS reported 5,913 people enrolled in a Medicare Advantage plan out of 26,372 people with Medicare in Albemarle County in September 2026, or 22.4%. The Virginia average was 39.6%.
Do you charge Albemarle County residents for help?
No, there is no fee. The insurance company pays us when you enroll, and your premium does not go up because you used an agent.
When can I enroll or change plans?
You can enroll during your Initial Enrollment Period around your 65th birthday, during the Annual Enrollment Period from October 15 to December 7, or during a Special Enrollment Period if you have a qualifying event such as moving into or within the county.
