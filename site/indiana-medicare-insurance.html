Insurance help for Indiana residents

If you live in Indiana and you're sorting through Medicare, health, or life insurance, it's easy to feel buried — mailers, TV ads, and call centers all pulling in different directions. Honorbrook exists to cut through that. We're an independent agency, which means we work for you, not for one insurance company, and we'll walk you through your real options in plain English.

We help Hoosiers who are turning 65, already on Medicare and unsure their plan still fits, retiring or losing employer coverage, shopping for coverage under 65, or simply wanting a second opinion from someone who isn't paid to push one product. And our help is always free — carriers pay us a commission when we place a policy, so you never pay a fee, and by law your premium is identical whether you use us or enroll on your own.

Common questions

Indiana Medicare & insurance FAQ

Do you charge Indiana residents for help?
No — our help is completely free. Carriers pay us a commission when we place a policy, and by law your premium is identical whether you use an agent or enroll on your own. You get our guidance at no added cost.
Which Indiana cities do you serve?
All of Indiana, handled by phone — including Indianapolis, Fort Wayne, Evansville, South Bend, Carmel, Fishers, and Bloomington, plus every community in between. No office visit needed.
What plans can I get in Indiana?
Availability varies by county. We compare the Medicare Advantage, Medigap, Part D, ACA, ICHRA, and life options actually offered at your exact address, so you only look at plans you can really enroll in.
Can you help if I'm new to Indiana or moving?
Yes. A move — including into or within Indiana — can open a Special Enrollment Period that lets you change or pick up coverage outside the usual windows. We'll check your timing and the plans available at your new home.
