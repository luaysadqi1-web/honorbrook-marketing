Atkinson County ranks 145th of 159 Georgia counties by number of residents age 65 and older, with about 1,217 people in that age group, or 14.7% of residents (statewide: 15.4%). The 481 residents now between 60 and 64 will become Medicare-eligible soon, and our turning 65 guide walks through it. Census 2023 estimates show 62% of the 65-plus population is under 75, 30% is 75 to 84 and 9% is 85 or older. CMS data show 71.5% of Atkinson County residents with Medicare chose Medicare Advantage, which is higher than the 56.2% average across Georgia. What most people do in a county is less important than what fits you. We weigh Medicare Advantage against Original Medicare with Medigap using your doctors, your drugs and your budget.

Who has Medicare in Atkinson County

Atkinson County is a rural county (USDA: urban population of fewer than 5,000, not adjacent to a metro area). Plan choices can be narrower than in a city, and we focus on networks that include the doctors and hospital you actually use.

CMS counts 1,488 Medicare beneficiaries in Atkinson County (2023). Their average age is 72, about the same as the Georgia average of 73. About 22% are “dual eligible,” meaning they have both Medicare and Medicaid, higher than the state figure of 12%. Where many people have both, special plans for dual-eligible members can matter; see our Medicaid and dual eligible guide, since availability varies by plan and area.

Among people in Original Medicare in Atkinson County, CMS reported about 672 emergency room visits and 285 inpatient hospital stays per 1,000 beneficiaries in 2023, which is higher than the state average for emergency visits (560) and higher than it for hospital stays (223). About 9% received home health care. These figures describe how people use care under Original Medicare; they do not show what any plan covers or costs.

Sources: CMS Medicare Geographic Variation, 2023; USDA Rural-Urban Continuum Codes, 2023.

Common questions

Atkinson County Medicare FAQ

Can I get help with Medicare in Atkinson County by phone?
Yes. You do not need to travel: we are licensed in Georgia and help Atkinson County residents by phone or video, comparing plans from many insurers at no cost to you.
Will my Atkinson County doctors and hospitals be covered?
On a Medicare Advantage plan it depends on that plan’s network, so before you enroll we check whether your doctors and the hospitals you use, such as Coffee Regional Medical Center, are in network. With a Medicare Supplement (Medigap) plan you can see any doctor who accepts Medicare.
How many people in Atkinson County have Medicare Advantage?
CMS reported 1,016 people enrolled in a Medicare Advantage plan out of 1,421 people with Medicare in Atkinson County in September 2026, or 71.5%. The Georgia average was 56.2%.
Do you charge Atkinson County residents for help?
No. You pay nothing to work with us. Carriers compensate us, and the price of your plan is the same as if you enrolled directly.
When can I enroll or change plans?
You can enroll during your Initial Enrollment Period around your 65th birthday, during the Annual Enrollment Period from October 15 to December 7, or during a Special Enrollment Period if you have a qualifying event such as moving into or within the county.
