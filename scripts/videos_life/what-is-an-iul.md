  [1] 0.2 s  What is indexed universal life? Here is the plain-English version.
  [2] 4.7 s  Indexed universal life, or IUL, is permanent life insurance. It has a death benefit and a cash value account.
  [3] 12.6 s  The cash value earns interest credits linked to an index. But your money is not invested in the market.
  [4] 19.2 s  A cap is the maximum credit in a period. A participation rate is your share of the index gain. A floor is the minimum credit, often 0 percent. Caps and participation rates can change, within policy limits.
  [5] 33.3 s  Policy charges reduce the cash value. The floor does not stop those charges. Surrender charges may apply.
  [6] 40.9 s  An IUL can lapse if it is underfunded. Loans and withdrawals reduce the death benefit, and they can cause a lapse.
  [7] 48.6 s  Guarantees depend on the insurer's claims-paying ability. Illustrations are projections, not promises. An IUL is not a substitute for a retirement plan. This is general education, not tax advice.

On-screen facts and sources:
  - IUL is permanent life insurance with a death benefit, paid while the policy is in force, and a cash value account.
    Source: https://honorbrook-insurance.com/blog/what-is-indexed-universal-life-iul-how-it-works-cost-risks  (page date: page dated Oct 12, 2026; read Nov 26, 2026)
  - The cash value earns interest credits linked to an index without being directly invested in it; you do not own the stocks and do not receive their dividends.
    Source: https://honorbrook-insurance.com/indexed-universal-life  (page date: page updated November 2026; read Nov 26, 2026)
  - Cap: the maximum interest credited in a period. Participation rate: the percentage of the index gain you receive. Floor: the minimum credited, often zero (commonly 0%). The floor protects against index losses, not policy fees.
    Source: https://honorbrook-insurance.com/indexed-universal-life  (page date: page updated November 2026; read Nov 26, 2026)
  - Caps and participation rates are not locked in; the insurer can change them within limits set in the policy.
    Source: https://honorbrook-insurance.com/indexed-universal-life  (page date: page updated November 2026; read Nov 26, 2026)
  - Policy charges come out of the cash value, and the cost of insurance rises with age. Surrender charges can apply if you cancel or take money out in the early years.
    Source: https://honorbrook-insurance.com/indexed-universal-life  (page date: page updated November 2026; read Nov 26, 2026)
  - If the cash value is not enough to cover the cost of insurance and fees, the policy can lapse. Loans and withdrawals reduce the death benefit and can cause a lapse.
    Source: https://honorbrook-insurance.com/indexed-universal-life  (page date: page updated November 2026; read Nov 26, 2026)
  - Every guarantee in the policy, including the floor, depends on the claims-paying ability of the insurance company. Illustrated values are projections, not promises.
    Source: https://honorbrook-insurance.com/indexed-universal-life  (page date: page updated November 2026; read Nov 26, 2026)
  - IUL is not a substitute for tax-advantaged retirement accounts for most people. Consult a tax professional.
