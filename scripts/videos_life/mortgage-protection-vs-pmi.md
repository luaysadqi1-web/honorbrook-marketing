On-screen facts and sources:
  - PMI protects the lender, not you, if you stop making payments on your loan.
    Source: https://www.consumerfinance.gov/ask-cfpb/what-is-private-mortgage-insurance-en-122/  (page date: reviewed Nov 9, 2026)
  - A lender may require PMI on a conventional loan when the down payment is less than 20 percent.
    Source: https://www.consumerfinance.gov/ask-cfpb/what-is-private-mortgage-insurance-en-122/  (page date: reviewed Nov 9, 2026)
  - FHA mortgage insurance is required on all FHA loans.
    Source: https://www.consumerfinance.gov/ask-cfpb/what-is-mortgage-insurance-and-how-does-it-work-en-1953/  (page date: reviewed Aug 28, 2026)
  - The VA guarantee takes the place of mortgage insurance and works in a similar way (protects the lender).
    Source: https://www.consumerfinance.gov/ask-cfpb/what-is-mortgage-insurance-and-how-does-it-work-en-1953/  (page date: reviewed Aug 28, 2026)
  - Credit life insurance proceeds are paid directly to the creditor (the lender).
    Source: https://content.naic.org/article/consumer-insight-credit-insurance  (page date: modified Jan 16, 2024)
  - Mortgage protection is a term life policy you own; it pays the beneficiary you name; it is optional. Compare what you owe, years left and coverage you already carry.
