On-screen facts and sources:
  - After the first policy anniversary, the policy owner may request updates on the policy, in the form of in-force illustrations.
    Source: https://content.naic.org/insurance-topics/life-insurance-illustrations  (page date: Last updated 1/8/2026)
  - Upon the request of the policy owner, the insurer furnishes an in-force illustration (an illustration of a policy in force one year or more).
    Source: https://content.naic.org/sites/default/files/model-law-582.pdf  (page date: NAIC Model 582, text dated April 2001 (model adopted 1995))
  - Guaranteed elements are shown before non-guaranteed elements; a zero is shown in the guaranteed column in a year with no guaranteed value.
    Source: https://content.naic.org/sites/default/files/model-law-582.pdf  (page date: NAIC Model 582, text dated April 2001 (model adopted 1995))
  - Non-guaranteed values assume current non-guaranteed elements continue unchanged; the insurer can change the assumptions; results could be higher or lower.
    Source: https://content.naic.org/sites/default/files/model-law-582.pdf  (page date: NAIC Model 582, text dated April 2001 (model adopted 1995))
  - Universal life lets you choose a flexible premium pattern as long as you pay enough to keep the policy in force.
    Source: https://content.naic.org/sites/default/files/publication-lig-lp-consumer-life.pdf  (page date: Copyright 2026 NAIC)
  - The universal life annual report shows any outstanding loan, and amounts debited to the policy value by type (policy charges).
    Source: https://content.naic.org/sites/default/files/model-law-582.pdf  (page date: NAIC Model 582, text dated April 2001 (model adopted 1995))
  - Surrender value is what is available after surrender charges, policy loans and loan interest.
    Source: https://content.naic.org/sites/default/files/model-law-582.pdf  (page date: NAIC Model 582, text dated April 2001 (model adopted 1995))
  - A current cap or participation rate the policy does not guarantee is a non-guaranteed element, subject to change (owner-required IUL caveat).
    Source: https://content.naic.org/sites/default/files/model-law-582.pdf  (page date: NAIC Model 582, text dated April 2001 (model adopted 1995))
  - Guarantees depend on the insurer (owner-required caveat; Form 30-A asks owners to compare the financial stability of the companies).
    Source: https://ris.dls.virginia.gov/uploads/14VAC5/forms/bd85f004265~1.pdf  (page date: Form 30-A (eff. 4/07))
  - A new policy has its own costs, and surrender charges may be deducted from the old one.
    Source: https://ris.dls.virginia.gov/uploads/14VAC5/forms/bd85f004265~1.pdf  (page date: Form 30-A (eff. 4/07))
  - A replacement may not be in your best interest, or it could be a good decision (suitable only some of the time).
    Source: https://ris.dls.virginia.gov/uploads/14VAC5/forms/bd85f004265~1.pdf  (page date: Form 30-A (eff. 4/07))
