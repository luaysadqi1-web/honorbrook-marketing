    Source: https://www.tn.gov/attorneygeneral/news/2022/6/29/pr22-22.html  (page date: June 29, 2022; checked Nov 26, 2026)

    Source: https://content.naic.org/article/consumer-insight-credit-insurance  (page date: Nov 1, 2008; checked Nov 26, 2026)
  - Mortgage insurance such as PMI protects the lender, not you, if you fall behind on your payments.

    Source: https://content.naic.org/article/consumer-insight-credit-insurance  (page date: Nov 1, 2008; checked Nov 26, 2026)
  - Level term: the death benefit and premium are fixed for the term. Decreasing term: the death benefit goes down over time.
