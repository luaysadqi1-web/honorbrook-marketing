# Targeting retirees who roll retirement money into annuities

Research brief, 2026-10-13. Status labels: **VERIFIED** = confirmed from a source found this session. **GENERAL** = standard rule from general knowledge, not confirmed here; check IRS Pub. 575/590-A/590-B or a tax professional before publishing. Nothing here has been published.

## 1. Bottom line
- The audience is not one group. It is about a dozen groups, each with a different account type, a different trigger and different traps. One "rollover to annuity" page cannot rank for all of them.
- Best approach: one pillar page plus one answer page per account type, each answering the real question for that account ("Can my 403(b) go into an annuity? What are the traps?"). The traps (penalty, withholding, two-year rule) are what people actually search for.
- We already have 12 related posts. The gaps are the account-specific ones (403(b), 457(b), SIMPLE/SEP, pension lump sum, inherited IRA, 1035 exchange, QLAC, in-service rollover, Roth).

## 3. Rules that apply across accounts (all VERIFIED unless marked)
- **Direct rollover vs 60-day:** an indirect rollover has a 60-day deadline; missed amounts are taxable.
- **20% withholding** on eligible plan distributions paid to you (401(k), 403(b), 457(b), TSP). IRAs are not subject to it.
- **RMDs** start at 73 for people born 1951 to 1959 and 75 for 1960 or later; an RMD cannot be rolled over.
- **QLAC:** premium excluded from RMD balance; payments must start by 85; limit reported as $210,000 for 2026 per secondary sources, **confirm on IRS before publishing.**
- **10% penalty:** the age-55 separation exception does not carry into an IRA.
- **Company stock (NUA):** rolling it into an IRA ends net unrealized appreciation treatment.

## 4. Compliance for annuity rollover content
- **DOL fiduciary rule:** the 2024 Retirement Security Rule was vacated on March 17, 2026, and PTE 2020-02 returned to its original 2020 text (best-interest standard, written disclosures, documented reasons for a rollover recommendation). Sources conflict on exact dates; check for appeals. **Whether and how this applies to Honorbrook's agents needs counsel or your carriers' compliance team.**
- **State best-interest rules (NAIC Model #275):** adoption counts conflict across sources (about 40 to 50 states). **Verify each of our 11 state insurance departments** before advertising by state.
- **Our standing rules:** no guaranteed returns, no "tax-free retirement", explain caps, surrender charges, fees and claims-paying ability, "not tax, legal or investment advice", no carrier-name claims, no invented statistics, no "free plan".
- **Not researched:** carrier approval of annuity advertising, state advertising filing rules, annuity training requirements, and paid-ad platform restrictions on annuity ads.
- **Open item from earlier:** which annuity types you are appointed to sell in which of the 11 states. State pages and ads depend on it.

## 5. Targeting by trigger (who searches, and why)

Inheritors. 10. People with an old annuity or cash-value life policy (1035). 11. Spouses, including after a death. 12. Laid-off workers rolling the last job's plan.
