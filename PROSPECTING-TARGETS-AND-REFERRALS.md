# Honorbrook — Full Target Universe & Referral Network Model
### Every target type, organized into a repeatable engine

This expands the prospecting bot with all the target types you listed. The big idea: **build a Referral Partner Network + a local Community Presence.** You can't cold-contact Medicare seniors — but partners and events send them to you, warm and compliant. This is the durable lead machine.

---

## ⚖️ Compliance note for senior/Medicare venues (important)
Events at senior centers, churches, lodges, fairs, and community centers fall under **CMS "marketing/sales event" rules** for Medicare:
- **Educational events** (pure info, no plan-specific selling, no sign-ups) have the fewest restrictions — great for building trust and collecting *opt-in* contacts.
- **Sales/marketing events** must be advertised, and you **may not** cold-call attendees afterward or collect info without permission.
- You **cannot** door-knock or cold-call seniors, or collect Scope-of-Appointment under pressure.
- Partner referrals and inbound (someone gives you their number and asks for help) are always clean.
Keep events **educational + opt-in**; let partners and the website drive the direct outreach.

## 🩺 Tier 1 — Healthcare Referral Partners *(highest value — they see seniors daily)*
They refer patients who need Medicare/insurance help; in return you keep them in-network and take work off their plate. Also potential ICHRA clients (their own staff).
- Independent pharmacies **+ retail pharmacy counters** (CVS, Walgreens — build relationships with local pharmacists/managers)
- Primary care / doctor's offices, **dental offices**, **chiropractic** offices
- **Surgical centers**, outpatient / ambulatory facilities
- **Independent lab** facilities
- **Hearing & vision** facilities (audiologists, optometrists)
- Home health / senior-care providers, physical therapy
**Approach:** "I keep your patients in-network and handle their plan questions, free — here are cards/flyers for your front desk." Build a **two-way** referral (you send them patients too).
**Find:** Google Maps by facility type + city, NPI registry, local independent-provider associations.

## 💼 Tier 2 — Financial & Professional Referral Partners *(shared clients)*
They have clients who need Medicare, life, annuities, or ICHRA; you have clients who need them. Cross-refer.
- **Financial planners / advisors**, wealth managers
- **CPAs / accountants**, estate-planning attorneys, elder-law attorneys
- **HR consultants / group benefit brokers** ("HR agents who handle the group")
- **Life & group insurance producers and agencies** (cross-refer the products you each don't write — or recruit them)
- Realtors who work with downsizing seniors, mortgage brokers
**Approach:** peer-to-peer. "You handle X, I handle Medicare/senior — let's send each other the business we don't write." Formalize with a simple referral agreement.

## 🏛️ Tier 3 — Community & Senior Venues *(presence + educational events, opt-in)*
Be the trusted local face. Educational "Medicare 101 / Turning 65" workshops, sponsor a coffee, staff a table.
- **Senior centers**, community centers, 55+ / active-adult communities, libraries
- **Churches** & faith organizations (many run senior ministries)
- **VFW, American Legion, Moose Lodge, Elks Lodge** and other fraternal/veteran posts
- **Fairs, festivals**, farmers markets, local expos, health fairs
- **Fitness facilities** (gyms with SilverSneakers/Renew Active skew senior)
**Approach:** offer a **free educational Medicare workshop** or sponsor an event; collect only **opt-in** contacts (a sign-up for a free plan review). Leave flyers + your review-page QR code.

## 🏢 Tier 4 — Employers & HR (ICHRA / Group)
- **Small-to-midsize employers** (2–100 employees) across all verticals
- **HR decision-makers / HR managers / office managers** who own benefits
- Retail facilities & franchises with W-2 staff
**Approach:** the ICHRA/group scripts in `PROSPECTING-OUTREACH-SCRIPTS.md`.

## 🧑‍🏫 Tier 5 — Recruiting (grow the agency)
- New / aspiring agents
- Captive agents → go independent
- **Life & group producers / agencies** → cross-sell partnership or recruit under Honorbrook
**Approach:** the recruiting scripts in the scripts file.

## 🗂️ Build the Referral Database (the real asset)
Track every partner in one place (a sheet or Integrity Connect) so this compounds:
`partner_name, type (Tier 1–5), contact, org, city, status (prospect→met→active→dormant), last_touch, referrals_sent, referrals_received, notes`
- **Nurture quarterly:** thank partners, share results, drop off fresh materials.
- **Reciprocate:** send them business too — that's what makes referral partners loyal.
- Goal: **20–30 active referral partners** = a self-sustaining warm-lead pipeline that never violates a single Medicare cold-contact rule.
