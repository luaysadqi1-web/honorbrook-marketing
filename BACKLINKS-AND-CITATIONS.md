**Do these first.** They are free, high-authority, and they generate actual leads, not
just link equity. #3 and #4 share one registration.

## Tier 3 — Insurance & Medicare directories (topical relevance)

These carry topical relevance signals that generic directories do not — a link from a
Medicare directory tells Google what you *are*, not just where you are.

| Listing | Cost | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| **MedicareAgents.com** | Free tier | Free forever, no card. Applications are manually reviewed for licensure and carrier appointments; approval typically 1-2 business days. |
| **Medicare Agents Hub** | Free | Bills itself as the largest Medicare agent directory, all 50 states. |
| **Certified Medicare Agents (CMA)** | Free to list | Consumer-facing directory of agents and brokers. |
| **Medicare Broker Directory** | Free | Local Medicare broker directory. |
| **MedicareSupp.org agent directory** | Free | Medigap/MA focused. |
| **NABIP** (National Association of Benefits and Insurance Professionals) | Paid membership | Membership gets you into their consumer "Find an Agent" tool. Also a genuine credibility marker for the ICHRA/group side of your book, which is your least-competitive niche. |

When you fill these in, use the **same NAP block** above, and where a bio field exists,
mention the specific products (ICHRA, SHOP, Medigap, final expense) — those directories
often rank for long-tail queries themselves and pass relevance on the terms you use.

## Compliance notes

- Medicare marketing rules apply to directory profiles too: do not state or imply you
  offer every plan in an area, and keep the CMS disclaimer language consistent with
  the site.
- Do not incentivize reviews in any form. The review funnel at `/reviews` is already
  built correctly — it asks everyone, with no gating, per Google and FTC rules.
- Never buy links or use a bulk "submit to 500 directories" service. For a regulated
  vertical that is a real risk, and the citations that matter are the ones above.

## Tracking

Keep a simple sheet: Listing | URL | Date submitted | Status | Live URL | NAP verified.

to Tysons move is exactly the kind of change that leaves stale duplicates behind.

Also worth doing once: search `"Honorbrook Insurance"` quarterly and check whether any
old *Vienna* listings exist that you do not control. If they do, claim and correct them
rather than leaving a conflicting address in the wild.
