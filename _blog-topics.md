# Blog Topic Backlog (SEO content engine)

The scheduled routine picks the FIRST unchecked `- [ ]` topic each run, writes the post, then checks it off `- [x]`. **Order = priority. Do not reorder by habit; reorder by season.**

## Editorial rules (set by Luay, Oct 2026)
- **Write the question a real person types or asks an agent** ("Can I switch Medicare plans after Dec 7?"), not an insurance-industry headline. Title = the question or a close variant; answer it in the first 2 sentences.
- **Audience, in order:** (1) individuals, families and small businesses under 65 (ACA, ICHRA, SHOP, group); (2) Medicare and every part of it; (3) high-income individuals (life insurance, IUL, FIAs); (4) seniors (final expense); (5) parents (kids' life/savings policies, Gerber-type juvenile plans, college funding).
- **Multi-state:** we serve 11 states (VA, MD, NC, SC, GA, AL, LA, TX, MI, IN, WV). Keep posts national in scope; do NOT write Virginia-only or city-only posts unless the topic is explicitly multi-state. Mention that rules/availability vary by state.
- **Season gate:** Oct 1 - Dec 7 = Medicare AEP topics first. Nov 1 - Jan 15 = ACA Open Enrollment topics are also priority. Jan 1 - Mar 31 = Medicare Advantage Open Enrollment topics. Do not publish evergreen filler in these windows.
- **Compliance for the new product lines:** never quote or imply specific returns, rates, caps, or "tax-free retirement" outcomes; always explain caps, participation rates, surrender charges, fees, and that guarantees depend on the issuing carrier's claims-paying ability; say these are not investments and not tax/legal advice; suitability depends on the individual. Annuities/IUL/FIA posts use the independent-agency disclaimer (from ichra.html) and must not use carrier names as claims. Juvenile/college posts: do not compare to a 529 as "better" - explain tradeoffs only. No invented statistics.
