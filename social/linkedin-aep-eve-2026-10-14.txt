Medicare's annual enrollment period opens tomorrow, October 15, and runs through December 7. Whatever you choose takes effect on January 1.

Most people do not need to change anything. Everyone should look, because plans can change their drug list, pharmacy network, costs and provider network from one year to the next, and the notice that explains those changes is easy to set aside.

A simple review takes four steps:

1. Read the Annual Notice of Change your plan mailed.
2. Check that every prescription you take is still on the drug list, and at what tier.
3. Confirm your doctors and your hospital are still in network.
4. Confirm your pharmacy is still a good fit.

If all four hold, keeping your plan is a perfectly good outcome. If one does not, you have until December 7 to act.
