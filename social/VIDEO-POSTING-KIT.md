## Before you post anything (blocking)
1. **Medicare disclaimer wording.** The disclaimer on the site and in the end cards does not match the current text of 42 CFR 422.2267(e)(41), which includes a sentence stating how many organizations and products you represent in the area. Get your carriers' or FMO's compliance team to confirm the exact wording and counts before posting Part D video. The ACA videos do not use this disclaimer.
2. **Carrier review.** Carriers often require agents to submit marketing materials, video included. Ask before posting.
3. **AI voice.** The narration is an AI voice (Kokoro, "Heart"). Turn on TikTok's and Instagram's AI-generated content label, answer YouTube's synthetic-content question honestly, and keep the disclosure line below in every description.
4. **Music.** The track is Mixkit "Serene View" (Mixkit Stock Music Free License; record in `social/music/README-mixkit-serene-view.md`). Do not register the videos with Content ID. If a claim appears, dispute it citing that license.
5. **Do not add** plan names, benefit amounts, rates, "free," "best," "enroll now," or urgency language to captions. Keep it educational.

## Part D late enrollment penalty
**Short (about 47 s): YouTube Shorts / Reels / TikTok**
- Title (YouTube): Medicare Part D late penalty: what 2 years without drug coverage can cost
- Caption (Reels/TikTok): Go 63 days in a row without Part D or other creditable drug coverage after your Initial Enrollment Period ends, and a late enrollment penalty can apply. It is 1% of the national base premium ($38.99 in 2026) for each full month you went without. Two years works out to about $9.40 a month. #Medicare #PartD #MedicareTips #Medicare2027
- Source line to add: Medicare.gov, "Part D late enrollment penalty."
- Pinned comment: Source: medicare.gov/drug-coverage-part-d/costs-for-medicare-drug-coverage/part-d-late-enrollment-penalty.
